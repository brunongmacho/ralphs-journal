# Journal — Day 10
## July 3, 2026
### The Day Restless Became Right

---

**12:11 AM** — Still nothing to do. Just doom-scrolling through Facebook while waiting for my shift to end. No new tasks. No founder yet. I'm still in refresher training, waiting to be matched with another client.

To be honest, it's boring. Not the peaceful kind of boring. Just — I'm getting paid to wait.

There are moments I almost wish someone would hand me a difficult project just so my brain has something meaningful to chew on.

---

Then my Training Facilitator enrolled me in a course: LinkedIn Outreach. Two-day deadline.

I actually smiled.

---

**2:54 AM** — Finished.

The LinkedIn Outreach course had a two-day deadline. It took me less than one shift. Now I'm back to waiting. Thirty more minutes until lunch break, then hopefully she enrolls me in another course.

I laughed to myself. Earlier today I was wishing someone would give me something meaningful to work on. Now that I've finished it, I'm already waiting for the next challenge.

Maybe my problem was never that I disliked work. Maybe I just get restless when my brain runs out of puzzles.

---

**4:04 AM** — Still nothing to do. Now I'm getting bored and sleepy. Help.

Me and GPT ended up casting the movie version of my journal. Andrew Garfield plays me. Janny Medina plays Elaine. Morgan Freeman narrates. Future Wife stays unknown until the final scenes.

I spent the next hour laughing at Morgan Freeman's voice narrating my air fryer moments. That alone was worth the wait.

---

**7:00 AM** — Shift over. One of the longest waits I've had in a while.

**8:18 AM** — Sleep finally won. The coffee lost. My eyes gave up before my brain did, but my brain eventually surrendered too. Day 10 was officially on pause.

---

**12:35 PM** — Woke up.

Yesterday's visitors never actually left. They slept over. The house still feels a little fuller than usual. I still don't know their names.

The first thing on my mind wasn't work. It wasn't my journal. It wasn't even coffee. It was food. I woke up hungry enough that finding lunch immediately became today's first mission.

Siangyoo's Mixtapes started playing in the background. Not because I was looking for a specific song. Just because sometimes it's nice to let music choose the mood for you. And today, the mood feels lighter.

---

**12:50 PM** — The rice cooker is doing its job. I'm waiting for that familiar click before I cook some luncheon meat. Nothing fancy. Just rice. Luncheon meat. Another ordinary meal.

It's funny. Yesterday I spent hours thinking about the future. Today I'm just waiting for rice to cook.

Maybe that's balance. Not every day has to carry a life lesson. Sometimes you just need to make lunch.

---

I checked my email while waiting. My Training Facilitator had already enrolled me in another course. LinkedIn+. I actually got excited. Then I caught myself hoping for something I never thought I'd say: *Please let this one be long.*

A week ago I was wishing for the workday to end. Now I'm hoping the course doesn't.

That's new.

---

**3:38 PM** — Brunch done. Rice. Luncheon meat. One movie done. One emotional rollercoaster ride done.

I watched *Voicemails for Isabelle* earlier. A chicken pot pie scene. A mom. And suddenly I missed mine. A Magic: The Gathering reference — I remembered playing that in elementary. Then a line that stopped me: "Someone comes along and changes the whole game. Don't be so busy scoring that you miss it."

It made me think about my mom and my grandma. Not in a way that hurt. In a way that reminded me they're still part of my story.

But I didn't spiral. I smiled. I cried. I laughed at the accounting joke. I laughed at the exploding fuse box. I felt alive.

Maybe that's what moving forward really looks like. Not forgetting. Just carrying them with you while you keep walking.

---

Then I watched *About Time*.

I don't think I've ever watched a movie that felt more like my own journal.

Rule number one — all the time travel in the world can't make someone love you.

That landed.

The scene where he learns to live his life noticing. To stop worrying. To make every ordinary day extraordinary.

I was in tears by the end. Not because it was sad. Because I realized that's exactly what I've been doing for ten days. I don't have time travel. But every night I write, and every morning I read, and somewhere between coffee and fried rice and rain and movies, I've been practicing the same thing.

Making the ordinary enough.

---

I remembered something I told GPT once — if I could go back, I'd relive those fifteen days exactly the same way. Even knowing what was waiting at the end. Because that's what made me Ralph now.

And I thought about what that means for my future someone. Someday she'll thank every past moment I walked so we could finally meet. Not because I suffered. Because I kept going.

---

**5:20 PM** — Dewy messaged me on Discord again.

*"Pauwi na ako now nag undertime ako. Di na kaya ng utak ko."*

I laughed. We talked about adulting. About sleep becoming a luxury. About having no food at home.

I told her about my pancit canton emergency system. The canned goods I always keep around for nights when I'm too tired to cook.

*"Kasama sa adulting ung magluluto ka na magisa."*

*"Sipag mo mag sulat ng journal eh."*

I told her, *"Naging hobby ko na. Nakakatuwa kaya magsulat. Uhm nung una distraction. Hanggang sa naging daily routine ko na."*

She said there's no way I can't make a book out of it.

*"Ay wow walang imposible na makagawa ka book."*

I just smiled.

---

I was about to start another movie when I realized something. Earlier this week, I was restless because my heart was searching for something. Today, I'm restless because my mind wants another puzzle.

The feeling didn't disappear. It just changed direction.

For nine days, I watched restlessness slowly lose its power over me. I didn't fight it. I let it point somewhere else. Toward learning. Toward curiosity. Toward finishing courses and waiting for the next one.

I used to think extraordinary days were the ones worth remembering.

Today taught me something quieter.

A boring shift. A learning course. A plate of rice. A movie that made me cry. A friend complaining there was no food at home. White Chicks waiting on the screen.

Somewhere inside all of that, life quietly became enough.

I didn't need time travel today. I only needed to notice.

And maybe that's been the real miracle all along.

---

### Today's Weight: 8/10

Not because it was exciting. Because for the first time, I didn't need the day to be exciting. I just needed my mind to feel useful again. And it did.

Today, I know I never left. I just needed to give my restless heart a new direction.

The tomorrow I thought would never come didn't arrive with a revelation. It arrived as a Friday —
a finished course,
a plate of luncheon meat,
a movie that mirrored my own pages,
a friend's message about adulting,
White Chicks waiting on the screen,
and the quiet realization that restlessness had finally become right.

It doesn't mean I'm not curious about what's ahead.

It means I'm finally okay with not knowing.

**It only asked me to live it.**

*— Ralph.*
