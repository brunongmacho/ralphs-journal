The Day I Wasn't Chasing Anything

✦

At 1:37 in the morning, I learned that my founder backed out.

A few months ago, I probably would've spent the rest of the night wondering why.

Tonight, I just shrugged and went back to training.

Another founder will come.

Another conversation will happen.

Life has been teaching me that not everything needs an immediate replacement.

Sometimes things simply change direction.

✦

The training continued anyway.

Mock meetings.

Pitch practice.

I wrote and rewrote my introduction until it stopped sounding like a script and started sounding like me.

Hi, my name is Ralph Bernard Serrano. I work as a Digital Marketing Virtual Assistant.

I help businesses build systems that actually make sense.

That part felt good.

Not because I had a client yet.

Because I was preparing for something instead of waiting for it.

There's a difference.

✦

By 2 PM, I was on my way to see the apartment.

For the past few days, it had only existed as photos, messages, and a Facebook listing.

Today, it became real.

I walked through the room.

Looked around.

Checked the space.

Imagined where my desk would go.

Where the PC would sit.

Where I'd probably leave my guitar.

It wasn't luxurious.

It wasn't huge.

But it felt quiet.

And somehow, that was exactly what I was looking for.

I liked it.

So I paid the reservation fee.

There wasn't a dramatic moment where I suddenly knew.

It simply felt right.

No long debate.

No endless second-guessing.

Just one quiet decision.

If everything goes according to plan, I'll move in on the 25th.

It's strange how one small payment can suddenly make the future feel tangible.

Yesterday it was just an idea.

Today, it has a move-in date.

Some futures don't arrive all at once. They begin with a reservation fee.

✦

On the way home, I realized something.

This will be the first place that's truly mine.

Not my parents'.

Not my grandmother's.

Not my cousin's.

Mine.

A room where the routines will belong to me.

The silence will belong to me.

The late-night work shifts.

The guitar.

The journal.

The ordinary days.

For the first time in my life...

I won't just be living somewhere.

I'll be building a home instead of borrowing one.

✦

The rest of the afternoon stayed wonderfully uneventful.

I ate fried daing na bangus.

Simple.

Comforting.

Exactly the kind of meal that reminds you home doesn't have to be expensive to feel complete.

Then I spent hours watching How I Met Your Mother.

Episode after episode.

No rush.

No guilt.

No feeling that I should be somewhere else.

Just a Saturday unfolding exactly as it wanted to.

✦

I wasn't chasing anything today.

Not a founder.

Not an answer.

Not a relationship.

Not a solution.

I just existed through the day.

And somehow, that's when the best things happened.

The founder backed out — and I was fine.

The apartment appeared.

I didn't convince myself.

I didn't negotiate with my instincts.

I just said yes.

Not because I was desperate.

Because it felt right.

Maybe that's what happens when you stop chasing.

You don't stop moving forward.

You just stop forcing it.

✦

Today's Weight: 8.5/10

Not because the apartment was perfect.

Because I stopped waiting for my future to happen on its own.

I walked into it.

Signed my name.

Chose it.

✦

A few months ago, I was trying to rebuild myself.

Today, I started choosing where that rebuilt version of me will live.

Maybe healing isn't only about learning how to let go.

Maybe it's also about opening the door to a place you've never lived before...

setting your bag down...

looking around the room...

and realizing,

"I think I can build a life here."

— Ralph.
