Ralph's Journal — Day 5.5

Sunday, June 28, 2026

8 min read

The Day I Opened the Archive

✦

Day 5.0 ended at 7/10. I thought that was it. It wasn't.

✦

The World Around Me Changed

It started with a notification I was expecting. Elaine left the Discord server.

My heart sank for a sec. I wasn't lying when I said I was ready for it, but you can't be ready for this kind of scenario. The sinking feeling didn't take over. It passed. And in its place came something I didn't expect. A quiet realization. One ended. While others return.

Irish came back. Elle has been checking in. Goblok invited me out. I'm talking to my MMO friends about Aion 2. I slept nine hours. I finished Python Day 4. I wrote another journal.

One chapter quietly closed a little more.

✦

I talked to Goblok. I told him she left the server. "Ayos din yon para di mo din chinecheck." No lecture. Just a friend. I told him I was giving her an entry in my journal. "Kailangan ko lang talaga ilabas lahat. Mahirap yung parating binibitbit eh."

He said, "Masasanay ka din ulit."

I said maybe acceptance is what I need to work on. The past can't be changed. So the only thing left is to carry it differently.

✦

Then something happened I didn't expect today. I joined Discord voice.

A few hours earlier my chest tightened just thinking about it. But after she left the server, there was one less thing pulling at me. So I joined. No relationship talks. Just random topics. Someone playing Dota 2 while I watched.

I fell asleep watching that stream. Joji playing in the background. Not a deep sleep. But it was good.

When I woke up, I checked in with myself.

I'm good.

Not I'm trying. Not I'm surviving.

Just... I'm good.

That was the first time I'd said it all week. I didn't even notice until the words came out.

My body is too relaxed. Also my brain.

For the first time since this started, my nervous system believed it was safe to rest.

✦

I noticed something about my music today.

Morning was RADWIMPS, Sparkle on repeat. Then Disturbed's The Sound of Silence. Then The Corrs. By afternoon it was Joji.

Without realizing it, my music started changing with my mood instead of trying to change it. That's something emotionally healthy people do. I just let it happen.

✦

Looking Back Without Going Back

While I was eating, a thought I didn't see coming arrived.

What if I didn't just move forward? What if I looked back, not to return, but to remember?

I told myself: I want to open the messages of Elaine. Not to message her. Not to share the journal. I want her to have a chapter in my journal. If someday someone will read this, or even if it's only future Ralph, they'll understand. Ralph will remember.

I paused. Then I compared yesterday to today. Yesterday I wanted to send her the journal hoping she might change her mind. Today the center was my story. I wrote one sentence before I started: I am opening these messages to remember, not to return.

I opened the Discord archive. I read everything.

My first words to myself: I understand now why this relationship hit me so deeply. Not because it lasted a long time. Because it became emotionally deep very quickly.

I noticed the comfort. How within days we were already teasing naturally, sharing pictures, making coffee over chat, calling each other hon. I saw her too: "Feel ko din naman," "Same tayo ng fear." She was letting herself trust.

Then I saw the painful shift. My messages became "Hon?" and "Hon pls let's talk." The silence was hard to read. One person reaching. Waiting.

But something surfaced that I needed to hear: The ending does not erase the beginning. There was something real here. Something that unfortunately didn't survive.

✦

I decided to write something. Not a report. A piece from someone who just spent an afternoon inside those old conversations.

I called it The Girl Who Made Home Feel Closer.

I read it and got teary-eyed. Not because it was sad. Because it was light. Calm. I felt the love. I didn't feel any anger or regrets. The ending didn't belong there, so I left it out. A photo of a smile doesn't need a caption about what came after.

✦

The Facebook messages finally arrived at 10 PM. I converted them and sent everything, Discord and Facebook together.

I wanted them in one chapter. Sometimes we talked on Discord, sometimes on FB, sometimes in person, sometimes over the phone. It was all the same love story. Splitting it by platform would tell the wrong story.

I decided to organize the chapter by moments, not by app.

✦

I read every single line from both archives. Thousands of messages.

I changed the chapter title to Fifteen Days That Were Never Small.

I sat there for a while after reading it. Some relationships last years and never feel that alive. It was real, and that's why it deserves to be remembered.

✦

Some people measure love in years. We measured ours in ordinary days. I'll remember that for fifteen days, I loved completely. That's something worth carrying for the rest of my life.

✦

What I Learned About Myself

Somewhere in the middle of all this, I asked myself something that made me stop.

When do you feel the most like yourself?

Not the happiest. Not the most productive. Just... That's Ralph.

I thought about it. My answer surprised me.

The constant planning. The man who becomes restless when he isn't thinking. If I'm awake, I want to do something, I want to think about something. Or I'll sleep to rest my brain. Not just in a relationship. In life.

I sat with that. Then I realized something I didn't expect.

That's your biggest strength. You don't stay still for long. Even when you're devastated, you start building something.

This week alone I built a healthier routine, five journals, a Python habit, deeper friendships, a better understanding of myself.

But I also spotted the shadow. My brain almost never gets permission to simply exist. There's always another plan, another project, another version of Ralph to become.

When was the last time you felt completely content doing absolutely nothing?

I couldn't answer.

Then I thought: maybe the 9-hour sleep this morning was exactly that. For nine hours, the planner took the day off.

✦

I asked myself a random question. If I were the editor, what title would I give my book?

The Long Way Home.

Not a love story. Not a breakup story. A man finding his way back to himself. I wrote an introduction too. "This book began on a week when sleep became a stranger, silence became loud, and every notification felt heavier than it should."

It was strange reading it. Like seeing my life through someone else's eyes. Like realizing I've been writing a book without knowing it.

✦

Yosh messaged. She talked about her boyfriend, how he waits for her, stocks the fridge with her favorite food, buys her a comb just because she casually said she liked it. I was genuinely happy for her.

Simple lang naman kasi mga lalake magmahal mars.

Underneath her stories I recognized my own patterns. I loved Elaine the same way. The planning. The attention. The details. The future.

I noticed it too. Whoever ends up with Ralph one day is going to be deeply loved.

✦

I kept thinking about something I called my anchor. I decided I wouldn't let her become the anchor. I'd let my response become the anchor. Every time I respond with peace instead of panic, I'm becoming someone I like more.

✦

After reading everything, I told myself something that had been forming in my head all day.

I think opening the Discord messages helped me a little. Bit by bit. Step by step. I'm learning to walk again. I'm accepting facts. Answering questions that are left unanswered — maybe my version of answer. But as of now, I'll take it. Not because Elaine isn't important. It's just that I'm moving forward. I'll still look back. Everything is important to me. But I won't move backwards.

That line deserves to live in the journal exactly as it is. Then I added: memories are places to visit, not places to live. I walked through those conversations, appreciated them, then came back to Joji, to Discord voice, to Python, to today.

✦

I knew I wanted to write Day 5.5 first, then Elaine's chapter.

Day 5.5 is still alive. Elaine's chapter is archival work. Day 5.5 is today's heartbeat.

So here I am. Writing today's heartbeat.

✦

A week ago, I thought healing meant waking up one morning and not thinking about her anymore.

Today I know it means something quieter.

Thinking about her...

and still choosing myself.

✦

Today's Weight: 7/10

Same as this morning. Not because nothing happened, but because everything happened and I stayed upright. I read our history. I joined voice. I napped watching Dota. I talked to friends. I waited for Facebook. I didn't break. I didn't message her. I just kept moving.

✦

One chapter ended. The story didn't.

Today, peace wasn't something I searched for. It quietly found me while I was watching someone else play Dota.

— Ralph.
