The Day Nothing Happened

✦

I woke up at 8 AM in a bed that doesn't know me yet.

My body aches.

Not from anything dramatic.

Just from sleeping somewhere unfamiliar.

It'll pass.

✦

I went to the grocery.

Bought breakfast.

Ate alone at my own table.

Then I watched a series.

Played Ragnarok.

Nothing surprising.

Nothing that needs to be remembered.

✦

At 3 PM I walked to the laundromat.

Stopped by the bakery.

Bought bread.

Came home.

I looked around. Every object in this room is here because I chose it.

That feeling doesn't get old.

That was the most exciting part of my afternoon.

And I was okay with that.

✦

This is my first official day being solo.

It feels strange.

Not bad.

Just... quiet.

The kind of quiet that takes getting used to.

✦

I am still talking to Yui.

We've been talking every day.

I mentioned it like it was nothing.

Because it is.

No questions about whether I'm ready.

No guilt.

Just conversations.

That's new too.

✦

At 10:50 PM we watched Disclosure on Discord.

It had an open ending.

I sat there after the credits wondering what happens next.

Then I realized I didn't mind not knowing.

Some stories don't need to be resolved tonight.

✦

Nothing remarkable happened today.

No breakthroughs.

No breakdowns.

No visits from the past.

Just laundry, bread, Ragnarok, and a movie with someone I like.

A month ago, a Sunday like this would've felt empty.

Today it felt like enough.

✦

— Ralph.
