The Day She Swallowed the Sea

✦

I woke up at eleven.

RO dailies.

Bought food.

Picked up my laundry.

A perfectly ordinary morning.

✦

And then I checked on her.

It was her practice day.

Freediving.

"Good morning andito na pala me sa diving class," she said.

The sea had already happened to her by then.

✦

Water got into her snorkel on the way to the practice point.

"A little drowning moment hahahha."

Then line training.

Hyperventilated from the waves — "nanikip dibdib ko."

Dizzy after.

Nauseous after.

Sardines before training, she said, like that helped.

Even the shower at the place broke.

Her worst practice, told entirely in jokes.

"Alam mo first time ko makaexp ng ganun," she said.

She'd even had a feeling the night before — "parang tinatamad ako dumalo today."

Foreboding.

✦

"Need mo mag blow," I said.

"Bawi next practice."

I told her about bonamine.

And about my own training during habagat season.

The elevator feeling.

Waves you can't argue with.

"Masasanay ka nalang tlga," I told her. "Wala naman technique dyan."

It's strange how easy the advice is when you've already been through the same water.

✦

Then she sent me her first line practice.

"Grabe ambilis mo mag descend. Galing!"

My first time, I didn't even reach halfway.

"Malakas lang hatak ko sa rope hahahhaa," she said.

Her ears hurt. Mine did too, back then.

I sent her a photo of my first mask.

"Same ata tayo first mask."

Same fins. Same black.

"As in?!" she said.

✦

October came up again.

"Mgddive ka ba sa oct pag punta mo dito?"

No dive spots near Manila.

Batangas then.

Four hours away.

Carpool at four in the morning.

Overnight, then back by noon.

Her whole visit is only three days.

She still asked about the drive.

"Lets just enjoy meeting muna," I said.

"Sige2 but if may chance na maextend pa naman ako edi goooo," she said.

The plan isn't a plan yet.

It's a maybe with a beach.

✦

She came home with sea legs.

"Ang alat pa ng lalamunan ko."

"Bat mo naman kasi sinubukan ubusin ung dagat," I said.

She laughed.

That's the thing about her bad days.

She laughs through them.

And I get to be the one who jokes back.

✦

Later, dinner.

"Jwu!!!! Musta naman jan?!" she said.

She checked on me this time.

The pattern, in reverse.

"D dumating ung shopee ko. 🥹🥲"

She sent clips. I sent clips.

"Miss mo na mag videoke no?"

"Mejooooo."

She asked if I'd seen Remember Me.

"D ko pa napapanuod yan. 😅"

Yet.

✦

Nothing dramatic happened.

That was the whole day.

I woke up, handled my life, and spent the rest of it talking to someone about a sea I used to know.

She didn't swallow the sea.

She just came home tasting it.

And I got to hear all of it.

The confession is behind us now.

And the days are still ordinary.

Which is the whole point.

— Ralph.
