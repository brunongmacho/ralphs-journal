The Day I Kept Working

✦

I slept after my shift, around 7 AM.

When I woke up...

the email was already there.

Ami.

"I'm pausing all work for now while I sort out some serious issues I'm having with Doneverse directly. This isn't about you. You've been kind and put in effort, including staying late to get me the reel. I appreciate that."

She said it plainly.

This isn't about you.

She didn't have to say that part.

But she did.

✦

It's disappointing.

The reels were finally starting to feel right.

I had just begun understanding her voice.

And now everything is on pause.

I had a founder who was trusting me with her brand.

Not because of my work.

Because of something on her side of things.

That's the realization that held the day together:

I can't control it.

✦

Pretty sad.

I won't pretend it isn't.

A month ago, an email like this would've started the spiral.

Did I do something wrong?

Was I not good enough?

Tonight, my first thought was just...

I can't control that.

✦

So I sent a screenshot to my DSL.

Kept the people who assigned me in the loop.

No drama.

Just facts.

Then the day continued.

Ragnarok.

Dailies.

Bopis with rice at 3 in the afternoon.

Supernatural until night.

None of it was avoidance.

I just understood there was nothing left to fix today.

✦

It's 11 PM.

I'll still show up for my shift.

That sentence feels small.

But it used to be the hardest one I could say.

Some versions of me would've spent the night replaying the email.

This one drafted a content calendar instead.

Because moving forward is easier than standing still.

✦

Not because I'm waiting by the door.

Because if Ami ever comes back...

I'll have something ready to show her.

And if she doesn't...

it's still not wasted.

It's practice.

Portfolio material.

Proof that I can think ahead.

A client can pause.

The skill stays with me.

✦

Forty-three days ago, my emotional weather depended on one person.

Tonight, one email changed my day.

But it didn't take away my direction.

I'm not chasing.

I'm preparing.

That's the difference.

Clients come.

Clients go.

But the person I'm becoming...

still clocks in tomorrow.

✦

— Ralph.
