The Day There Was No Hidden Meaning

✦

I slept at 7 AM.

Woke up around noon.

Opened the PC.

The whole thing was corrupted.

✦

There was a moment, in the first minutes, where my brain tried to find the emotional version of this.

Heartbreak? No.

Uncertainty? No.

Windows.

Just Windows.

✦

So I did the only thing that made sense:

Backup everything that could be saved.

Reformat.

Rebuild.

There was nothing to decode. The steps were obvious, one right after the other. The problem didn't mean something. It was just broken.

That might be why I didn't panic.

After the past few months, this felt almost relaxing.

Concrete things fail and you fix them. No hidden weight, no guessing what it meant.

✦

In between, I ate, and I kept Supernatural running while the backups crawled along.

The operating system was dying, and Dean was on.

Honestly, that summed the day up pretty well.

✦

It's almost midnight, and I'm still at it.

Not because something else went wrong.

Rebuilding just takes hours.

Transfer what can be kept. Reinstall what was there before. Put the pieces back in the right order.

There's a rhythm to it.

And somewhere in the middle of it, I caught myself thinking:

This is the kind of day I used to treat like a threat.

Now it's just a list.

Save.

Reformat.

Fix.

Done beats pretty, every time.

✦

Tonight's day didn't need to be a lesson.

It was a machine, a meal, an episode, and a hundred small repairs.

That's it.

That's life, sometimes.

— Ralph.
