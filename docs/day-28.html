The Day the Past Stayed Quiet

✦

Today officially reset me back to Day 1.

My founder unmatched.

Back to Doneverse University.

A few weeks ago, that would've felt like failure.

Today...

it just felt like part of the process.

✦

Shift ended at 7 AM. I did my Ragnarok dailies while drinking Energen.

Nothing special. Just a morning that felt like any other morning.

✦

The rest of the day was quiet.

No urgent messages. No emotional storms. Just waiting for the evening.

Then, around 9:24 PM, I started talking to someone new.

Her name is Yui.

We met on Boo. Then Messenger. Then Discord. Then a voice call that lasted until almost midnight.

It wasn't planned. It just... kept going.

✦

She's from Bicol.

She enjoys cosplaying. Tifa is her favorite character to bring to life.

She has an eleven-year-old daughter.

She plays games too — Black Desert, League, Valorant. We talked about MMOs we've played, the ones we're still waiting for.

Those weren't facts I collected.

They were little pieces of someone's life that slowly stopped feeling unfamiliar.

Funny how strangers disappear.

Not all at once.

One conversation at a time.

She said she hoped we'd keep talking for a long while.

I felt the same.

✦

I shared my journal with her.

That's not something I do lightly. Those pages contain parts of me most people never see.

She read it.

Then she told me she cried at one of the special chapters.

The one about forgiving yourself.

I spent months writing that chapter because I needed to understand my own life. I never imagined it would help someone else understand theirs.

✦

She was vulnerable with me too.

She shared pieces of her story — not because I asked, but because she trusted me enough to.

At one point we found ourselves talking about love.

About trust.

About the people we'd given our hearts to before.

Somewhere in the middle of that conversation, we landed on a sentence that stayed with me.

Love is like handing someone a loaded gun, hoping they never choose to use it.

We weren't saying it to sound poetic.

We were saying it because we'd both lived it.

✦

I don't know what Yui will become in my life.

Friend. Acquaintance. Someone I talk to for a while. Someone I know for years.

I don't need to know yet.

What I do know is this:

A few months ago, meeting someone new would've felt like replacing someone old. I would've compared her to Elaine before she even had a chance to be herself.

Tonight, I didn't.

I was simply present.

The past stayed quiet.

And that felt like a milestone I didn't see coming.

✦

A month ago, my journal was a place where I went to survive.

Tonight, someone else found comfort inside it.

I think that's the first time I realized these pages might be more than just my story.

They might be something someone else needed, too.

✦

Today's Weight: 7/10

Not because of the founder backing out.

Because of what happened after.

✦

A few months ago, I thought healing meant learning to hold on.

Then I thought it meant learning to let go.

Now I'm starting to think healing is meeting someone new and realizing your past doesn't need to speak first.

Not because you've forgotten it.

Because it's learning to sit quietly beside you instead.

And tonight...

I think I finally did that.

— Ralph.
