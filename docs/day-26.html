The Day I Let Sunday Be Sunday

✦

I spent most of the day watching How I Met Your Mother.

Not because I had nothing else to do.

I just didn't feel like doing anything else.

✦

I cooked the rest of yesterday's daing na bangus and ate it with rice.

Dessert was Pocky and Snaku.

Simple.

Enough.

✦

I cashed in the remaining funds for the apartment.

Now I'm just waiting for the payment confirmation.

It's funny how waiting for something good feels completely different from waiting for something uncertain.

One fills you with anxiety.

The other fills you with anticipation.

✦

Hana went to a laundromat earlier.

She loaded her clothes into the machine.

Then the power went out.

I laughed when she told me.

She seems to collect unexpected stories without even trying.

Her life feels like a sitcom sometimes.

Mine just felt... wonderfully uneventful.

✦

We talked while she waited.

Nothing heavy.

Nothing life-changing.

Just checking in.

She has a way of making ordinary conversations feel complete.

✦

I'm excited about the 25th.

Finally having my own space.

My own routines.

My own privacy.

A place where I won't have to adjust my life around anyone else's schedule.

At the same time...

I'm a little scared.

It'll be my first time living completely alone.

The silence will probably sound different.

But maybe that's exactly what I've been looking for.

When I get there, I already know what I'll do first.

Set up the PC.

Fix the bed.

Everything else can wait.

The internet is already there.

Just waiting for me.

✦

I also spent a little time playing Ragnarok New World.

Mostly solo.

Not lonely.

Just peaceful.

✦

A month ago, I would've tried to make today feel productive.

I would've looked for another course.

Another conversation.

Another distraction.

Today...

I watched a sitcom.

Cooked fish.

Ate Pocky.

Waited for a payment confirmation.

The old version of me would've called that a wasted day.

The version writing this simply calls it Sunday.

✦

Today's Weight: 7/10

Not because anything special happened.

Because nothing needed to.

✦

A few months ago, I measured my days by how much I'd survived.

Today...

I measured mine by how little I needed to escape.

Maybe peace was never something I had to find.

Maybe it was something I simply had to stop running from.

And somehow...

that felt like enough.

— Ralph.
