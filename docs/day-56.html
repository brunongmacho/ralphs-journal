The Day We Talked Like We Were Already Together

✦

If I wrote Day 56 down, it would look like nothing.

Shift.

Talk to Hana.

Sleep.

Wake up.

RO.

Nothing much happened.

Except that somewhere in it, we started asking each other questions.

✦

"Ma private kang tao?" I asked.

"Or do you like myday or post your RS online?"

"Sakto lang hahahaha," she said.

Memories, she explained. Gala. Outings. Things like that.

Not the "kumakain lang or natutulog tas may cheesy message tas parang powerpoint ung myday" kind.

She laughed at her own example.

"I want to be present naman kung ano ung ginagawa at the moment," she said.

"Dapat andun ung attention namin."

"Di ako naniniwala na pics ung keeper ng memories. I think ung time nyo dun at that time is what makes u go back to remembering it."

"Sameeeee," I said.

"Mapapansin mo pag kasama mo ko. I dont use my phone. Unless icheck ko lang emails or message. As in like 10 secs lang."

"Minsan loud and proud ako eh," I said.

"Gusto ko alam lahat ng tao na partner ko cya. But thats it."

"Ung ginagawa namin. Private na."

She kept the memories by being there.

I kept one photo.

We both liked it that way.

✦

"How about being physical sa partner mo?" I asked.

"Clingy si Hana?"

"Maybe sa public d naman masyado hahahaha," she said.

"Okay lang HHWW ahahahha. Tas for example if nasa ride ung ilelean ko lng ulo sa balikat ganern."

"Nakakakeleeeeg naman yaaaan," I said.

I noted that one for October.

✦

"Selosa ka ba?"

"Hmmm before oo hahaha," she said.

"Nowww maybe not really na. If intentionally pinagseselos ako, out na agad."

"Luh, out kagad talaga? Di man lang iccomfront?"

"If intentional lang naman hahahha."

"Pero yk, if I don't feel secure, then maybe not for me."

It made sense, I said. "Ayaw mo LDR."

"Kasi if may partner man ako, no. Ung iniisip nalang namin dapat is ung ibibuild namin. If ofc part ako sa plano nya."

"Pero if may plano pa magtingin2 ng options ung isa, he is free naman. Pero maybe i don't want to get involved in it anymore."

"Dapat ganyan na talaga mindset," I said. "To settle down na."

"Bat mo sya natanong? Hahahahha."

"To know you," I said. "Like if nagaalign din ba tayo pag dating sa ganyan."

"Kkeokeee. Hb I turn naman the question to you?"

"If seloso ako? Nasa lugar."

"I dont mind na may friends kang lalaki. Pero if i say iwasan mo un, alam ko na kasi may something na un."

"Ihinahayaan ko talaga. To the point na nakakalimutan na nila ung limit nila. Hindi kasi talaga ako mahigpit."

"Parang dapat automatic na din un. Na alam na nung partner ko ung limit nya. Hindi ko na dapat ienforce un."

"Being faithful is something na hindi ko need iremind constantly."

"Thas a non-nego for me. You cheat, i'll walk away. Kahit gaano pa kita kamahal."

"Etoooo, this is what I am trying to say," she said.

"Magiging toxic ka lang if ever pagbibigyan mo ng pagbibigyan," she said. "Kasi the hurt and paranoia will always be there."

"Kasi may history na eh," I said.

Neither of us wants to be watched.

Both of us already know where the line is.

We weren't negotiating.

We were just finding out we already agreed.

✦

"Ang rigid ko daw masyado," she said.

"Sinasabi yan ng mga taong naiintimidate sayo," I said. "Kasi alam nila na they cant handle you."

"Don't get me wrong, ang communicative ko naman," she said. "Pero that rigidness kasi, it's coming from wanting happiness for both of us. Kung sino man ung makakadeal with ko."

"Kasi for me," I said, "bkt ko babaguhin ung isang tao if thats the reason why i did like her at the first place."

"Kung happy ung someone somewhere else, edi why not? I'd wish him well."

"We do have our standards," I said. "Di mo naman need i lower un para sa tao para matanggap ka."

"Parang bare minimum nga lang ung inaask mo eh. Dapat automatic na ganyan na talaga."

"Ok lang yan ah. Just be you, Hana. Cyempre gusto ko din makilala ka. So dont force anything."

"Nakakamaze nga eh. :D"

"Aweeeee, thank youuuu 😭🥹," she said.

We don't lower our standards for each other.

We just found out ours match.

✦

"Pag nagtatampo ka or galit," I asked, "what should your partner do to calm you?"

"Tbh? Maybe space lang muna para makapag isip hahahhaha," she said.

"Let me internalize the situation. Then iniisip ko rin if may nagawa ba ako. Or something. Ends up ako rin ung nagiging apologetic hahahaha."

"Same," I said. "Tatahimik lang ako nyan for awhile. Pero mangyayari ako ung unang maglalambing. 😅"

"Huy oks lang hahaha," she said. "Edi mauubusan na tayo ng questions nyan in person."

We already know how we'd fix a fight we haven't had yet.

✦

The afternoon was quieter.

The mic arrived.

"Mukha talaga syang pangpodcast beeh 😭🤣," she said.

"Maganda daw ung pickup ehhh," I said. "Hindi na putol putol."

She was drowning in work.

"Malalang kape talaga today."

"Samahan kita :D," I said. "Wala padin ako task."

"Thank youuu huhuhu. Pero wala kang choice, same tayong shift hahahha."

I'm going to Makati tomorrow, I told her. A friend's PC. "Mga 400+ ung fare pag mag grab haha."

"Busy ka pa ba?" I asked. "Do you want me to call?"

"We can call naman go lang hahaha," she said. "Pra walang antukin satin."

"Sa discord."

"Cgeee."

She asked if I skate.

"Pwedeeeeeé. Hahaha natry ko palng ice skating."

"Sige2, go try natin ung rink if d crowded," she said.

One more thing for October.

✦

We talked about the other thing too.

The one that doesn't make it into a recap.

We don't want to revolve around each other.

We still have priorities. Careers. Friends. Lives we're still building.

We said it like it was obvious.

Because to us, it was.

A relationship should add to the life you're building.

Not become the whole life.

✦

If I wrote Day 56 down, it would look like nothing.

But in one ordinary day, we covered how we'd love.

How we'd get jealous.

How we'd fight.

How we'd fix it.

What we'd never lower.

We talked like people who had already decided.

We just haven't met yet.

Kulang na lang talaga... mag-meet tayo.

— Ralph.
