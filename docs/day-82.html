The Day the Movie Played Anyway

✦

I stayed home all Sunday, ate once, and still the day ended full.

The NTE hadn't answered. The applications hadn't either. It was a quiet day in a stretch of quiet days, and the plan still hadn't come through. But by the end of it we were in Discord watching The Substance, then Grafted, and the rain had lost.

It started with movies, which I should have taken as the sign it was.

And then The Substance played anyway, at whatever hour the rain finally released her, and Grafted after it, two body-horror movies that somehow made the whole night feel lighter.

By nine in the evening she sent the one song the night had earned. Endor, Pump It Up.

The movie played anyway. That's the part worth keeping.

The NTE is still answering at its own speed. The applications are still thinking. I spent a Sunday at home with one meal and a little job hunting in the pockets between, and it would have been easy to call it a flat day.

But she was the one who planned it. She kept it alive through the rain. She bought popcorn for a movie that almost never happened, and I watched it with her like it was the only thing in the world.

Some days don't need to do much. They just need to get to 3 PM and have someone agreeing to meet you there.

— Ralph.
