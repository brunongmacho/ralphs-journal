The Day I Forgot

✦

Today was... ordinary.

I logged out at 7 AM and went straight to bed.

No overthinking.

No journal.

Just sleep.

✦

Around 2 PM, the delivery guy woke me up.

My new pillows finally arrived.

I actually got excited over pillows.

I opened the package, smiled for a second, then went back to sleep.

I guess adulthood is celebrating things that would've bored me a few years ago.

✦

I woke up again around 5 PM.

Went outside for a late brunch.

Took a shower.

Came home.

Turned on Mr. D.

Nothing unexpected happened today.

No heavy conversations.

No difficult emotions.

No surprises.

Just another afternoon inside my apartment.

✦

Around 6 PM I suddenly remembered.

"Oh shit... my journal."

Thirty-four straight days...

and today was the first time I completely forgot about it.

Not because I was avoiding it.

I genuinely forgot.

That feels different.

Maybe that's a good sign.

✦

I finished my Ragnarok dailies.

Logged in for work at 11 PM.

Sat down at my desk.

My desk.

My room.

My apartment.

I don't think that feeling has fully sunk in yet.

Every time I sit here, I still catch myself thinking,

"Oh... this is home now."

✦

Nothing remarkable happened today.

But I don't think every day needs to become a chapter.

Some days exist simply to remind us what normal feels like.

And after everything that's happened these past few weeks...

normal feels pretty nice.

✦

— Ralph.
