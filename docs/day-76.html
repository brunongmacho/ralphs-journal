The Day I Didn't Sleep

✦

I hadn't slept yet since yesterday I woke up.

It was one in the afternoon, and I was starving again.

The boss fight was food. It always is now.

✦

The day had actually started in the dark.

Last night I asked her about the internet. This morning, she answered.

"feel ko papakonek ako ng net hahaha"

"Ay baket? Mabagal?"

"Di naman… pero pgkatest ko mejo low nga ung DL and UL speed"

"and gagamit pa naman akong VPN so"

"Ay oo kailangan mo nga magpakabit. Ano naman sabi nung may ari?"

She needs it for work. For when she's in the new place.

The night kept going. I kept being awake.

✦

By lunch she was out somewhere.

"nasan ka na now? pauwi ka na?"

"naglalunch lang tas uuwi na"

"9pm pasok ko mamaya pero 6 hrs shift lang"

"Ano food mo"

"siomai lang tsaka gulay… nagdimsum break lang aakezzz"

"Sana all hehe"

"sana ol ka jan hahaha dapat nga d kami operational ee"

"holiday sa US mamaya"

"Aba. Atleast may pay hahahaha"

Sana all. Her shifts are always shorter than mine.

✦

Then she started telling me about the place.

"maganda talaga dun pero prang mainit sya sa afternoon so kelangan magbukas talaga ng pinto"

"which is understandable naman… pero mas mainit ung samin kesa dun hahaha"

"so ung problema nalang talaga is ung net and if marunong ako magtipid ng kuryente hahaha"

"Saka pag afternoon feel ko nasa dagat ka naman eh."

"sa bagayyyy pero magbabaon nako dun ang mamahal ng kape nila"

The sea. The afternoons. The room she can finally leave home through.

She showed me the sunset.

"ganda ng sunset sa myday ko noooo hahahaha"

"Kaya nga ehhhhh. D mo ko vinideo call. Nakita ko din sana."

"natutulog ka naman nun"

She meant the afternoon I slept through.

I let that one sit.

✦

Later that evening, I was still up.

"Malay mo gising ako nun."

"Shift mo na kagaaaad. Naol. Idlip pa ulet ako."

"okeoke posxz sleep well"

"Kamusta na dyan?"

I finally lay down.

✦

And I thought about it.

She's almost out. She said so herself. "mejo malapit na aa"

The internet, the electricity. The last walls between her and that room by the sea.

On the calm days I count them off in my head. Food. RO. Her.

Tonight the difference was time.

I hadn't slept yet since yesterday I woke up.

And I'd been awake for all of her.

— Ralph.
