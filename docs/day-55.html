The Day October Got a Map

✦

It was Monday.

I bought bread.

My stomach hurt.

RO.

Waiting for my shift.

Nothing about the day said important.

✦

The night before had been weird too.

"Mainit ung hinga ko eh," I said.

Bioflu.

"Huy if magpersist yan haaa magpacheck ka po," she said.

"Lalo na ikaw lang mag isa jan."

"May HMO ka naman just in case?"

It's strange.

Having someone worry about you on a Monday.

For no reason except that you exist.

✦

Later, I asked her to marathon a show with me.

"Noooo aantukin nga ako jaan hahahahhaha."

"Prang may commitment issues ako pag mahaba ung papanoorin ko."

Then she got quiet.

"Anw sorry I appreciate the thought thooo hope d kita naoffend huhu."

I laughed.

"Kung maganda naman ung papanuorin feel ko hindi ka aantukin," I said.

"Napapakain ka pa nga ng madami. 😂"

She laughed at that one.

She remembered the movie too.

✦

"Btw nakapunta ka naman na ng Intramuros no?"

I grew up inside those walls.

Mapua is inside Intramuros.

Tambayan namin yun nung college.

Bilyaran.

Inuman.

Walls.

"Lista mo na lahat," I said. "Puntahan natin. :)"

She started a list.

Binondo.

"Magkalapit lang un."

Museum of Fine Arts?

"Tatawid lang."

"Gawin natin. Museum muna. Then kain binondo. Then tambay walls ng intra. Habang nagpapatunaw ng kinain hahaha."

"Huyyy huhuhu kaexcite naman."

"So... it's a date? :D"

"Alright I think it indeed is hahahaha."

October stopped being a question.

It became a route.

The dive could wait. "Basta di prio un."

✦

She's crocheting something for Bali.

"Baka umabot for mnl HAHAHA."

A see-through dress, she joked, at a museum.

"Sa gilid nga ng pool."

"EEEE hahahaha alam mooo mahihiya ako pag ganyan."

"Dahil makikita ko?"

"Well no pressure Hana. 😉"

Even her friends don't see her like that, she said.

"I wont force anything naman talaga. :D"

"Awwww thank you 🥺🥺🥺"

"Lahat dapat may consent," I said.

And then she told me something I didn't expect.

"For what it's worth I appreciate it… not used to this response sa lahat ng mga nacommu ko so far. It's unfortunate I got to exp those."

I'm not special.

I'm just not one of the bad ones.

And apparently, that's rare enough to thank.

✦

She asked about my past.

"Di ba sabi mo mapuan ka?" she asked.

"So like nakakaangat ka pala sa buhay ee. Hahahaha."

"Luh d ako. Ung nanay ko. Hahahaha."

College funds that went under.

Two years out of five.

Working student after that.

Medyo bulakbol ako nung bata.

"Gusto nila mag nursing ako. Hahahah."

"Takot nga ako sa injection. 😂😂😂😂"

"Mahina ako mag memorize. More on math tlga hilig ko. Or logic."

"Pansin mo din cguro the way i plan things."

"Chaotic pero alam ko kung saan nakalagay. 😅"

She laughed at that one too.

✦

Then I asked about hers.

Licensed to teach.

"Just to discover d ko sya calling hahajhaa."

Arts wasn't practical, so she didn't take it.

"Nursing or archi sana."

She showed me old drawings.

YouTube tutorials, she said.

"Uy gagi. Anong marunong lang ka dyan."

She drew Whang Od once.

Gave it to her when she visited.

"Angas nun."

"Tagal na ng mga yan. I'm trying to get my spark back haysss."

"Kaya mo yan," I said. "Draw ka ulit. Ako no 1 fan mo."

"Now focus muna maging good freediver hahahha."

"Dapat makasabay ako sayo if ever magfefreedive tayo no."

"Huy d ako magaling ahhhhh," I said. "Marunong lang ako."

"D nga ako marunong mag frenzel eh. Wahahahahahaha."

✦

Later, I logged in early.

"Henloooo waiting nalang ako ng shift. Nag login na ako para d malate hahaha."

It was just a Monday.

Bread.

Stomach ache.

RO.

And somewhere in it, October finally had a map.

Museum first.

Then Binondo.

Then the walls I used to call home.

She said she's not used to being treated like this.

I told her the gentlemen aren't extinct yet.

It was just a Monday.

And it was the biggest one in a while.

— Ralph.
