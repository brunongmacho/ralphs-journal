The Day Boredom Was Welcome

✦

Shift ended at 7 AM.

I slept.

Woke up at 11:22.

First thing I did:

Open the PC.

Play RO.

Do my dailies.

Starving.

Went out to find something to eat.

✦

Forty-four days ago, waking up meant looking for relief.

Something to escape into.

This morning, it was just a routine.

Open the PC.

Play RO.

Do my dailies.

Every day.

The last few entries, and even Ami's email yesterday, made this page feel thin.

✦

I was starving.

That used to be math.

"I only have ₱160."

"Budget until the 7th."

Today it was just a feeling.

"I'm hungry."

Small thing.

But being able to eat without thinking of it as arithmetic...

that's a quiet luxury.

✦

It's funny.

Ragnarok found me when I needed a distraction.

Now it's just part of the morning.

Wake up.

Dailies.

Then the rest of the day.

Maybe routine was what I was really asking for all along.

✦

The song today was Maximillian — Beautiful Scars.

This song reminds me where I've been.

June was "I'll Never Love Again."

July was "Sunflower."

August is "Beautiful Scars."

Still carrying scars.

Just no longer hiding them.

✦

"What is lost ain't gone... 'cause it's a part of you that will make you strong."

My mom.

In "Drops of Jupiter."

Mama Del.

In every Chowking.

Jinro.

An old howl.

My past.

In the version of me who learned how hard I could love.

Nothing is lost.

It became part of me.

✦

It isn't a coincidence this played after Ami's email.

That email could've reopened everything.

It gave me another bruise.

Didn't let it become a wound.

I'm not trying to become who I was before.

I'm learning to become who I am now.

✦

Nothing happened today.

No breakthrough.

No heartbreak.

No surprise.

Just a shift.

Breakfast.

A game.

Hunger.

Food.

Maybe that's the lesson.

Not every page has to change your life.

Some pages exist to remind you that you finally have one.

— Ralph.
