The Day Ordinary Was Enough

✦

I finished my shift at 7 AM and went to sleep.

That was the first thing that happened on Day 21.

Not a realization. Not a breakthrough. Just sleep.

I woke up around noon, went out to eat, came home, and watched Eminence in Shadow Season 2.

I reached out to more tattoo artists. Sent them my concept. Waited for replies.

At 10:44 PM, I logged into work.

My dinner was lychee.

That was the whole day.

✦

Not every day needs to be the day everything changed.

Sometimes the day is just:

Sleep.

Eat.

Watch something.

Talk to people.

Work.

And that's enough.

I noticed my journal entries are getting shorter.

That felt strange at first.

Like I was losing something.

But I think what's actually happening is simpler.

I don't need as many words to explain my day anymore.

Because there's nothing to survive.

No crisis.

No heartbreak.

No emergency waiting around the corner.

Just life.

And for the first time in a long while...

ordinary life feels like enough.

— Ralph.
