Thursday, July 16, 2026

3 min read

The Day the Hours Were Mine

✦

Day 23 started with me being 30 minutes late for my shift.

Not because I was overwhelmed. Not because I stayed up overthinking. I just overslept.

Which is funny considering how many nights I used to struggle to sleep at all.

Somewhere between healing and ordinary life, I developed the ability to accidentally sleep through alarms.

Progress comes in strange forms.

✦

I woke up around noon and stayed in bed for a while. At 2 PM, I went out to Bon Appetea and ordered chicken carbonara with a mango lychee drink. Before heading home, I bought Sprite and fries because I felt like it.

No reason. Just wanted them.

I spent the afternoon watching anime and trying Ragnarok New World.

Small things.

But they filled the afternoon.

✦

Hana was already asleep for our shift later when I woke up. But by the time she was up, we picked up where we always do.

She went out to buy veggies for her and her cats. Dropped by a cafe to study.

About her course.

About the cert she's working toward.

About how she learns better hands-on too.

About the Backrooms movie — I mentioned it because I played the game and got curious how they adapted it.

She said she'd use it as background while studying.

About tacos and chicken pasta and mango lychee.

She sent a photo of her food. I sent one of mine.

"Grabe naman tugtugan dito nakakarelapse."

I sent her a voice message of the music playing at the cafe.

About freediving gear. Hers arrived — all black. She said it could pass for a bank robber outfit.

"Sakto naghahanap ako ng pwedeng bangkong manakawan."

She laughed.

About a lot of things that don't need to be listed to matter.

✦

Somewhere in the middle of the conversation, I mentioned that Elaine messaged me.

A new game launched. She asked if I was going to play.

We had plans to play it together before everything happened.

I didn't reply.

Hana didn't tell me what to do. She just said:

"It's of your own volition on how you place her in your life."

I liked that she didn't make it about her.

✦

Around 7:30 PM, I cooked pancit canton with salted egg and tomatoes. Watched Backrooms with Eana over Discord.

A horror movie.

Someone to watch it with.

A shift that started later.

Nothing connected those moments.

And somehow, that was the point.

✦

Today's Weight: 7/10

Not because anything special happened.

Because nothing needed to.

No realizations about healing.

No reflections on the past.

No dramatic breakthroughs.

Just a Thursday where I overslept, ate well, talked to people I enjoy, and ended the day watching a horror movie with a friend.

The hours were mine.

And that was already enough.

✦

A few months ago, I would've tried to figure out where every conversation was leading.

Today, I'm just grateful they're happening.

A carbonara lunch I didn't plan.

A voice message about nakakarelapse music.

A bank robber freediving suit.

A horror movie with salted egg on the side.

A day that belonged to no one but me.

And for now...

that's enough.

— Ralph.
