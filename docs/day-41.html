The Day the Guitar Came Back

✦

It's 3:50 AM.

I'm still awake.

Supernatural is playing.

A month ago, staying up this late meant something else.

It meant replaying conversations.

Trying to understand someone else's feelings.

Tonight, I couldn't sleep because of something entirely different.

My first founder meeting.

At 11 PM.

That's a different kind of future.

One I built.

✦

I don't think I was anxious.

I think my body simply knew today mattered.

So it quietly decided,

"Let's stay awake a little longer."

I finally fell asleep around four.

Five hours later, I woke up.

My first thought wasn't fear.

It was food.

✦

Adobo.

Laing.

Brunch.

Then Sunflower started playing.

Post Malone.

The Spider-Verse soundtrack.

A song about staying with someone while life keeps pulling people in different directions.

Warm.

Bittersweet.

A few weeks ago, I kept replaying I'll Never Love Again.

Grief at full volume.

Today...

Sunflower.

Maybe that's what acceptance sounds like.

✦

There's a lyric:

"You'll be left in the dust, unless I stuck by ya."

Most people hear it as a promise to someone else.

Today I heard something different.

I'm not waiting for someone else to stay anymore.

I've been the one staying.

Even during the boring days.

Even when I only had ₱160.

Even when songs made me cry.

Somewhere along the way...

I became the person who stayed for Ralph.

✦

By 3 PM, I was eating Hawaiian pizza.

₱96.

A few days ago, I was calculating how to survive on ₱26 a day.

Today...

I bought pizza.

It tasted like progress.

And yes...

pineapple belongs on pizza.

I don't care what anyone says.

✦

Then I picked up my guitar.

For the first time in about a month.

I was rusty.

I kept forgetting the lyrics.

First,

"I Like You So Much, You'll Know It."

Simple.

Comforting.

The kind of song you play because it feels familiar.

Then...

A Thousand Years.

That's when everything stopped.

✦

The chorus caught me.

My eyes filled with tears.

I could barely get the words out.

The strange part was...

I wasn't thinking about anyone.

Not Elaine.

Not Yui.

Not anyone.

The tears weren't for a person.

They came from somewhere much deeper.

Forty days of writing.

Forty days of opening doors I'd kept locked for years.

Mom.

Lola.

Elaine.

Jinro.

Coco.

The younger version of me.

Forgiveness.

Peace.

The song simply found the place where all of those things now live.

✦

A month ago...

I probably would've stopped playing.

Today...

I kept going.

Rusty fingers.

Forgotten lyrics.

A shaky voice.

And somehow...

that felt more honest than playing it perfectly ever could.

Maybe coming home to yourself isn't supposed to sound polished.

Maybe it's supposed to sound exactly like that.

✦

Afterward, I shared the songs with Yui.

Not to impress her.

Not because I wanted to say something indirectly.

Just...

"Hey, I played today."

We talked about music for a while.

That was enough.

✦

I've noticed pieces of myself quietly returning.

The writer.

The gamer.

The designer.

The VA.

The guitarist.

The singer.

One by one...

they've all started coming home.

✦

By 10:30 PM, it was finally time.

Nesting first.

Then the founder meeting.

Forty days ago, I started this journal because I was trying to survive.

Tonight, I was walking into my first founder meeting.

Not to prove I was good enough.

Simply to meet someone and see whether we could build something together.

That's all.

✦

Whatever happens after tonight...

I've already done the hardest part.

I kept showing up.

Every single day.

And that's something nobody can ever take away.

✦

— Ralph.
