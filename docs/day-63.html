The Day She Sent the Footage

✦

7 AM. Shift done. I hadn't done much because I was still really sick. Told myself I'd catch up later. By 6:20 PM the plan was still the same: eat, sleep, wake up for the next shift. Third day of this.

✦

She was back from Moalboal. Sent the video her photographer took. "Naunahan ako magpost ng vid ng photographer ko hahaha." I said patingin. Then: "Hala ang gandaaaaaa." She said "nako if alam nyo lang BTS nyan." I asked if she had the raw videos. She did but couldn't send them directly. "Save mo sa Google Drive tapos send mo sakin ung link." She joked: "tara recommend kita kay kuya hahaha." I said sana kapitbahay lang kita eh no. Ganun lang kadali umalis.

✦

She started watching her own diving videos and cringed. "Ampanget ng finning huhuhu." I told her the form just needed correcting. Keep it straight. She said she panicked through most of the fundive. The sea was too alive. Too much happening at once. But her breath hold kept improving. Patagal nang patagal kada lusong.

✦

Then I asked how much a flight to Cebu costs. Long weekend next week. No Monday shift. She said "sanaolsss dami holiday." Around14k round trip. She said "paBali na yang mga yan aa." I said next yr pplanuhin ko na nga mga holidays. Tap0s ppuntahan talaga kita dyan.

She said "Lezgowww hahahahha."

— Ralph.
