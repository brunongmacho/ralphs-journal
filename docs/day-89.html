The Day I Built December

✦

The day was slow. I said so out loud before it was even ten in the morning.

"this day is kinda slow. its just 9:27am now"

It was the day after the cancelled plan. Nowhere to be, nothing planned. The kind of day that has to fill itself.

So I'll tell it the way it filled itself.

✦

It started hungry. Five fifty in the morning and I was starving, so I went out before the day could decide what it was.

"im back. so i got okoy and gising gising with rice"

Spicy, of course. The vinegar dip on the okoy, the sigarilyas in the gising gising, rice under both.

Then the day settled into its usual positions. Master Cutter from episode seventy-six to seventy-nine, with all the cringe action, the guns that never reload. The dailies in my RO new world, a high priest who plays solo. A new vape flavor that tastes like bubblegum.

The morning filled itself. Quietly.

✦

By nine twenty-seven I had run out of ways to fill it.

"i want something to do that is useful but not the job hunt."

I said no to the useful suggestions that sounded like chores. Practicing the bicol, the apartment, the plans. Not those. The day had to make something, not get managed.

So I made a playlist. The rules were simple. It didn't get to feel like a love mixtape. It was the month's feel, December in this country, everything that has been building since June. Some songs for her, some for the season. A small name, nothing that announces itself. Made now, heard later, sitting in a drawer until December.

The songs came in order, and then I rearranged them.

"now can we rearrange it like telling a confesion."

So it became one. It starts with not knowing how to say it and ends with walking five hundred miles to fall down at her door. In between, the confession itself, the shy part after it, the loud part, the joy, the promise. I knew the Galway Girl I meant was the one from P.S. I Love You, not the famous one. I added Lay Me Down last, because the middle of it might as well be my grammar.

Then the name. I tried small ones and none of them sat right. I said I'd hold the title until it came.

"The Songs That Remember You"

I wrote it with an exclamation first. I took it out. Some names read better as a whisper.

I made a special chapter about it while it was all still fresh.

✦

By eleven the day was still slow, and that was fine.

"im still looking forward for her to succeed on her solo life"

Hana was out doing her day. Laundry, food for Torta, the pregnant cat, maybe a coffee shop, maybe a jog and the night market later. I still didn't know the plans. I was just looking forward to her succeeding at living alone. She wins first. Then December is whatever comes after that.

And if my Cebu trip this December pushes through, the deal is already on the table. She cooks for me, I cook the bicol.

✦

The afternoon turned into a movie plan. She doesn't like romance movies. Horror, Korean films, snacks before every movie. I kept the options coming until we landed on The Prestige.

She was still at the mall, so the movie moved. That was fine. I said take her time.

I bought the snacks before she was even home. Piattos and v-cut, large ones, and six pieces of spanish bread. Snacks ready, movie waiting, only Hana left. And then, while I waited, I ate the spanish bread. All six of them, before the movie even started.

The call started at 3:42. The Prestige over Discord, the way we do these things. New discovery in the middle of it.

"wow new discovery. we both love david bowie. he is nikola tesla in the movie."

By six we were done. We loved it. Her iPad died on her, she forgot to charge it. Tenet next maybe. She'd jog, go to the night market, and if she wasn't sleepy, Tenet. I'd take a nap first, I said. And I did.

✦

The evening came from the other side of the log, so I'll tell it the honest way. She went jogging, then to the night market. She sent a photo from Magellan's Cross and said the crowd got to her, so she rested there and went home early. I was asleep through most of it. The Tenet plan quietly fell through.

We talked at night anyway. There's something I keep: her brace adjustment tomorrow at ten in the morning, two months late. And my own piece, the one I don't say often. Car accident in 2017. My face hit the steering wheel. Surgery, when the budget allows.

"Anw. Pahinga ka na hana. Goodnight sayo. See you tom. ☺️"

✦

The day was okoy, fourteen songs, six pieces of spanish bread, a movie over a call, a photo from Magellan's Cross, a goodnight.

Slow, start to finish.

But there's a drawer now that wasn't there this morning. It's not empty.

December is built. It just has to get here.

— Ralph.
