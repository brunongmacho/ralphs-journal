The Day Later Became Today

✦

The plan was simple. Sleep at four in the afternoon, wake up before the shift at eleven. That was the whole schedule.

The sleep came at eight. The shift came at eleven.

Everything else just happened on its own. So I'll tell it the way it went.

✦

It started before five in the morning, looking for something to watch. I wanted a series that would poke my mind, make me think, and not talk too much, since I fall asleep to that kind of show. I went through every suggestion. Severance, From, Black Mirror, Umbrella Academy, Twin Peaks. Done. The Nolan movies. Done. Even the Adam Sandler ones.

"this is sad. even all the adam sandler movies. i have finished them"

The shelf was empty, so the day moved to what was already running. Master Cutter had two episodes left. I finished ninety-eight and waited for the last two to air. I already know the ending will cringe. Then the new Jo Koy special, and the first bit was about a dentist disaster. She was at the dentist that morning. I couldn't believe the timing.

The game shelf was the same story. Automation without enemies, sandbox, nothing to fight and everything to build. Shapez, Shapez 2, Factorio, Satisfactory, Dyson Sphere Program, all of them played already. One suggestion finally stuck. Oxygen Not Included. A colony with no enemies in it.

✦

Hana's day had one appointment, and it was two months late. The brace. She was getting ready for it before eight, afraid of getting scolded this time.

"masesermonan ako nito ng dentist ko hahahhaa"

I told her to update me. The report came closer to noon. She wasn't scolded.

"d naman ako nasermonan"

One tooth still needs fixing, she said. From now on it's monthly.

"soooo dapat every month na talaga akong pupunta dun"

She tried to bundle the day into one trip, but SSS had no walk-in slots and the Pag-IBIG at the mall was closed. The errands ended there. She went home.

The talk went to teeth. She asked if I'd ever get mine fixed, and I told her the state of them. The 2017 accident, my face on the steering wheel, the structure that got weak, some already gone. Veneers where they can still be saved.

"so dentures talaga"

Her brace finally happened today. Mine is still on the list. But at least it all got said.

✦

The movie came from her first.

"so anooo watch naaa?"

Her room's roof leaked while it rained, so she cleaned up before anything else and said she'd watch while doing other things. The roof gets fixed when the rain stops. I said I'd call on Discord.

Somewhere in it, I remembered I hadn't told anyone yet.

"we are watching tenet. me and hana"

The movie we put off yesterday, the plan that quietly fell through. This time it was just playing, the two of us over the call, the way we do these things. Even mid-watch the catalog kept running. I asked about the Nicolas Cage movie where he can see the future. Next. Two minutes ahead, and he plays every possible future until one of them wins.

✦

The sleep came after the shower. I tried at five. The coffee had other plans.

"so we tried to sleep at 5pm. i slept around 8pm because i drank coffee. and i just work up around 10:40pm. and now its 11:35pm and it is my shift."

The plan slipped by three hours. The shift didn't.

The day was two empty shelves and one new colony, a dentist who didn't scold, a movie that waited a day, a nap that came three hours late. Not one thing was on time except the shift.

But everything that was supposed to happen happened. The brace. The movie. The sleep. Nothing moved until it had to, and it all still got here.

Later became today.

— Ralph.
