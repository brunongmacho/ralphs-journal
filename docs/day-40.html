The Day I Let the Day Pass

✦

I woke up around nine.

Made brunch.

Adobo and bread.

That was the whole plan for the morning.

And it was enough.

✦

Supernatural was on.

Third rewatch.

A first watch is about the story.

A second watch is about catching the foreshadowing.

A third watch is about spending time with old friends.

I already know what happens to Sam and Dean.

I know which episodes I skip.

I know which ones I never skip.

Nothing surprises me anymore.

✦

Everything else in my life is new.

New apartment.

New routine.

New everything.

So my brain reached for the one thing that isn't changing.

Supernatural.

It isn't escape anymore.

A month ago, I watched to outrun my own thoughts.

Today I watched it the way you eat comfort food.

While grinding RO.

While talking to Hana.

While talking to Yui.

✦

Something changed in the way I talk to people.

There's no pressure with Hana anymore.

There's no expectation with Yui.

I don't feel the need to decide where either conversation is going.

They're just... people I'm talking to.

That used to exhaust me.

Not knowing what someone is to me.

Today, not knowing felt like space.

✦

It's 7:29 PM.

I'm writing this at the end of a day that looked like:

Woke up.

Food.

Series.

RO.

People.

Sleep comes next.

Repeat.

That's it.

A month ago, a day like this would've ended with me asking why nothing was happening.

Today the answer is simple:

Nothing needs to.

✦

I think I finally get it now.

Not every day has to teach you something.

Sometimes a day's only job is to let you live it.

June was about surviving.

July was about choosing.

Maybe August is about being allowed to just... have a day.

An ordinary one.

Without it needing to mean anything except that I'm okay.

✦

And then there's Castiel.

My favorite character.

Not Dean.

Not Sam.

Castiel.

The quiet one.

The awkward one.

The one who doesn't always know what to say.

But the one who, when he chooses someone...

stays.

That's the part I recognize.

I've been writing Castiel sentences all month without realizing it.

"I'm here."

"Kakampi mo po ako."

"I didn't fix any of them. I just didn't leave."

That's what he does.

He doesn't stay because he gets something back.

He stays because that's who he is.

✦

My July letter ended with:

"I wasn't becoming someone new.

I was becoming someone who chose his own life."

I wrote that before today.

I didn't realize it was already the story of my favorite character.

He stops living by Heaven's plan.

He starts making his own choices.

Bad ones.

Good ones.

Messy ones.

But his.

✦

I used to think my favorite Cas moment was his introduction.

"I'm the one who gripped you tight and raised you from perdition."

It's not anymore.

It's the line he says much later.

"The one thing I want... it's something I know I can't have."

Not because it's romantic.

Because it's the moment he finally accepts that loving someone doesn't guarantee you get to keep them.

And he loves them anyway.

That's the part that hits different now.

✦

So that was Day 40.

Woke up.

Food.

Series.

RO.

People.

Sleep.

The first real routine I've had in a long time.

When life is chaotic, routines feel boring.

When life has been chaotic for a long time...

routines become a luxury.

I think I've finally found one.

✦

— Ralph.
