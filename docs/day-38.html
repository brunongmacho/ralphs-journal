The Day the Silence Got Loud

✦

I slept through my whole shift.

Tasks were already done.

No new ones came.

For the first time in a long while, work asked nothing of me.

I woke up at 9 AM starving.

That was my first thought.

Not someone.

Not something.

Just hunger.

A month ago, I would've woken up thinking about a person.

Today I woke up thinking about food.

I think that counts as progress.

✦

Then I did something I haven't done in six years.

I bought a pack of Marlboro.

Lit one.

The nicotine hit me almost immediately.

My head spun.

I sat down and stared at the wall for a while, wondering why I thought this would help.

When I finally asked myself why...

I gave the most honest answer I had.

"I needed something to take the edge off."

Not because I missed smoking.

Not because I wanted to start again.

Because the edge was there.

And for one brief moment, an old habit felt easier than sitting with it.

✦

I think the edge has been there for weeks.

I've just been keeping it busy.

Work.

Movies.

RO.

The journal.

The apartment.

Everything I built to fill the space.

But today there were no tasks.

No deadlines.

Nothing demanding my attention.

And when life finally got quiet...

the silence knocked.

Not because the apartment is bad.

Because my mind finally had room to feel everything it had been avoiding.

✦

The cigarette didn't help.

It just made me dizzy.

My body basically said,

"What the hell are you doing?"

That's when it hit me.

This isn't me anymore.

Six years ago, a cigarette promised relief.

Today it was just smoke.

Whatever I was looking for wasn't inside it anymore.

I put the pack down.

That was the last one.

Not because I was strong.

Because I didn't even want it.

✦

Maybe that's what this apartment is becoming.

A place that keeps showing me what I no longer need.

First it showed me I could be alone.

Then it showed me I could be peaceful.

Today it showed me something else.

The old ways of coping no longer fit the person living here.

✦

I don't know what's supposed to come next.

I only know that a month ago, I needed something to take the edge off almost every single day.

Today, I reached for an old version of myself.

He wasn't there anymore.

And somehow...

that feels like the most hopeful thing that's happened in weeks.

✦

— Ralph.
