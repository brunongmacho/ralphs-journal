The Day a Maybe Got a Date

✦

Day fifty.

I slept from around five in the morning until one in the afternoon.

Eight hours.

Probably collected some of the sleep I've been owing myself.

I didn't see the halfway mark arrive.

I was asleep.

Maybe that's fine.

Milestones don't need an audience.

✦

Woke up.

Bought food.

Ate.

By five PM, I was starving again.

My metabolism has apparently decided that Day Fifty is a feeding day.

At least hunger is a problem I know how to solve.

✦

Fifty days ago, a meal came with math.

I'd count the pesos before counting the plates.

Today, the only calculation was what food I wanted.

"I'm hungry," I said.

And I went and got food.

That's it.

That's the whole thing.

✦

I also made something today.

A little auto clicker, just for my game.

Not downloaded.

Built.

I was tired of clicking, so I made myself a small helper instead.

It's the programming I've been learning, finally doing something useful.

It solved a tiny problem.

My tiny problem.

A few months ago, I would've just downloaded one.

Today, I built it.

Small proof that the lessons are landing.

✦

I remember when we were just messages.

Then she found me on Facebook.

Then there was a call.

The first time I heard her voice.

"Sorry ang lalim ng boses ko," I said.

She said it was fine.

After that, we found ourselves on Discord.

A soundtrip in the server.

Hall & Oates.

A song I used to sing at videoke.

That's how fast it can happen.

Strangers become people you talk to every day.

✦

Today, Hana and I talked almost the whole day.

We both woke up late.

We teased each other about sleeping.

She asked if I headlock people.

I told her I'm a good big spoon.

Then we talked about types.

I told her mine isn't really about a checklist.

It's about someone who simply makes sense to talk to.

She's one of only three people I actually talk to regularly.

Small circle.

On purpose.

I chose to tell her that.

Because small circles are a choice.

So is letting someone in.

✦

Then she said she might come to Manila around October.

Coffee, I asked.

Maybe Binondo, she said.

"Malayo pa naman," she told me.

She's right.

We'll cross that bridge when we get there.

It's not a plan.

Not really.

But it's the first time "someday" had a season.

October.

✦

Fifty days.

Day one, I was surviving.

Day fifty, I slept in, ate twice, played, built a tool because clicking was too much work, and spent the day talking to someone who wasn't there fifty days ago.

The urgent things now are small.

The life around me is not.

And maybe that's the real milestone.

I didn't see fifty days arrive.

I was too busy living them.

— Ralph.
