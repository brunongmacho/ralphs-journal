The Day the Quiet Got an Answer

✦

My shift passed without much happening.

I watched things.

Slept.

Played RO.

Waited for emails.

None came.

And it was alright.

✦

I logged out around 7 AM.

Dailies first, like always.

Then binagoongan for breakfast.

By 3 PM I was hungry again, so I went out for lunch, with Supernatural waiting for me when I got back.

I think this is just my routine now.

Not something I'm escaping from.

Just the shape of my days.

And I'm okay with that.

✦

Hana and I traded songs today.

Told each other what we were doing during work hours.

Sent each other funny reels while we both doom-scrolled.

None of it needed to mean anything.

It was just two people passing pieces of their day back and forth.

She's still saving for that place in Moalboal.

"Wait lang ang pera," she said.

I told her I'm rooting for her.

She said that if she's lucky, she'd live there.

I told her she can do it.

Sometimes that's all a conversation needs to be.

Someone saying,

"I believe you can."

✦

At some point, I stopped waiting and wrote something myself.

A follow-up email.

Warm.

Low-pressure.

I let them know the content calendar for the founder was ready.

And I asked, softly, if there was any word on whether she had already reached Doneverse about her personal issue.

I'm not in a hurry.

I just wanted to keep my side of the door open.

The whole day, I'd been waiting for emails that never came.

So I sent one of my own.

✦

9 PM.

Dinner, bought.

Series, playing.

The shift waiting for me at the end of the night.

A few months ago, a day like this would have sat on me like unfinished business.

Tonight, it just felt like a day.

Full of small things.

And when the quiet didn't speak...

I answered it myself.

— Ralph.
