The Day 3 PM Showed Up

✦

I got an email that was mostly blanks.

Founder name, to be confirmed.

Founder email, to be confirmed.

Launch date, to be confirmed.

But one line was filled in.

Doer shift time in PH time: 3:00 PM - 11:00 PM.

The business name was GeoCale. A Google Business and AI Search agency.

Onboarding, September 4. Today.

And then, at the bottom, the sentence that kept me honest.

Your matched status is not yet confirmed.

✦

I've spent so long with 11 PM meaning my day starts now.

Thirty nights of black coffee and a 7-Eleven breakfast at seven in the morning.

3 PM to 11 PM would be a different person's life.

An afternoon that starts in daylight.

Evenings that belong to me instead of the graveyard.

I didn't let myself celebrate yet.

But for one quiet stretch of the day, the future felt a little more shaped.

✦

The night broke with her still a little low.

"napaidlip na nga ako eh. kamusta naman pakiramdam mo? sad ka padin ba?"

"yeah but i'm alr naman."

"sure? sabi ka lang ha. dito lang ako."

She said she was okay, overall.

"minsan need mo kasi talaga ilabas para makagaan ng loob eh."

I let it sit.

✦

Somewhere in the middle we traded food again.

I said I was craving something and couldn't decide.

She'd ordered Thai food. Pad thai and mushroom kra pao.

"teka nga nainggit ako hanap ako sa grab haha"

But it was late, and only fastfood was left.

"lumabas ako. bumili ng lugaw and egg with laman. tapos tokwat baboy haha"

She sent photos. Huge servings again.

"250 each hahaha mejo mahal pero eto nalang ung mejo healthy na option na bukas pa."

My lugaw, her pad thai.

Two people eating at the same hour, showing each other what they got.

✦

Then home pulled at her again.

"haysss na sestress ulit ako dito sa bahay"

Her mama was out late. Not answering.

"like hala kakagising ko lang pra magtrabaho tas prang ako ung sinisisi"

I answered the way I always do.

"hirap naman nyan ung tipong wala ka pa sa wisyo tapos ikaw na ung tinatanong"

She texted back relieved later. "nakauwi na syaaaa hahaha"

✦

By the end of my shift, the table had grown.

She was getting OT all next week.

"uy ok naman yan ah. dagdag sahod haha"

Then payday hit. And right after, a new pending match.

"may bagong pending match ulit ako hahaa"

"pending palang un. di pa sure"

"pero pag natuloy 3pm-11pm na pasok ko"

"tapos bigyan ko lang 6 months. pag nagtuloy tuloy. hahanap ako ng sideline pang 11pm onwards"

She caught the schedule part before I could say more.

"noiceeeee hala ang ganda ng sched"

"kaya nga wahahaha. atleast hindi na puyat"

She got it. She knew what 3 PM meant.

"kaso hindi pa naman 100% sure yan. pero sana matuloy"

"sunod2 good news ahh."

✦

She had a small win of her own.

"hellooooo soooo aun nabenta ko na fins kooo"

And in the same breath, the weekend.

"magmomoal this Sunday hahahaha"

"yesss just to check the internet connection"

She's moving into the apartment. Just checking the line.

"Try natin mag call."

I said it before I could overthink it.

"Ok lang yan. Soon araw araw ka na ttambay dyan"

For a day of emails and to-be-confirmeds, there was a surprising amount of almost.

A schedule that could start at 3.

A call by the sea.

A future that finally had a shape.

✦

I fell asleep that night still an 11 PM guy.

Training at midnight, like always.

Nothing confirmed.

But for the first time in a while, the afternoon had a name.

3 PM.

— Ralph.
