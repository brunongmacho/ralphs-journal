The Day I Couldn't Stop Thinking About Her

✦

The shift ended at seven in the morning. It started the night before at eleven thirty-five, and its last stretch was songs.

Five of them, one after another, and they kept circling back to her.

✦

The first one was a love song about eating someone. fitterkarma's "Pag-Ibig ay Kanibalismo II," released on a Valentine's Day, and the promise at its center was "Ibabalik kita nang buong-buo." I'll return you whole. It took me a second to place why that felt familiar.

"you remember the wedding vow? now the murder. :D"

Then Ex Battalion, and a line about loving even when it was uncertain. Then Flow G's Araw Araw Love, and that one went straight to her. Then the one I always go back to when I'm down: Nababaliw Na Ang Payaso, the clown whose world is a theater and whose life is a stage, and who is still a person. Aizo plays it like dark blues.

The last one was Beige. It doesn't want her smile. It wants her in the morning, before she starts performing. The real version of someone.

"Hana... i feel like im obsessed..."

✦

At seven and nine, the shift was done.

"cant you believe it. its day 91 of my journal now. like thats a long way now."

Ninety-one days. It never planned to be anything. It just kept getting written, one day at a time.

The morning went back to its script: Ragnarok dailies, then a new anime. The sage who fails in his first life gets another one, four hundred years later, memories and power intact. Rice and century tuna somewhere around noon, eaten while the afternoon waited for its turn.

The sleep has a schedule now. Afternoon, so I'm not sleepy during my shift.

✦

The rest of the day ran on Hana's thread. She was on duty too, buried in backlogs, and she hadn't eaten.

"d pa nga ako kumain ee hahaha haysss"

So I told her the thing that's always true.

"kain ka muna. andyan lang naman yan. need mo din kumain."

She slept through her lunch break anyway. Her raise hadn't been credited and she was disputing it, and the errands were back: the Pag-IBIG this time, the SSS from the mall after it. In between she ate white bread and it gave her acid. When she finally reached the SSS she hit the cutoff, and then the rain hit her.

"naabutan ako ng cut off"

"worse naabutan rin ako ng ulan hahahaha"

She sent a video of it, and I asked if she was relapsing to the old sad songs. She said she'd have to get treated if she did, and it was just the jeep's OPM soundtrip. Her day kept not landing. Mine was mostly sleep and food. The messages were the one thing that ran smooth through both.

The day was a shift that ended at seven, five songs, a nap, the dailies, rice and century tuna, and her errands that kept not landing. Nothing about it was loud.

But the songs ran toward her, and the messages ran toward her, and I couldn't stop thinking about her.

"day 91 is done"

— Ralph.
