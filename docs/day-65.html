The Day the Training Started Over

✦

After the shift, I got matched again.

New founder.

But I have to redo all the training.

I just finished the whole thing, and now I'm doing it again for someone else.

The last one was just a pending match.

"Ok may client na ulet ako. Pero sept ko pa makakausap. So ulet nanaman training."

I told her they fooled us with the training this week.

Uulitin nanaman lahat.

"Pero madali na un. Mamaya tapusin ko lahat tlga yan."

She sent a heart back.

▪

Then my dad messaged me out of nowhere.

Nangamusta.

Then the very next line: kailan ako magaasawa.

I told her and she laughed.

"Hala bogo hahahaha."

She asked why we're apart now.

I said nasa middle east erpat ko.

"Ohhhh I didn't know that. Nagwowork pa rin sya hanggang ngayon?"

Not even close kami.

She asked if I minded her asking why.

Broken fam.

Sa mom ako lumaki.

"Pero ano feeling mo sa tatay mo? D mo naman hate?"

Im past that.

Wala na. Haha.

Kung kinausap ako edi kakausapin ko.

Pag hindi nagparamdam edi wala na ulet.

"Ohhh okeokeee pero yeah we just get past those. Lalo na if tumatanda na tayo."

No room to get mad na eh.

Peace of mind na hinahanap talga.

▪

Then she told me about hers.

"Well if I may share mas dapat nga siguro broken kami ee hahaha. Kaysa nagsasama nga pero puro away naman nakikita namin."

I said ung samin nambabae siya tapos nananakit.

"Traumatized me for life I think hahaha kaya wala ako close talaga sa kanila."

"Maybe I shouldn't say it but may mga tao talagang d deserve maging parents ever."

Then, laughing:

"…including me hahahjaha. By choice thooo."

I said iba naman ung responsible ka sa ginawa nyo tapos d mo naman deserve.

She asked if I was still firm on being childfree.

Wala na din.

Di na talaga.

"Mas pinipilit nila ako mas lalong ayoko na eh hahahahah."

▪

She changed the subject.

"Maiba lang hahahha excited ako for you magkavideo ka dito sa fundive."

I said I'm happy she did freediving.

I think my stance on kids isn't the only reason we get along.

"Like when we learn that we are both freediver. Mas lalong naging interesting ung bagay bagay."

I told her to try a boat dive in Manila.

Sepoc island.

Sobrang clear ng tubig and white sand.

"Ohhh this niceeee. Prang nasa dunes lang."

Para kang lumilipad.

Tapos sa Samal island naman.

May 50m na butas.

Into the unknown.

"Prang ung sa the Blue Hole."

▪

Later we tried to plan a movie.

Comedy or horror or action.

She suggested mystery detective comedies.

Enola.

Knives Out.

I'd seen them all.

"Baka gusto mo goods lang."

I said I'd buy food and we could watch.

Namiss ko lang din.

But I fell asleep.

Uyyy kakagising ko lang.

We can watch later.

She replied:

"Yeahhh baka pass muna ko dami hahabulin ee huhu."

The movie kept slipping.

But the day didn't feel wasted.

— Ralph.
