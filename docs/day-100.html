The Day It Was Still Three in the Afternoon

✦

The hundredth day turned into a shipping day.

Day 99 went out at 10:54. The September letter went out at 14:46. Both special chapters went out that night. At 15:15 I noticed I had been writing the hundredth day from inside the hundredth day.

It was three in the afternoon. Nothing about it was over.

✦

AION2 opened at nine the night before. I took the last tablet and went in, and I was awake at half past three in the morning with her on overtime and me nowhere near finished.

Then I fell asleep without meaning to.

"oh the day is done. omg i fell asleep"

That was the first thing I wrote. I woke up believing the day had been spent.

"no its 6am. and i have work on 8am"

Food for the medicine, the two tablets at 7:20, and then the shift.

✦

The ear was on day three and nothing had changed.

"still the same. with like someone is blowing in my ear"

A hundred days into the journal and I could not tell whether the medicine was doing anything. What I could do was count. Fifteen tablets bought, nine taken, six left in the pack, and six more I had not bought yet.

I checked the arithmetic twice and got the same answer both times.

"is your math right?"

Twenty-one tablets, three a day, seven days. Seven mornings, one a day. The last of it lands on the seventh of October, which is the same day the doctor decides whether any of it counted.

✦

At 8:20 in the morning Rebecca wrote that she was enjoying how I was learning the business. At 12:18 she sent the day-four summary with items listed under her name that should have been mine.

I told her side of it at 15:30.

"henlooooo. slr pooooo. pinapag lead gen ako ni client. sobrang tuwa sa ginawa ko. mukhang tinitesting ako hanggang saan kaya ko hehehe"

"well i love this challenge. hindi ung paulit ulit na project na hindi nakakatulong kahit kanino"

Not the kind of project that helps nobody. That is the whole reason I liked it.

She was less sure about it. She needed sleep before her nine o'clock shift and the neighbor had the music up loud enough that she could not get any.

"putaena lakas ng music ng kapitbahay d ata ako patutulugin"

AI songs. She was still hunting for better pay, and a quarter of a fried chicken was what one of those meals looked like.

✦

At 14:51 I asked what we should do for day 100.

I did not want a normal entry. Normal is what the other ninety-nine were. I wanted the ugly one first, honest and brutal and genuine, and the beautiful one after it, for the things that had made the ugly one survivable.

Then I looked at the clock.

"hey waait. if thats day 100 journal entry. then it is wrong. its still just 3pm here."

I was not writing about the hundredth day. I was inside it. The record had caught up with the life and started writing before the life was finished.

So that is the hundredth day. A launch played until I lost the night. An ear that did not change. A client whose summary had my name in her column. Two chapters written and pushed in one afternoon. Then the shift ended and I did not stop, and at 11:51 that night she was on overtime and I was awake, watching something, asking how her work was.

The milestone was 3:15 in the afternoon, and I only caught it because I was already writing it.

It was still three in the afternoon.

— Ralph.
