The Day We Stayed

✦

I woke up at 8 AM still carrying last night.

Arguments have a way of following you into sleep.

When I opened my eyes, I wasn't wondering who was right anymore.

I was wondering if we'd still be here.

We were.

She apologized.

I apologized too.

Neither of us was really fighting each other.

We were fighting people who weren't even in the conversation.

Her past.

Mine.

Somewhere in the middle of all that, we forgot we were standing on the same side.

Then she said,

"You like me and I like you. Dun nalang muna."

For now...

that was enough.

✦

Brunch was simple.

Tasty bread.

San Marino corned tuna.

Tipid mode.

Almost every peso I had went into this apartment, and somehow that didn't bother me.

The room already feels worth it.

✦

I finally found a karenderia nearby.

Four cups of rice.

One vegetable.

One meat.

Seventy-five pesos.

I almost laughed.

A few streets away, that meal would've cost me twice as much.

My kettle finally arrived too.

I made my first Energen here.

Sat back.

Watched Mr. D.

Left Ragnarok grinding in the background.

Nothing extraordinary happened.

Just another day inside a place that's slowly beginning to feel like mine.

✦

At 8 PM, everyone gathered in Discord.

We watched The Colony.

For a while...

I forgot there had ever been an argument.

Sometimes normal conversations are enough to remind you that life keeps moving.

✦

Later that night...

everything became quiet again.

She told me things I wish nobody ever had to carry.

She talked about wanting to leave.

Not her house.

Everything.

About wondering what it would feel like not to wake up.

About hurting herself.

About believing she had become a burden.

I sat there reading every message.

Trying to find the perfect words.

There weren't any.

So I stopped looking for perfect.

I just stayed.

"We'll figure it out."

"I'm here."

"Kakampi mo po ako."

Maybe that's all anyone really needs sometimes.

Not solutions.

Presence.

✦

After everything settled, I kept thinking about something.

A few months ago, I thought love meant never arguing.

Now I think love might actually begin the moment two people choose not to leave after they do.

We weren't arguing about each other.

We were arguing with old versions of ourselves.

The scared versions.

The abandoned versions.

The ones still expecting history to repeat itself.

Tonight...

we stayed anyway.

✦

I don't know where this road ends.

Neither of us does.

But for the first time in a long while...

uncertainty doesn't scare me as much as silence does.

And tonight...

neither of us chose silence.

Maybe that's enough.

For today.

✦

— Ralph.
