Thursday, July 9, 2026

3 min read

The Day I Made Room

✦

Yesterday, I found myself on the other side.

Today, I realized what that actually means.

It doesn't mean the past disappears.

It means there's space now.

Space I didn't have before.

And today...

I started filling it.

✦

The morning began with nothing.

No disputes.

No emergencies.

No unexpected messages.

Just a quiet logout at 7 AM and the simple thought of sleep.

In a strange way, having nothing to react to still felt unfamiliar.

But I think I'm getting used to it.

✦

After work, I slept.

Only a few hours.

Just enough.

When I woke up, there were messages waiting.

That still feels new.

✦

Eana had greeted me good morning moments after waking up.

> "Tara kape."

I laughed and told her I was about to sleep.

She had already joined our Discord server the night before. She didn't make it a big deal. She just showed up.

I asked for her consent to put her in my journal. Just her name. Nothing more.

She said yes.

It was small.

But it meant something.

✦

Then Dewy messaged.

She was getting ready for her DFA appointment. Passport. Japan.

I told her to go early. Bring food.

She told me about her morning. About her sibling. About commuting to Manila. About wanting to visit Quiapo church after.

I let her share. She doesn't open up to just anyone. I felt that.

I liked that she trusted me with this.

Then she asked me about grammar. How adding an endearment changes the weight of a sentence.

> "Babe, hindi ka na nag update?"

We laughed. I noticed she thinks about the same random things I do. The kind of small, useless details most people wouldn't notice.

She told me we have the same keyboard. RK S98.

I smiled at that.

It's just a keyboard.

But it was another thing we had in common.

Then she told me about her ex. She paused the conversation to do it.

> "Unahin ko muna ikwento yung samin ni ex."

I listened. I asked questions. I let her tell it the way she needed to.

When she was done, I thanked her for trusting me.

I meant it. More than she probably knew.

✦

She told me to watch Off Campus.

I started it today.

Somewhere between the episodes, a song played.

Baby, Now That I've Found You.

For the first time in months, a love song didn't pull me backward.

It pointed forward.

It made me think of the people I'd met along the way.

New connections.

New conversations.

New reasons to look ahead.

I didn't know what to do with that feeling.

So I simply let it stay.

But I noticed.

And that felt like progress.

✦

I fell asleep around 4 PM.

I woke up at 7 PM, starving, with two episodes of the finale left and nothing in the house.

No food.

No plan.

Just an empty refrigerator and the quiet realization that my biggest problem right now was figuring out dinner.

That's not a complaint.

That's progress.

✦

Today's Weight: 7.5/10

Not because something extraordinary happened.

Because healing isn't just about surviving the past.

It's about making room.

For new conversations.

For new trust.

For new feelings, even if I don't understand them yet.

For ordinary days that don't need to be dramatic to matter.

Soundtrack of Day 16

- The Foundations — Baby, Now That I've Found You

✦

Today, I didn't try to move forward. I just made room. And somehow, that was enough.

— Ralph.
