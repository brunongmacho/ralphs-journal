The Day Two Worlds Shared My Desk

✦

3:31 AM.

Still awake.

I didn't fight it — I just let the night decide when I'd sleep.

Supernatural in the meantime.

Two options sat in front of me:

Sleep when it catches up.

Or wait for 6 AM, do the RO dailies, then sleep.

✦

There was no list tonight.

No PC to rebuild.

No client work.

No problem to solve.

Just Castiel, Sam, and Dean... and maybe some dailies at sunrise.

The night didn't have to mean anything.

It just had to pass.

✦

Slept at 7 AM.

Dailies first, obviously.

Woke up around noon.

First order of business, as always: food.

✦

By 11 PM, I was waiting again.

Bench or shift?

"Am I working tonight or not?"

A few weeks ago, that question would have bred forty-seven scenarios.

Tonight it got one answer:

I'll wait.

Might as well watch Cas in the meantime.

✦

Two monitors.

Dean fighting supernatural nonsense on one.

My character fighting monsters on the other.

Different worlds.

Same night.

And if the answer was benched?

Both screens were still lit.

— Ralph.
