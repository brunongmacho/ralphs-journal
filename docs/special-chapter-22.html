Named After a Giant Nut

ₚ·.°.☆•

After Jinro, I told myself I'd never love another dog.

I meant it.

Then came Coco.

✦

He was six months old when we got him.

From a friend.

And we wanted someone to come home to again.

So...

it was time.

✦

His name was Coco.

Because he was copper brown and white.

Like a coconut.

There's a song by Smokey Mountain.

"A coconut is a giant nut."

That became his nickname.

My giant nut.

✦

We used to joke that he never bit anyone.

But whenever he argued with us...

it sounded like he was cursing in Filipino.

"Gagogagogago!"

That was his answer.

To everything.

✦

He was an excellent rat catcher.

He'd proudly bring one to me...

then wait for his payment.

One rat.

One treat.

A businessman through and through.

✦

He slept beside me.

Clingy.

Just like Jinro.

Belly up.

Taking ninety percent of the bed as though he'd been paying rent.

Demanding belly rubs the moment I stopped.

✦

And he howled.

Not at sirens.

Not at other dogs.

Only at the opening of "Yeah!" by Usher.

Not even the singing.

Just the intro.

For some reason...

that song belonged to him.

✦

We spent three years together.

Long enough that he stopped feeling like a pet.

He simply became part of everyday life.

I trained him.

Commands.

Discipline.

Patience.

But somewhere along the way...

he trained me too.

✦

Then life changed.

The relationship ended.

Money became tight.

I had to move into my cousin's apartment.

No pets allowed.

There wasn't really a decision.

Only something I had to survive.

✦

The day they came to pick him up...

I handed him over.

Then I walked away faster than I wanted to.

Because if I'd looked back...

I don't think I could've done it.

✦

After they left...

the condo became unbearably quiet.

I barely ate for a week.

Everything reminded me of him.

His bowl.

His leash.

The empty space beside my bed.

Silence has a different sound when you know exactly who's missing.

✦

I'm tearing up writing this.

It's been more than a year.

And not every memory of him comes without guilt.

Sometimes I still wonder if I gave up too easily.

Sometimes I still wonder if I should've found another way.

I miss him.

More than I usually admit.

✦

People sometimes ask me which one hurt more.

Jinro.

Or Coco.

I don't know.

I never compared them.

Love isn't measured that way.

I loved them differently.

I miss them equally.

✦

Maybe that's why I haven't brought another dog into my life.

Not because I don't want one.

Because I know exactly what comes attached to loving them.

The joy.

The routines.

The quiet companionship.

And eventually...

the goodbye.

I'm not ready for another goodbye.

Not yet.

✦

I hope he remembers the good parts.

The belly rubs.

Sleeping across my bed.

Trading rats for treats.

Howling every time Usher started singing.

Being loved every single day.

✦

He traded rats for treats.

I traded a piece of my heart...

for three beautiful years.

Still the best trade I ever made.

✦

— Ralph.
