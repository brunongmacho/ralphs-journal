The Day My Voice Gave Out

✦

I slept through most of the day. Told her sorry, puro sleep ako, sama pakiramdam. She said it's okay, may meds naman? I said Iinom lang meds then ssleep. Then ggising after 8 hrs for meds ulet. She said she was heading home from Moalboal. Sent a photo of her fundive shots. In fairness ang ganda daw.

✦

Then 11 PM hit and I had to work. Benched again. Same loop, different week. May recordings pa. Sabi ko inuubo ako. They said Itry ko lang daw. She said "Baka gusto nila ng rockstar. Recording tas lagyan mo screamo." I told her bahala sila. Tatahol ako ng tatahol dun.

✦

She was watching her diving videos and laughing at her own finning. "Ampanget ng finning huhuhu." I said edi alam mo na ano aayusin mo next time. She asked how I was feeling. Still bad, I said. Ubo ng ubo. She said "huy pero mashare ko iba ung kaba ko dun sa dagat sa Moal. Buhay masyado nagpanic ako buong fundive." I asked what happened. She said it was just her, one instructor, and a cameraman. Andaming corals kahit 5 feet lang. Paslope daw ung wall so parang mahuhulog siya. But the breath hold got commended. Patagal daw nang patagal kada lusong. She said the sea floor felt like it was breathing. Like it had its own life.

I said wag ka kabahan. Dpt nga mas naeenjoy mo ung ganun. She said andami kasi pwede tingnan. I said balikan mo na ng balikan.

Then she sent a Jackass clip and said "d ka millennial pag d mo napanood kahit isang movie." I told her to watch all of them. Sobrang laftrip. She remembered the shopping cart scene with the fat guys on the slope. I said Steve-O was the reason I followed them.

I bought food but couldn't finish it. Wala akong panlasa. She said normal lang, rehydrate ka nalang. I said konting tiis nalang, out na, papahinga na ulit. She said "yeah you look like you need it." Out na po. Goodnight.

— Ralph.
