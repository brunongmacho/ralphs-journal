The Day My Voice Gave Out

✦

I slept through most of the day because I was still sick. Then 11 PM hit and I needed to work. Benched again — same loop, different week. The cruel part was the recordings. They needed my voice and my voice was wrecked. I told them I'd try tomorrow.

✦

She was back from Moalboal by then. Sent a photo of the room she'd picked — the fifteen-thousand one near Poblacion, sink outside, one ride to the sea. Her dad was still quiet about it. I asked about the duck dive and she said the instructor was editing the video but they commended her breath hold. Her finning needed work, she said, but she was getting the idea. The sea had been full of sardines and visibility was perfect.

✦

I was sick and stationary. She was out there moving through water. That was the whole day.

— Ralph.
