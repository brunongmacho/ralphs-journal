The Day I Kept the Night Moving

✦

I slept through the afternoon and woke up for real near eleven.

The day after I bounced had a shape to it. Everything that could move was already moving. The NTE reply wasn't, the job applications weren't. Nothing had answered yet.

I logged in anyway and sat with the two things I could control. Job hunting with RO open in another window.

I haven't eaten. But it's ok.

✦

The day had been full in between, even the parts I slept through.

It opened at midnight with food.

"ako eto job hunting. we can do voice if di ka masyadong busy ah. like movie trip hahaha"

"cgeeee. kain na din muna tayo. bumili ako bopis."

nagorder ulit akong thai food hahahaha

I had two servings' worth of opinion about portions by two in the morning.

"kaya nga dapat may kashare ka dyan. ;)"

Twelve midnight, Thai food, and a movie we'd get to eventually. The failure was a day behind us and we'd already replaced it with food plans.

✦

Her lunch came at four in the morning.

lunch naaaa

"eto hanap lang work. ikaw ba? sleep ka muna habang lunch"

kaso bigla sumakit tyan ko sooo

We were both on clocks nobody else shared. She was eating lunch at a time that was still bedtime for the world, and I kept telling her to sleep. She blamed the meal schedule, not the food. By five in the morning I was learning that the interviewers had gone AI on me.

"slr. AI na pala ung ibang interviewer ngaun hahahaa"

Then I clocked out around seven, and she handed me her workaround.

yesssss ung trick jan is icopy mo ung job description tas ichatgpt mo ung possible questions and ano ung keywords sa answers mo to pass screening

She was job-hunting for me too, one trick at a time.

✦

By late morning her move was paperwork.

SSS. MP2. Pag-IBIG. She walked through the mall-branch options like a checklist, and PhilHealth gave her the best laugh of the day.

"mag HMO ka nalang mas ok pa"

as in wala nako balak maghulog jan

so like pano pala ung inambag namin our whole working lives?

By the time the plans were made she was too dizzy to go, so she tried online first and slid the walk to Monday.

"iprio ko nalang sya sa Monday"

Two people, opposite corners again, getting their lives into some order.

✦

Then she brought out Torta.

eto katabi ko si Torta hahahaha

Tortoise-shelled, so the nickname. Not the baby she used to carry. That one had been gone for a while already.

wala na sya last month pa

d nya kinaya... premie baby kasi

I told her I still couldn't do pets. I'd already learned what happens when they leave.

"yes it brings joy. but. the heartbreak is permanent pag nawala na sila"

"mas masakit pa sa niloko ng jowa eh hahahahah"

ung namatay first cat namin iyaqq malala talaga

umabsent akong school

"kaya ayoko na muna ulit"

The goodbye is the built-in price. She'd paid it more times than she liked to count.

✦

Later she showed up with the end of the world.

We abt to enter Terminator type of shi hahahaha

A CNN interview about AI ending everything by 2030.

"Ganyan din sabi nila sa y2k bug diba. Hahahaha"

"Mas maniniwala pa ako na by 2030 madami na walang work. Since papalitan ng AI."

Haysss gusto ko nalang maging farmer hahahaha

retire ng maaga don't wanna deal with this shi

"Mangingisda nalang din."

"Spearfishing. Hahaha"

The future was loud. We answered it by dreaming smaller.

✦

In the evening she asked how the shift went, and the answer was the quietest part of the day.

"Yup. Wala tlga. Inaantay ko pa reply ng NTE"

The noise came back at night from both of us. Her housemates had no consideration. Someone knocked on my door at seven. We were both weighed down by the same surface, the kind that isn't yours yet.

"haysss gusto ko na umalis mga walang consideration mga tao dito"

"konting tiis nalang naman. saka last day of the shift na"

And then the better thread. Her net at the new place was working, so she wanted out even sooner.

"No naman comply pa rin ako sa rent date tas inaantay ko na lang ung power station"

as innnn huhuhu malapit na ang peace of mind hahaha

"go go kaya mo yannnnn :D"

We were both almost-anywhere. Her peace of mind. My next paper.

✦

The rest of the night didn't give me anything to do but wait.

I stayed logged in. RO dailies, then food, then more hunting. The paper I was waiting on was somewhere in a queue I couldn't see, and the applications were somewhere in theirs.

By the end of the night nothing had answered.

I wasn't worried.

So I just kept the night moving. Play the game, look for work, eat when the dailies are done.

— Ralph.
