Ralph's Journal — Special Chapter 23

6 min read

Every Song Is a Door

ₚ·.°.☆•

Some songs stay.

They don't ask permission.

They just play in your head...

until you have to sit down and listen.

✦

There was a time I was told...

you can't simply tell your brain to stop thinking about a song.

You try to push it out.

And it only plays louder.

The pink elephant problem.

Then someone told me something I still think about.

"The song isn't the wound.

It's the doorway."

✦

The first song was Porque.

Maldita.

It kept looping in my head.

"That's the one I want to tell her."

Every word of it.

The song said what I couldn't.

So I stood in that doorway for days.

Until I learned the trick.

You walk through it.

You don't have to live in it.

✦

I used to play guitar.

Before all of this.

I was the singer.

The guitar player.

Somewhere along the way...

I almost forgot.

There was a guitar collecting dust in my room.

Waiting in the corner.

✦

One day...

I picked it up again.

For the first time in a while.

It reminded me of her.

But for the first time...

it reminded me of myself too.

The guitar belongs to me.

It always did.

It was there before she arrived.

It stayed after she left.

I just needed someone to remind me of that.

✦

There was this piece I wanted to learn.

River Flows in You.

I saved the tutorial.

Not to intensify the sadness.

But because sometimes music says the things language can't.

✦

Then I wrote my own song.

We used to talk every day

Now your name just fades away

Funny how a stranger knows

More than me these days

I wrote the whole thing.

I'm not sure if it's good.

It didn't need to be.

It only needed to be honest.

✦

There was Something Just Like This.

Everyone hears it as a love song.

I heard it as a map.

"I want a life just like this."

Not superhero-level.

Python projects.

A stable job.

A guitar.

Coffee.

Friends in Discord.

You don't have to be Superman.

You just have to be Ralph.

✦

Then there was the song I kept replaying.

I'll Never Love Again.

A Star Is Born.

Lady Gaga singing her heart out.

I cried.

Not because of the movie.

Not entirely.

The movie just gave my heart permission to admit it.

Grief at full volume.

✦

Then one day...

Sunflower started playing.

Post Malone.

A song about staying with someone while life keeps pulling people in different directions.

A few weeks before...

I was replaying I'll Never Love Again.

Today...

Sunflower.

Maybe that's what acceptance sounds like.

✦

There was an afternoon when the music started.

One song after another.

Drops of Jupiter.

The song that will always belong to my mother.

No matter how many years pass...

it still finds her.

Then Marilag.

A reminder that somewhere out there...

love still exists.

You're Still the One.

The song I still imagine hearing on my wedding day.

And Stand By Me.

The song I imagine dancing to with my future wife.

Simple.

Quiet.

Meaningful.

Like the wedding scene from Crazy Rich Asians.

Then Waltz of Four Left Feet.

A song that once meant longing.

Today...

it simply reminded me how far I've come.

And finally...

Beige.

The song that somehow became my daily companion.

A month ago...

these songs would've broken me.

Today...

they simply made me smile.

✦

There's a rapper I listen to.

Hev Abi.

His songs sound like someone thinking out loud.

Not devastated.

Not ecstatic.

Just existing.

That's what some afternoons feel like.

✦

And then there's the funniest song of all.

Yeah! by Usher.

Not the vocals.

The intro.

Coco would howl along to the intro.

Every time.

An instrumentalist husky.

Some songs aren't about love or grief at all.

Some songs are just...

a dog who loved the intro.

Sometimes I still play the intro and wait.

Just for a second.

✦

Then the songs started finding people again.

There was a time I had no one to share my playlists with.

Then I met someone.

And finally...

I had someone to share the songs I listen to.

"Sa wakas... may nassharean na ako."

Finally, I have someone to share my songs with.

I gave her Your Universe.

She gave me If I'm James Dean, You're Audrey Hepburn.

Somewhere between those songs, playlists stopped feeling personal.

They became conversations.

I realized songs become different once someone else knows why you love them.

I sang her the song she used to tease me with.

Gusto Ko Sakin Ka Lang.

She was playing it in the voice channel one night.

I just sang along with it.

We weren't in love yet.

The song was already between us...

before we were.

Her songs became mine.

Mine became hers.

✦

Then the chats were gone.

The songs...

the conversations...

all of it.

Gone.

One night, I played my favorite song.

Short Stories With Tragic Endings.

From Autumn to Ashes.

My favorite.

The song I run to at my lowest.

A new voice told me...

"Only listen to this one time.

Then change the song."

The same words I'd been given months before.

Maybe that's what new voices are for.

To remind you of what you already know.

✦

She played me her angry songs.

Congratulations, I Hate You.

Apology.

Her favorite band.

What she plays when she's mad.

I gave her NoThingz.

I Hope Your New Favorite Song Sucks.

Then something stupid.

The Ordertaker.

To make me laugh.

And then the relapse songs.

MICO.

Senses.

HOMESICK.

"Want to hear the relapse song of an 11-year-old?"

I laughed.

Because that's what sharing music is.

You hand someone a piece of your playlist...

and it's like handing them a piece of your heart.

✦

And someone asked me to sing again.

"Just sing it again.

Now."

So I did.

✦

And then there's A Thousand Years.

I picked up the guitar again.

Rusty.

Forgetting the lyrics.

First, something simple.

"I Like You So Much, You'll Know It."

The kind of song you play because it feels familiar.

Then...

A Thousand Years.

That's when everything stopped.

The chorus caught me.

My eyes filled with tears.

I could barely get the words out.

The tears weren't for someone.

They were from somewhere.

✦

I understand the doorway now.

Songs were never prisons.

They were rooms.

And you can't live in a room forever.

But you can keep the door open.

You walk in.

You feel it.

You walk out the other side...

with the guitar in your hands.

✦

When you write...

the feelings leave your head and become words.

When you play guitar and sing...

those same feelings leave your chest...

and become music.

That's why I'll never stop playing.

That's why the songs stay.

They're not the wound.

They're the way home.

Maybe that's why I still listen.

Not to remember who I lost.

But to remember who I became...

while the music was playing.

✦

— Ralph.
