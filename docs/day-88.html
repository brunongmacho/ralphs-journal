The Day I Didn't Go

✦

The plan got cancelled. That's the whole story, if you tell it by the plan.

So I'll tell it the other way.

The day started early, in the dark. Lunch at 3 in the morning, on work, all my submissions done. Nothing left to do.

By 5, the plan was set. Cavite, Jed's birthday, a ride with Jalo.

It was cancelled at 2:47 in the afternoon.

After that, the day had to make itself up as it went.

And it did. Quietly.

✦

I woke around noon, waiting for Jalo to pick me up.

The wait had food in it. Ten pieces of spanish bread. A series playing in twenty-five minute episodes. Two monitors and nowhere to be. Jalo still offline. Hana still asleep after her last shift.

"oh i also bought spanish bread, 10 pcs"

This is what I keep. The waiting wasn't empty. It was full of small things, and they were enough.

✦

Somewhere in the waiting, the conversation went somewhere real.

"im more of a barney guy. without the womanizer"

It came around to June. I broke the bro code with my bestfriend way back then. He swiped first in the dating app, then we became partners, and I never told him. There was no courting phase. It just happened in an instant, and we only told him a week later.

They're with each other now. I stopped talking to them. It's too late for a conversation, and I don't want to meddle in something that might become a problem.

"yeah i build this from june upto now. so i wont do anything to destroy it. also i have hana. even if we are not together. im happy on what we are right now."

October moved too.

"october meetup wont happen because her budget going here will go to her apartment rent on moalboal. but its ok. i might just go there by december."

The meetup became December. Her apartment in Moalboal comes first. And that's okay.

✦

At 2:47 in the afternoon, the plan got cancelled.

"so jalo message me on fb. and the plan got cancelled. his atritis is acting up and his feet is really namamaga"

It was okay. Master Cutter was still playing, and I had the whole weekend ahead. I'd buy food later and finish the show through the weekend.

Hana woke up by evening. We'd video call later when she gets to the night market; she'll show me the food there.

At 5:44 I ate. Bicol express and rice. Mine has extra coconut milk, cooked through, oily from the coconut itself, a bit salty. A bowl of rice isn't enough for it. It's my favorite thing to cook.

"but what i am looking forward is when hana and i meet, she promise to cook for me. and also i'll cook my famous bicol express to her"

✦

By 7:33, the series brought back Coco. The day I gave him away, and how I couldn't look at him while they left. It still makes me sad.

"2 years. and he is always sleeping beside me. i miss him :("

✦

The plan left at 5 in the morning and was cancelled at 2:47 in the afternoon.

I didn't go to Cavite.

But the day was spanish bread and a conversation from June. Bicol express and a video call waiting at a night market. A promise about December.

None of it was the plan.

But it was the day.

— Ralph.
