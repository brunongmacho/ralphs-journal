The Day Life Started Giving Back

✦

A month ago, everything left.

My ex.

My last client.

Some friends.

My best friend.

My phone.

It felt like life was taking things away one by one.

And the worst part wasn't the losses themselves.

It was not knowing when the taking would stop.

Then somewhere along the way, I just kept going.

Fixed what needed fixing.

Survived the bad days.

Kept working.

Kept writing.

Kept showing up.

And today...

life started giving back.

✦

The morning began quietly.

No disputes. No emergencies. Just a shift ending at 7 AM and the simple feeling of another workday done.

I stayed up a bit, scrolling through a course I'd been meaning to finish.

Nothing special.

But it felt good to be learning again.

✦

Then Dewy messaged.

"Hello morning kuya."

Her last day at work. She was excited about it, even if the day itself was chaotic — training to conduct, applicants to handle, memos to deliver.

I teased her about the heat of her mood.

"Gusto mo Coco? Para lumamig ulo mo?"

She laughed and said no.

Then she told me about her Coco order anyway.

White Pearl Milk Tea Large. 50% sugar. Less ice. Salty Cream.

She said it proudly.

I smiled at that.

People don't usually share their specific drink orders unless they're comfortable with you.

✦

Then it happened.

I checked my email.

There it was.

"You've been matched."

I stared at it for a moment.

Months of applications. Certifications. Portfolios. Waiting.

And then, just like that, an email.

I didn't scream. I didn't cry.

But I smiled.

A real one.

I told Dewy immediately. She'd been rooting for me through the applications.

I just wanted someone to share it with.

And she was the first person I thought of.

✦

I slept. Woke up at 4 PM to the sound of rain.

Cold. Heavy. The kind of rain that makes you want to stay under a blanket and forget the world exists.

But not because you're hiding from it.

Because it feels good.

I took a bath, joined a Discord call with friends, and just sat there listening to them talk while the rain filled the afternoon outside.

Nothing extraordinary.

But that was the point.

A month ago, a rainy afternoon would have felt lonely.

Tonight, it felt like peace.

✦

The songs changed.

This morning, I was listening to I Like You So Much, You'll Know It and Ikaw Kase.

By evening, I was listening to Driftstar. My Thang. Lean Back.

The same day.

Completely different moods.

Not sadness.

Not longing.

Motion.

I noticed that.

The songs didn't change.

I did.

✦

Then the conversation turned to food.

She recommended Ramen Kuroda.

I told her I'm more of a Kuroda guy — curry rice meal, extra noodles.

She laughed and said she recently started exploring food because of someone.

"Kaya okay rin mabroken."

I understood that more than she probably knew.

And then I said it.

"Kung gusto mo pag nag SM Manila ka ulit sa passport mo. Samahan kita para kumaen. If ok sayo."

She said yes, but it's not set in stone.

She still has to fix her PSA. Then the National ID. Then Philpost.

"We'll see," she said.

I told her no rush. My schedule is flexible.

I don't know if it'll happen.

But I meant it.

That's just what you do for someone you care about.

✦

At 11 PM, I logged into my first onboarding session for a new client.

It was also the last day of my work week.

A month ago, I would have been terrified.

Tonight, I felt excited.

Life wasn't asking me to survive anymore.

It was asking me to begin.

✦

Today's Weight: 8/10

Not because everything is perfect.

Because a month ago, I was surviving.

Today, I was living.

There's a difference.

And I think I finally understand it.

Life doesn't always give back what it took.

Sometimes it gives back something better.

Possibility.

Hope.

Confidence.

And the quiet realization that you're going to be okay.

✦

Maybe that's what healing actually looks like.

Not forgetting the past.

Not pretending it didn't hurt.

Just...

waking up one day and realizing life doesn't feel heavy anymore.

And that's not a small thing.

That's everything.

— Ralph.
