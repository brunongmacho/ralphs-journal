The Day I Didn't Mind Waiting

✦

I slept at 7 AM.

Woke up at 5 PM.

The day just bent around me.

✦

Open the PC.

Play RO.

My Discord friends were online, so it was never really alone.

Bought dinner in between.

Then came the shift — except tonight nothing was waiting on it. So I played. I watched a few episodes of Supernatural in the quiet parts.

There's usually a point in the night where boredom turns into overthinking.

Tonight it just stayed comfortable.

✦

Nothing to do used to sound like a threat.

Like the moment I stopped moving, everything would catch up with me.

Now "nothing" is just a window I can spend however I want.

✦

I worked on the journal site today.

Not big things — the writing was done.

Just the small finishes: a button so someone can copy a link to a single entry.

A skip-to-content link for readers.

The cover image set to load itself in the dark.

One quiet house-keeping line in the head of every page.

Then I sent it up.

Finished.

Quietly. The same way the book has been settling all along.

✦

RO.

Supernatural.

Teammates.

Dinner.

A shift with nothing to do.

A few months ago, "nothing happened today" would have sat on me like cement.

Now it's just a day.

I'm not impressed by it.

I'm not bored by it.

I'm just awake inside it.

Sitting with it.

That's not a small thing — to survive the quiet and finally let it be quiet.

— Ralph.
