The Day the Answer Was Monday

✦

I slept around ten and woke up at two in the morning. The ringing was still there. Ten hours to the appointment.

The appointment never happened. That morning the phone gave nothing, the mall was too far, and by nine I had already moved it to Monday myself.

The day underneath was a Sunday. The last one before the hours move to eight in the morning.

✦

I couldn't sleep. So I opened the job hunt file.

Twenty-six applications. Three rejections, and the rest had gone quiet. I went through the whole file and closed it down.

"all the entries there. i think wont be contacting me anymore. no one did"

I closed them all so the next hunt starts clean.

At four in the morning she woke up, her sleep had gone long. I asked if she'd eaten. She asked about the ear: no pain, nothing coming out, the ring the same, just no hearing on that side.

She told me she got ambushed by dogs on her way home, and that dogs are a phobia of hers. I told her about Coco, who doesn't know how to bark but curses. She stays team cat. I said I can be flexible.

The old list ended quiet that night. The ear kept me up, but her messages filled the hours.

✦

The morning went back to job hunting, then to a movie I finished with the Discord friends, then to food. At 9:23 I called the clinic.

Nobody answered. The appointment still had no confirmation, and a trip to the mall that bought nothing cost money I didn't have right now. I wasn't going to spend it unless I knew an ENT was there. So at nine I made the decision myself:

"then i think monday it is. since PCGH is just walking distance from here. after my launch day meeting. i will go there."

Monday. The hospital behind the house, ENT duty eight to five. The launch day meeting ends at nine, then the walk over, then home to work. And nothing was getting worse: no pain, the ring no louder, just the same silence on that side since yesterday.

Then the worrying came anyway. Earwax pushed to the eardrum? Something inside that needs taking out? Everything can be cured, right?

"ok i'll stop worrying for now. i finished eating too."

✦

The hunt was done too by then. The newest post was ten days old. Tomorrow I start cold again. The sleep caught up, and I woke again at 11:56, not sure when I'd fallen.

Then at noon, the SMS came:

"wow and the medicard replied on my sms. next ENT is thursday 10am"

Thursday, ten in the morning. A real slot, a confirmed one. It was also already the backup. I had picked Monday at nine.

I told her the news. She was out on her random lakad, ukay-ukay, and a fried siomai and rice from Manila Foodshoppe that was supposed to be good for one and still wasn't. Her shopping money is for Bali, but Bali moved, so it's for Moalboal now. She found a black dress, then fell for a pink one. I kept giving her somewhere to wear them: walking on the beach, going on a date. "dami dami eh" Then:

"meeting me <3"

"As innnnn…. where are u when I need you the most?!!! charr"

"nasa maling fersons pa ngaaaa hahahahahhaha"

By evening she was making sure I ate right:

"lagyan mong gulay haaa need mo yan"

Still healing, she said.

The day was a ringing ear, a job board that went quiet, a phone that gave nothing, and her all the way through.

The phone did answer at noon. Thursday, ten in the morning. It only confirmed what I'd decided at nine.

The answer was Monday.

So I slept early. Tomorrow work starts at eight.

— Ralph.
