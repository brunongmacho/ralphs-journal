The Day the World Felt Bigger Again

✦

Some days become memorable because everything happens.

Others...

because you quietly realize the world has been moving around you the whole time.

Today wasn't about finding love.

It was about finding people again.

It was simply about remembering that there are millions of lives happening alongside mine...

and maybe saying hello to a few of them.

✦

2:00 AM — I was still awake.

Not because I couldn't sleep.

Because I didn't want to yet.

My friends were on Discord playing Valorant. I don't play FPS games for very long, they make me dizzy. So I became the spectator. The commentator. The one laughing while everyone else was trying to win.

Eventually we switched games and played Meccha Chameleon together.

Nothing life-changing.

Just an ordinary late night spent with people whose company I've slowly learned not to take for granted.

Around 5 AM, my body finally gave in.

I slept.

✦

11:28 AM — I woke up.

No alarms.

No urgency.

No work waiting for me.

Just another Sunday.

I stayed in bed for a little while before finally convincing myself to get up.

Fixed the sheets.

Washed my face.

Pressed play on Yoke Lore – Beige.

Then walked into the kitchen.

Pancit Canton sounded like the right decision.

✦

After eating, I went right back to The Sarah Connor Chronicles.

Episode after episode.

Nothing dramatic happened outside my screen.

The only mission I had today was finishing another episode.

Maybe another one after that.

I wasn't trying to escape reality.

I was simply enjoying having nowhere else to be.

✦

Around three in the afternoon...

I realized I hadn't done anything "productive."

No studying.

No writing.

No work.

No errands.

I was just...

living.

Later, I reheated the Pancit Bato I'd ordered earlier and somehow decided it deserved rice too.

Peak Filipino logic.

Carbs with carbs.

No regrets.

✦

Earlier today...

I downloaded Boo.

Not because I was searching for my next relationship.

Not because I felt lonely.

Actually...

I told every person I matched with exactly the same thing.

"I'm not looking for anything right now. Just friendship and a good conversation."

Some conversations ended almost immediately.

Some never even started.

Strangely...

it didn't bother me.

A few months ago, I might have wondered if I'd said something wrong.

Today...

I simply wished them well and moved on.

Other conversations surprised me.

Movies.

Food.

Work.

Random stories.

Nothing extraordinary.

Just people being people.

And somehow...

that felt enough.

Maybe I wasn't looking for connection anymore.

Maybe I was simply enjoying conversation.

✦

I think that's what changed today.

For a long time, my world had become very small.

It revolved around memories.

Around one story.

Around one chapter I kept rereading.

Today...

the world quietly expanded again.

Not because I found someone.

Because I remembered there are still countless people I'll never meet...

and some I probably will.

Not every conversation needs to become forever.

Sometimes five minutes is enough.

Sometimes a stranger simply reminds you that the world is far bigger than your own memories.

✦

Around ten that night, I told myself I'd take a quick nap before writing.

The next thing I knew...

it was Monday morning.

Seven o'clock.

Apparently my "nap" lasted nine hours.

Maybe my body knew something my mind didn't.

Maybe I needed the rest more than I needed another episode.

Either way...

I laughed.

Only I could accidentally sleep my way into tomorrow.

✦

Today's Weight: 9.5/10

Nothing remarkable happened.

And maybe that's exactly why today mattered.

I smiled.

I ate well.

I laughed with friends.

I talked to strangers.

I watched a series.

Then I slept so deeply that Sunday quietly became Monday without asking permission.

Soundtrack of Day 12

- Yoke Lore — Beige

- Pancit Canton boiling in the kitchen

- Friends yelling over Discord

- The quiet sound of conversations beginning...

- ...and others ending without needing an explanation

✦

Not every open door needs to be walked through.

Sometimes it's enough to know they're there.

Today...

the world felt bigger again.

And for the first time in a long while...

I wasn't afraid to meet it.

— Ralph.
