The Day I Only Had to Check

✦

Last day of August.

Nothing much again — just sleep.

It's a holiday here.

The only thing on my plate was logging in from 10:30 to 11.

If nothing comes in, I have no work.

✦

So I did the math of the day the way I've been learning to.

Sleep.

Eat.

Holiday.

Log in.

Check if anything needs me.

And if it doesn't...

go back to my life.

✦

It's funny, really.

I started August sitting on the floor with a hundred and sixty pesos, doing arithmetic until payday.

I'm ending it with a thirty-minute check-in I might not even need.

The month went out the way I learned to live it.

Quietly.

✦

Some months end loud.

This one ended with me sleeping, eating, and noticing that almost nothing was required of me.

That's not a small thing.

For most of this year, almost everything was required of me.

Tonight I checked, and then I went back to my life.

That's where the whole month was pointing.

— Ralph.
