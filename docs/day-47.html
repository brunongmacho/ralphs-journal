The Day the List Got Shorter

✦

Woke up at 12:38 PM.

Slept around 6 AM.

Stood in front of a freshly rebuilt PC, trying to remember what I was supposed to put back on it.

✦

There's a phase after a full rebuild that nobody warns you about.

Not the fixing.

The remembering.

The programs I used twice a month.

The ones I used once.

They came back randomly, like loose change.

"Oh. That one."

Then another one would surface hours later.

✦

Went out for brunch.

It felt good to be away from the screen for a bit, after spending yesterday watching backup bars crawl.

Came back.

The machine was still working.

✦

By 11 PM, I hadn't done anything else.

Just RO.

All evening.

Not grinding toward anything.

Just playing.

✦

There are two ways to spend a day like this.

Wondering if it counts.

Or just letting it be.

I let it be.

Yesterday I rebuilt the machine.

Today I let it run.

Both turned out to be the same kind of day.

A list.

One that shrinks a little more every day.

— Ralph.
