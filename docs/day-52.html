The Day a Horror Movie Felt Like Company

✦

Kadayawan.

A long weekend.

The kind of day that asks nothing of you.

And I gave it nothing back.

✦

Slept.

Ate.

Played Ragnarok Online.

Repeat.

That was the whole plan, and I kept it.

I didn't need the holiday to be productive.

I didn't need it to be another milestone.

Some days are meant to just pass through you.

✦

The day stayed quiet until 11 PM.

She messaged.

A call, on Discord.

The first call, I had to ask for.

This one just happened.

It's strange how quickly something becomes part of the night.

✦

Then the movie.

Hereditary.

From Hall & Oates to that — I still laugh at the jump.

A horror movie, on a call, past midnight.

The scares were loud.

The night was easy.

✦

I kept thinking about that.

A day that needed nothing ended with a horror movie that didn't feel like one.

Because the movie was the excuse.

The company was the point.

✦

A long weekend spent doing nothing.

And it turned out to be exactly what I needed.

Not because anything happened.

Because someone was there to share the quiet with me.

— Ralph.
