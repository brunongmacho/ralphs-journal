The Day I Had Nowhere to Be

✦

I logged out at 7 AM and went to sleep.

That was the first thing that happened on Day 22.

Not a plan. Not a realization. Just sleep.

✦

I woke up around 10, stayed in bed doom scrolling until noon. Then I went out to eat.

Small things.

But they filled the morning.

✦

Eana called.

We talked the way we always do. Comfortable. Easy. No pressure.

Somewhere in the middle of the call, one of my guitar strings snapped.

I laughed.

I'd been meaning to bring the guitar in for repairs for a while. The strings have been too high for months. I just never had a strong enough reason to stop postponing it. The snapped string was the nudge.

I messaged the shop on Facebook and told them I'd bring it in.

✦

Then there was Hana.

We talked the way we've been talking these past few days.

About freediving.

About tattoos.

About travel.

About why neither of us wants kids.

About the guilt of wanting to move out even when you love your family.

About the islands she's been dreaming of.

About coffee.

About horror movies.

About music.

About our families.

The conversation didn't feel like the beginning of anything.

It felt like catching up with someone I'd already known.

✦

I showed her the special chapter I'd been working on, the one I wrote about her.

She said: "I can totally say the same things."

And then: "Please know that I also notice you."

I shared the journal with her.

She said she'd read it slowly, one entry at a time.

✦

I spent the rest of the day doing nothing in particular.

Hana on Instagram. Eana on Discord. A guitar waiting for repair. A tattoo still being planned.

Conversations that didn't need to be profound to be worth having.

A month ago, I would have measured a day by how much I'd healed.

Today, I measured it by how present I felt.

✦

Today's Weight: 7/10

Not because something happened.

Because nothing needed to.

No realizations about healing.

No reflections on the past.

No dramatic breakthroughs.

Just a Wednesday where conversations kept finding me.

And for the first time in a long while, I realized I wasn't chasing anything anymore.

✦

A few months ago, I would've tried to figure out where every conversation was leading.

Today, I'm just grateful they're happening.

A voice call with a friend.

A guitar that finally asked for attention.

A stranger who felt familiar.

A shared journal.

A day that asked for nothing.

And for now...

that's enough.

— Ralph.
