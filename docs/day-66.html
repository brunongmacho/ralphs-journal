The Day I Made OC Do the Work

✦

Still have the cough.

Woke up at 6:30 PM from the alarm.

Did my RO dailies.

Then found out I'm not even a confirmed match.

Pending lang pala.

But the pay's the same, so it's fine.

Just training and training and training.

Same module.

For the nth time.

Hahaha.

▪

So I trained OC to do the modules for me.

The research.

The word tailoring.

Almost everything.

Since it's really repetitive.

Work smart, not harder.

Gulat sila tapos ko kagad.

I just said kanina ko pa ginagawa.

OC's basically my personal VA now.

Tapos ako na ang client. Hahaha.

▪

Hana asked about it.

"Huy alam moooo prang sinusuportahan talaga ako ng universe sa plan kong lumipat hahaha."

Her hourly went up.

"Inincrease ung hourly ko hahahhaa."

Sana all.

Next yr pa akin.

January.

Every 6 months +1k increase ako eh.

"Congrats sayoooo."

"Pero yah goal na dapat 200k per month hahaha."

▪

Then at some point the talk turned to sleeping.

"Pag matanda na 2 nlang taga pinagpipilian ee hahaha tulog or pera hahahaha."

I said samahan mo naman ng cuddle pag gabi.

Para masarap tulog.

Hindi sakit lang ng katawan iniinda.

"Pwede pero ung d sana humihilik hahahahhahaha."

I told her to cover my nose.

Malakas pa naman ako humilik pag pagod.

Deal breaker pala.

"Nako pano yan haahhaa."

"Isa rin yan bat ayoko ng live in hahahhaha ayoko ng may maingay na katabi matulog."

Bilhan kita earplug.

"Well. Alam mo ung mga ganyan. From the right partner. Yan pa ung hahanap hanapin mo."

▪

Then carbon fins.

Her dream daw.

"Pero dream ko talaga yang carbon fins someday."

I told her to buy the cheapest.

Then design her own protective film.

Edi 1/1 lang meron.

"Dream ko yan magkacarbon fins."

"Meron dito sa Oceantag sinusukat nila mismo ung paa mo."

Mahal lang daw.

Mahirap maging pangmayaman.

▪

We kept trying to plan a movie.

I downloaded one for her.

Then she had a pahabol meeting.

Demo ng new CRM nila.

9am daw maybe, if gising pa ako.

I said sabihan mo din ako kung ssleep ka ah.

But she fell asleep.

"Uhy sorry huhuhu naidlip pala akooo huhuhu."

"Sorry if maybe tentative pako sa movie bawi akong weekend."

Weekend nalang.

Holiday din kami ng Monday.

So I can spend time with you if di ka naman busy.

"Oh okeoke gooo pero as usual work pa rin ako nyan haa hahaha."

Then, the last thing before I rested:

"Chat ka pag namiss mo ko."

She answered with a laughing face.

▪

Last shift of the week tonight.

Then Monday is a holiday.

Long weekend again.

Still coughing.

But the week's almost over.

— Ralph.
