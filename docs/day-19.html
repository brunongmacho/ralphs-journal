The Day Conversations Found Me

✦

I woke up to messages.

That used to mean something different. It used to mean checking, hoping, wondering if a specific person had replied.

Today, it just meant people.

✦

Hana messaged me first.

We met on Boo a while back. Just friends. She's from Cebu. A freediver like me.

I wasn't expecting anything when I saw her name. We talked for hours.

About freediving.

About relationships.

About the strange things people carry after they've been hurt.

Movies.

Music.

Cats.

Tattoos.

The conversation wandered everywhere and nowhere at the same time.

None of it was romantic. It was two people discovering they'd been through similar things and come out the other side still willing to talk about it.

It was just nice.

The kind of nice where you look up and realize hours have disappeared.

✦

Then Eana.

She had woken up in the middle of the night, tried to go back to sleep. I messaged her good morning.

"Sakit katawan ko."

"Kulang ka lang sa stretching."

We talked about the rain. About coffee. About TikTok. She's not active on it anymore. I followed her anyway, just so I could see if she ever shared something.

Small things.

But they filled the morning.

✦

At some point, I changed my Discord note.

"The Sunset Is Beautiful, Isn't It?"

I didn't overthink it. I just liked how it sounded.

Most people attach a meaning to it—I love you, but I'm letting you go. But that's not why I chose it.

I chose it because I'm looking at the sunset again.

Not the person who left.

Not the heartbreak.

Just the sunset.

Just life.

✦

I spent the rest of the day talking.

Hana on Instagram about her diving. Eana in Discord about nothing in particular. Conversations that didn't need to be profound to be worth having.

A month ago, I would have measured a day by how much I'd healed.

Today, I measured it by how many times I smiled before 2 PM.

✦

Today's Weight: 7.5/10

Not because I figured something out.

Because I didn't need to.

No realizations about healing.

No reflections on the past.

No dramatic breakthroughs.

Just a Sunday where conversations kept finding me.

And for the first time in a long while, I realized I didn't have to chase them anymore.

✦

A few months ago, I would've tried to figure out where every conversation was leading.

Today, I'm just grateful they're happening.

A freediver from Cebu.

A friend in Discord.

A shared laugh.

Conversations that meant nothing.

And somehow became something worth remembering.

And for now...

that's enough.

— Ralph.
