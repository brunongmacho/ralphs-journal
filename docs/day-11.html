The Day Love Changed Its Name

✦

I think today deserves to be remembered.

Not because something extraordinary happened.

Because almost nothing did.

✦

I woke up much later than usual.

My shift had ended that morning, and after sleeping through half the day, I found myself wandering around the house looking for food before anything else.

No deep thoughts.

No heavy emotions.

Just hunger.

So I ordered Pancit Bato through Grab.

While waiting, Siangyoo's Mixtapes played quietly in the background.

It wasn't trying to heal me.

It was simply keeping me company.

Sometimes...

that's enough.

✦

Lunch arrived.

Then something unexpected happened.

Nothing.

I ate.

I watched Terminator: The Sarah Connor Chronicles.

Episode after episode.

When I got hungry again, I cooked fried rice and corned beef.

Then I sat back down...

and kept watching.

That was my Saturday.

No emotional rollercoaster.

No heartbreaking movie.

No journal breakthrough.

No tears.

Just a TV series...

good food...

and nowhere else I needed to be.

Somewhere around Season Two, I caught myself smiling.

Not because the show was funny.

Because I realized...

I hadn't checked my heart all day.

✦

A few days ago, I watched About Time.

One line refused to leave me.

Live life...

noticing.

Today...

I think I finally understood what that meant.

Noticing doesn't always mean sunsets.

Or life-changing conversations.

Sometimes it means noticing that fried rice tastes better when you're not distracted by grief.

Sometimes it means noticing that an entire afternoon disappeared because you were enjoying a TV show.

Sometimes it means realizing...

peace doesn't announce itself.

It quietly becomes your routine.

✦

Around eleven that night, while I was taking a shower, my phone vibrated.

When I checked it afterward...

I froze for a second.

It was my second ex.

The message only said two letters.

"RB."

Twelve years.

Out of nowhere.

I stared at the screen longer than I probably should have.

Not because I felt anything.

Because I didn't know what I was supposed to feel.

I wasn't happy.

I wasn't angry.

I wasn't hopeful.

I was simply...

curious.

So I replied.

"Uhm? Yes?"

✦

She asked for my help.

DNA.

At first, I honestly didn't understand what she meant.

Then she explained.

She wanted proof.

Or maybe...

she wanted peace.

She believed I could help.

For a moment, twelve years quietly stood between us.

I could've answered emotionally.

I could've become defensive.

I could've started digging through memories I worked so hard to put down.

Instead...

I found myself asking different questions.

"What happens after?"

"If the result says what you already believe, what changes?"

"If your husband doesn't know you're doing this... what happens when he does?"

The more we talked...

the less it became about me.

It became about a marriage.

A family.

A child.

Consequences.

I realized something.

Sometimes helping someone isn't saying yes.

Sometimes helping someone is asking them to slow down before they make a decision they can't undo.

✦

When the conversation ended, I wished them well.

I told her I hoped they fixed whatever had broken between them.

Then I put my phone down.

And I went back to my evening.

That sentence surprised me.

I went back to my evening.

A year ago...

a message from my past might have stolen my entire night.

Tonight...

it became one page in an otherwise peaceful Saturday.

Nothing more.

✦

Maybe that's what healing really looks like.

Not forgetting people.

Not pretending they never mattered.

Not becoming numb.

Healing is allowing the past to knock on your door...

without inviting it to move back in.

✦

Today's Weight: 10/10

Not because today changed my life.

Because today didn't ask me to survive it.

It simply asked me to live it.

And for the first time in a very long time...

living was enough.

Soundtrack of Day 11

- Siangyoo's Mixtapes

- The sound of a Grab notification

- Fried rice sizzling in a pan

- Sarah Connor saving the world

- Silence that no longer needed fixing

✦

A month ago...

I would've called today uneventful.

Today...

I call it peace.

Maybe love never leaves.

Maybe it simply keeps introducing itself under different names.

Today...

I finally knew its name.

Peace.

— Ralph.
