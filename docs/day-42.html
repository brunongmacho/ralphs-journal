The Day the Book Grew a Cover

✦

7 AM.

I logged out from work.

Slept like the day had finally finished asking things of me.

✦

I woke up around noon.

Not because something was wrong.

Just noon.

No rush.

No alarms.

No emergency waiting on the other side of waking up.

Brunch.

Nothing fancy.

Just enough to get through the afternoon.

✦

Then I opened my PC.

And I kept working on Ami's reels.

For most of the day.

Looking for the right visuals.

Trying to make everything feel like PURE.

It's slower than I expected.

Not because it's difficult.

Because I actually care about getting it right.

✦

Her email came in the morning.

A recap of the call.

Reels.

Social posts.

A blog or newsletter draft.

"I'd like to see what you can put together independently."

That line stayed with me all day.

She's not looking for perfection.

She's looking at how I think.

That realization made the work feel heavier.

But in a good way.

It's a trust exercise.

I didn't see it at first.

But it's true.

✦

She asked again in the afternoon.

"Do you have anything for me yet?"

For a second, I wanted to explain everything.

Why it takes time.

Why the visuals need to feel right.

But I kept the reply simple.

Confident.

"I'd rather take a little more time to make sure the first pieces are strong than send something rushed."

That's the line I sent.

And I meant it.

✦

A few weeks ago, my days were measured in songs I couldn't listen to.

Now they're measured in deliverables.

Reels.

Captions.

A voice and tone document.

It's strange.

The same hands that wrote "Save me." are now trying to write like someone else.

Not because I'm hiding.

Because someone is trusting me with their brand.

And I want to honor that.

✦

By evening, the reels were done for the day.

But the night had one more thing waiting.

The book cover.

We'd been talking about it for days.

The title in cursive, sitting above the storm clouds.

Just Enjoying Ordinary Days.

Where ordinary days slowly became enough.

The window sill.

The coffee mug.

The headphones.

The fountain pen.

The rain against the window.

The city at dusk.

✦

And the open journal.

On the cover, there's an open grid journal.

Blurred handwriting.

Except for one line.

Crisp.

Readable.

"Day 1. — Save me."

An easter egg.

Hidden in the open journal.

You only see it if you look closely.

✦

I put the cover on the website tonight.

Made it clickable.

Made it zoom.

Because if someone was curious enough to look closer...

I wanted them to find where all of this began.

Because I wanted people to see it.

All of it.

Even the details that are easy to miss.

Especially the details that are easy to miss.

✦

There's a line on the site now.

"From all these days..."

"a book grew."

I wrote that.

For me.

Because it's true.

The journal that was supposed to be somewhere to put the weight...

turned into something with a cover.

A title.

An easter egg.

A book.

✦

Today wasn't really about healing.

It was about creating.

Reels for Ami.

A cover for the book.

And somehow...

I enjoyed both.

✦

Normal life.

That's what this is becoming.

Waking up late.

Buying brunch.

Working.

Making things.

For myself.

For someone else.

It took forty-two days to get here.

Forty-two ordinary days.

The kind I used to think didn't matter.

Funny.

They're the ones that built the book.

✦

— Ralph.
