The Ones Who Loved Me Without Asking Why

ₚ·.°.☆•

Some loves never ask you to explain yourself.

They don't care whether today was productive.

They don't care how much money you make.

They don't care if your heart is heavy.

They only care that you came home.

Jinro and Coco loved me like that.

✦

They never knew my dreams.

They never knew my failures.

They didn't know whether I'd had a good day at work.

Or whether I'd spent the afternoon overthinking something that no longer mattered.

To them...

I was simply Ralph.

And that was enough.

✦

There's something incredibly comforting about being loved by someone who expects nothing except your presence.

No performance.

No explanations.

No pretending.

Just...

"You're here."

Some days, I didn't realize how much I needed that.

✦

People often say that we rescue animals.

I've always wondered if they quietly rescue us too.

Not from danger.

From ourselves.

From the days we become too serious.

Too busy.

Too distracted to notice life happening around us.

They remind us to celebrate ordinary things.

A familiar face.

A shared afternoon.

Someone finally walking through the front door.

✦

Jinro and Coco taught me that love doesn't always arrive with words.

Sometimes it arrives with quiet company.

With small routines.

With moments so ordinary that you don't realize they're becoming memories until years later.

✦

The house is quieter now.

Jinro is gone.

Coco found another home.

Two different endings.

One I never wanted.

One I hoped would become a better beginning for him.

Love sometimes asks us to let go in different ways.

Neither one is easy.

✦

I still catch myself expecting them sometimes.

Not because I forgot.

Because love leaves habits behind.

For a second...

I still imagine hearing paws on the floor.

Or seeing someone waiting near the door.

Then the silence gently reminds me that time has moved forward.

✦

People sometimes ask whether pets are "just animals."

I don't think anyone who's truly loved one ever asks that question.

They become part of the rhythm of a home.

Part of your mornings.

Part of your evenings.

Part of your ordinary life.

And when they're no longer there...

the silence has a shape.

✦

Memory rarely keeps perfect records.

Instead...

it keeps feelings.

I remember coming home and feeling welcomed.

I remember never having to earn their affection.

I remember a love that never asked whether I deserved it.

Only whether I was there.

✦

Maybe that's what they left me.

Not only memories.

Perspective.

A reminder that love doesn't always need language.

Sometimes it's simply presence.

Sometimes it's waiting.

Sometimes it's choosing you every single day without asking for anything in return.

✦

Jinro.

Coco.

Thank you.

For every ordinary day you quietly made better.

For every welcome home.

For every lesson you never knew you were teaching.

The house may have grown quieter...

but my heart never forgot your footsteps.

You'll always be part of the home I carry inside me.

✦

— Ralph.
