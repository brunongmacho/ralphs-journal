The Day My Dream Held the Line

✦

Last shift before the long weekend.

Hana was wrapping up her own day.

"wala hahahaha ayoko ng meetings hahahaha."

"alipin tayo ng pera hahahaha."

I was eating Jollibee spaghetti.

I told her I always put a note for more cheese.

"Grabe sa family size hahahha d naman sya nakkaumay so far?"

"may once din naging addiction ko is spanish sardines pasta hahahhaa."

Then she was almost out.

"5 mins nalaaaang hahhhaha."

She said she had plans.

Meeting someone to buy fins.

Then the movies with her friend.

"Sinemalaya?"

"yasxzzzzz koreekttt."

I clocked out for the long weekend.

Out ko naaaaaa.

✦

It was raining hard here.

"Grabe na nga baha dito eh."

Well, not here.

She noticed it too.

"bakit andami ng baha jan esp Bulacan no? patient zero ng flood control projects hahaha."

"Wala naman magagawa eh."

So we laughed at it instead.

✦

I woke up at 8 PM.

Long weekend.

My Shopee package was waiting.

A diffuser.

Bamboo scent.

I needed it so the room wouldn't just smell like food.

The first time in a while, no urgent thing was waiting on me.

I could sleep till 8 PM and just go out for food.

✦

That night she got home from the movies.

"nakauwi na posxzzzz."

She asked how I was.

"Bahay ka na?"

"Ano ba to. We are reserving seats na? Hahahahahaha."

"Ha???? what seats?"

"hospital seats? hahahaha."

"Hell seats gagahahaha."

That was the whole joke.

And it was enough.

✦

Then, sometime before the day ended, I fell asleep and dreamed.

I was saving the world.

It was at my best friend's house.

He was my brainstorm guy before everything.

The past was there too.

She came close, like before.

And every time, I kept saying:

"Hey, there is your boyfriend."

I moved when she approached.

I told him it was all on me.

That no one had anything to do with it.

"I'm not there to steal the girl."

Then I woke up.

At first I didn't know why I dreamed that, because I'm okay now.

But I think I get the ending.

The old world showed up in my sleep.

And I didn't want it back.

I just remembered it.

— Ralph.
