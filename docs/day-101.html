The Day Nothing Went Wrong

✦

I said the day was done at 00:57, and at 08:00 I said done again while logging in to work.

Somewhere in between I forgot the last steroid.

"ohhh i forgot the last dose. its 1am"

I ate some bread and took it anyway. By the lunch dose at 13:18 the count was twelve of twenty-one.

✦

At 08:14 Rebecca wrote that things were sounding great and that she would send a Zoom link for a call at about eleven her time, nine mine.

The link never came and neither did the call. At 09:13 she wrote about the website instead, and I went and looked at the site before I answered her, on the grounds that if we were researching the SEO anyway we might as well finish the research.

The academy page has no H1 on it at all, and it is in the sitemap and not in the index. Everything else is already wired. The videos, the pixel, the tag.

"do the bottom line instead of suggestion it"

I have admin on that site. She told me not to edit anything yet because Elementor can put a whole website into a mess, so I wrote it down instead and asked for it as an SOP. By 10:07 there were six documents in the folder.

The call she offered me turned into the thing the call would have been about.

✦

At 10:40 I stopped and went looking for a way to press a key without touching the keyboard.

I had built a key presser for AION2 a while back and then lost it.

"im not using it to cheat. im using it to press the crafting button because im crafting thousands of material."

The first attempt did not survive being looked at. No backend. A driver that needed test mode and a reboot. A script that died on line eight before the window even opened. At 12:58 the executable was built and it launched.

Then it kept going. It pressed shift as well. F11 and F12 do nothing in a windowed game. It had to find the process on its own, press only while AION2 was the target, and stop the moment I alt-tabbed away. It worked, sort of, and then it broke again.

By 18:17 I was not fixing it. I was telling it what to do.

"oh and the alt+tab session should have a delay like 10-15 mins in random."

So it is supposed to leave the game and come back on its own, after a random wait. That part was still being built.

✦

At 13:27, in the middle of that, the payroll landed and the balance went to zero.

"ok so there is a problem now. its my payroll today. and UB ate my payroll...."

It is a loan. The auto-debit took everything instead of what I told it to take.

"Dapat 5k lang ung debit."

Five thousand is the amount I set. I set it that way so I would not forget, because I had set it that way before and it had never caused anything.

"Para d ko malimutan."

I called the landline at 13:43 and nobody picked up, so I sent an email. She read it while I was still writing the bug report.

"pero kampante ka naman na 5k lng tlaga dapat mabawas?"

That is what the statement says. The next one is the end of October.

✦

I logged out at 16:11. It was her last shift before the sea.

She was going to celebrate with jajangmyeon and there was none in stock, so she had guava ice cream instead.

"konting tiis nalang ahhaha"

We compared what we had watched. She has a film she has been waiting for. I had divers and sharks. By the time she lands she is going straight into the water and then sitting down with a coffee.

"Kaya nga ehhhh. This is it is it na ehhhh"

She picked up an overtime shift that night. I was awake anyway, and at 22:51 she asked why I had no shift and was still there.

So the day was a tablet taken at one in the morning, six documents in a folder, an executable that finally opened, a balance at zero, and a woman packing for the sea.

The five thousand was the amount I set. The tool was mine, and I lost it, and I built it back. The tablet I forgot and then took anyway.

Nothing went wrong.

— Ralph.
