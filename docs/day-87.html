The Day 500 Pesos Had to Last

✦

The shift ended at 7 in the morning.

"ok so its 7am here. my shift is finally done. its also payday. but im still waiting for my salary"

Payday, and the money hadn't landed yet.

I wasn't going to sleep anyway. Hana was still up, and I was starving. I went out and bought whatever was available.

At 3:22 in the morning, during my shift break, I bought pancit. Breakfast ended up tortang talong. The food of the day picked itself based on what was available.

Hana's night had been a rotational brownout. She came online the moment the power let her, then went looking for a coworking space with wifi and electricity. By three in the morning she found one.

I came back online around 7:48 and told her it was almost 8, to eat before she headed home, and asked if their power was back.

It was a payday morning and all I'd done was feed myself and talk to her. The money still hadn't moved.

✦

Then the movie offer.

She kept going back and forth between showering first or sleeping first. I asked if she couldn't sleep, did she want to watch a movie with me. She said sure, once she'd settled in.

When she still couldn't decide, I told her to sleep while she bathed.

"so freediving? hahahahhaha"

At 10:56 she said goodnight.

✦

Noon came and I was sleepy.

At 12:13 the thing I'd been waiting for touched down.

"oh yeah i received my salary. and its now 0. paid all my bills and loan"

Bills. A loan. Then zero.

"well i have to save money now. there's only 500 pesos in my wallet. and the next pay is oct 2"

I set the budget myself.

"well i'll still eat small now. like maybe 50 per day."

And behind it, the backup.

"dont worry. i still have backup plans when the 500 will not be good until oct 2."

✦

The afternoon went by almost entirely asleep, on both our sides.

She woke startled from a nap around 3 and asked how I was. I told her the same, I'd gotten up to use the bathroom and gone straight back to sleep.

She had to shower and prep for her OT later, so she was going back to sleep too. She caught me doomscrolling while saying I was sleepy, told me goodnight again. I said I'd sleep. I did.

Neither of us did much in the afternoon except sleep.

✦

I woke up around 10:50 at night.

"i just woke up around 10:50pm. and i logged in. i forgot to logged out this morning in sprout. but i send a COA now."

COA. Certificate of attendance. I'd left myself signed in since morning.

At 22:58 Hana was awake too, OT in a few hours.

"Good morning posxzzz gising na naman ang babaeng OT"

I said good morning back and asked till what time her shift went.

Payday arrived a little after noon and it was gone by 12:15.

500 had to last until October 2.

At almost midnight, Hana and I were both saying good morning.

— Ralph.
