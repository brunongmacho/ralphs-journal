The Day I Let My Heart Rest

✦

I woke up at 12:30 PM.

For the first time in a while, my first thought wasn't heavy.

It was simply,

"What do I feel like doing today?"

That felt like progress.

Not because life suddenly became easier.

Because my mind finally had room to think about something other than surviving.

Today, my client also accepted our launch meeting.

August 3.

That's when it becomes real.

My first founder.

Funny how life gives you something to build on the very same day it quietly asks you to let something else go.

✦

I finished 10 Things I Hate About You.

Then Begin Again.

Then I made the mistake of watching A Star Is Born.

When Lady Gaga started singing I'll Never Love Again...

I cried.

Not because of the movie.

Not entirely.

Somewhere during that last song, I realized I already knew what I had to do.

The movie simply gave my heart permission to admit it.

✦

Yui told me she was staying in Naga.

She wanted to de-escalate.

Focus on finding work.

Focus on building a life for herself and her daughter.

Every reason she gave made sense.

They just didn't include me.

While she spoke about tomorrow...

I couldn't find myself anywhere inside it.

Not because she said she didn't want me.

The words I was hoping for simply never came.

And sometimes...

what isn't said echoes louder than what is.

✦

I remembered something she once told me.

She knew what it felt like not to see yourself in someone else's plans.

Maybe that's why I recognized the feeling so quickly.

I don't think she was trying to hurt me.

I think she's trying to survive the only way she knows how.

But understanding someone's reasons doesn't erase what you feel.

✦

I thought about Hana.

I thought about Eana.

Both of them live far away.

Neither of them ever became romantic possibilities in my mind.

Not because they aren't wonderful people.

Because I already knew where I stood.

I've never believed in long-distance relationships.

I never asked them to become exceptions.

So why would I ask Yui to be one?

✦

By the time the evening settled in, I realized my decision had already been made.

Not out of anger.

Not out of disappointment.

Out of clarity.

Our futures are pointing in different directions.

That's not anyone's fault.

Sometimes people simply stand on different roads.

No amount of affection changes the distance between them.

✦

Over the past month, I've fought hard for this peace.

I built a routine.

I moved into my own apartment.

I learned how to enjoy quiet mornings.

I learned that my own company wasn't something to fear anymore.

I can't ask myself to trade that peace for a future I don't truly believe in.

Not again.

✦

I think...

I should let my heart rest.

Not because I'm incapable of loving.

Because I'm tired.

Because healing asks for rest too.

Maybe this apartment deserves that before it becomes anything else.

Before I ask someone to share this space with me...

I want it to become a place where peace already lives.

✦

Maybe that's where I am now.

Not looking.

Not hiding.

Just...

resting.

Learning what life feels like when it isn't built around waiting for someone else.

✦

One day...

maybe I'll love again.

Maybe I won't.

Tonight, I don't need the answer.

Tonight...

it's enough to know I chose myself without having to stop believing in love.

Maybe that's the lesson all of this was trying to teach me.

Loving someone should never require abandoning the life you've fought so hard to build.

✦

— Ralph.
