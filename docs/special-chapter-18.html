The Girl Who Didn't Have to Save Me

ₚ·.°.☆•

There is something dangerous about meeting someone after heartbreak.

Not because you'll fall too quickly.

Because you'll spend every conversation asking one silent question.

"Am I talking to this person...

or am I talking to my past?"

I didn't realize I was still asking that question.

Until I met Yui.

And one day...

I noticed I'd stopped.

✦

Healing is strange.

People imagine it as climbing out of a hole.

I don't think that's what it feels like.

I think it feels more like walking through a familiar room...

and suddenly realizing you haven't looked over your shoulder in a while.

You don't notice the exact day it happens.

You only realize...

you're finally facing forward.

✦

Yui didn't arrive with perfect timing.

She arrived with ordinary timing.

A match.

A few messages.

A voice call.

Another conversation the next day.

Nothing extraordinary.

Nothing dramatic.

No fireworks.

Just two people who kept finding reasons not to end the conversation.

Looking back...

I think that's how most meaningful things begin.

Not loudly.

Repeatedly.

✦

She never asked me to become someone else.

She never asked me to move faster.

She never asked me to prove that I had healed.

She simply met the version of me that existed that day.

And somehow...

that was enough.

I didn't have to explain why some nights were quieter than others.

I didn't have to pretend the journal didn't exist.

I didn't have to hide the parts of me that were still unfinished.

She met them.

Then stayed in the conversation anyway.

✦

There was a moment I still think about.

Not because it was romantic.

Because it surprised me.

She thanked me...

for not comparing her.

I remember answering almost automatically.

"I don't do comparisons."

At first, I thought I was reassuring her.

Later...

I realized I was telling myself the truth.

Elaine wasn't sitting between us.

Not because I'd forgotten her.

Because she no longer needed to.

✦

That's when I understood something.

Moving on isn't finding someone better.

It isn't replacing names.

It isn't convincing yourself the past never mattered.

It's meeting someone new...

without asking them to compete with someone they've never met.

Yui never had to win against my past.

She only had to be herself.

And somehow...

that was enough.

✦

She read the journal.

I still don't know why that affects me as much as it does.

Those pages were never written to impress anyone.

They were written because I needed somewhere honest to place the things I couldn't carry anymore.

When she told me she cried while reading one of the chapters...

I realized something I hadn't expected.

Those pages weren't only helping me survive anymore.

They were reaching someone else.

Somehow...

my healing had become useful.

I don't know if there's a greater compliment a writer can receive than that.

✦

We talked about almost everything.

Faith.

Parenting.

Money.

Fear.

Bodies.

Love.

Broken teeth.

Diabetes.

Cosplay.

Candy.

Adobo.

It sounds ridiculous written like that.

But maybe that's exactly what intimacy looks like.

Not one giant conversation.

Hundreds of little ones.

Random enough to feel ordinary.

Honest enough to become important.

✦

Then one sentence escaped before I could stop it.

"Move in with me."

I still don't know where it came from.

Maybe it came from the apartment.

Maybe it came from hope.

Maybe...

it came from finally believing my life had room for another person again.

She didn't say yes.

She didn't say no.

She simply stayed.

For now...

that felt like enough.

✦

People might read this chapter and think it's about Yui.

It isn't.

Not really.

It's about the version of me she met.

The version that no longer introduced himself through his wounds.

The version that could laugh before explaining why he was sad.

The version that didn't need to prove he'd healed.

Because healing had quietly become part of the way he lived.

✦

I don't know where this story goes.

Maybe years.

Maybe months.

Maybe only a chapter.

Life doesn't tell us those things in advance.

But I do know this.

Yui wasn't the first woman I loved.

She wasn't the first person I trusted.

She wasn't even the first person to read these pages.

She was simply...

the first person who met me after everything...

and never had to compete with who I used to be.

✦

Maybe that's why she feels different.

Not because she changed my past.

Because she arrived after it stopped deciding my future.

✦

— Ralph.
