What Love Became

ₚ·.°.☆•

If you had asked me years ago what love was...

I probably would've answered with certainty.

I would've said it was finding the right person.

Staying together forever.

Growing old side by side.

I thought love was measured by endings.

If the relationship lasted...

it was real.

If it didn't...

maybe it wasn't.

Life has a funny way of correcting us.

✦

Today...

I don't think love works like that anymore.

I don't think love disappears because two people stop walking together.

I don't think time decides whether it was meaningful.

And I don't believe a goodbye has the power to erase what was genuine.

Love doesn't vanish.

It changes.

✦

The love I gave yesterday isn't trapped inside yesterday.

It's here.

Inside the way I listen.

Inside the patience I've learned.

Inside the questions I ask.

Inside the way I notice small things.

Inside every quiet "Have you eaten?"

It stopped belonging to one person.

It became part of the person I am.

✦

Maybe that's what growing up really is.

Not collecting relationships.

Collecting lessons disguised as people.

Every person we genuinely love leaves something behind.

Sometimes it's joy.

Sometimes it's heartbreak.

Sometimes it's courage.

Sometimes it's wisdom.

None of it is wasted.

✦

People often talk about "moving on."

I've never liked that phrase.

It sounds as though love is something we're supposed to leave behind.

I don't think we move on from love.

I think we move forward with it.

It becomes lighter.

Quieter.

Less about longing.

More about gratitude.

✦

Someone once asked whether I'd ever love someone the way I loved before.

I hope not.

I hope I love better.

Not because the people before mattered less.

Because they mattered enough to teach me.

The greatest love of my life shouldn't replace the ones that came before.

It should be shaped by them.

✦

I don't believe our hearts have a limited supply of love.

I think every person we truly care about quietly teaches our hearts how to become larger.

Not fuller.

Larger.

Capable of holding more compassion.

More patience.

More understanding.

More grace.

✦

Maybe that's why I no longer regret loving deeply.

Even when it hurt.

Even when it ended.

Because the love itself never betrayed me.

Only the circumstances changed.

Love remained exactly what it had always been.

A gift.

✦

If someday I meet the woman I'll spend the rest of my life with...

I hope she never worries about the people who came before her.

Because she won't be receiving whatever love I had left.

She'll receive everything love has taught me.

Every goodbye.

Every lesson.

Every ordinary morning.

Every healed wound.

Every version of me that learned how to love a little better.

She won't inherit my past.

She'll inherit my growth.

✦

Maybe love never leaves.

Maybe it simply keeps introducing itself under different names.

Today...

I finally recognized it.

✦

— Ralph.
