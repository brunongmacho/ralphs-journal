The Day the Worst Parts Didn't Matter

✦

I finished my shift at 7 AM and found an email waiting.

Congratulations on being matched with a new Founder.

Ami McKay. PURE Design Inc. Interior designer from West Vancouver. Training starts today. Launch date is August 3.

A few months ago, this would've consumed me. I would've overthought every word. I would've already started imagining the worst.

Today I just smiled.

I got my founder again. <3

Then I messaged Yui.

✦

The morning was playful.

She just woke up. Groggy. We picked up where we left off — teasing, joking, testing each other's patience in a good way. Sadboi. Love bomber. Memes.

Then the jokes settled into something real.

She asked why I have a hard time saying those three words. I told her I've only said it once, and it was too late.

She said start small. Say it to yourself.

Then I told her what I actually want those words to mean.

"I am here. I am real. I won't go anywhere."

She said that's more beautiful than saying I love you.

I don't think she knows how much that stayed with me.

✦

She went to the municipal hall to fix documents. The rain sent her home. We talked about the lawyer she needs, the uncertainty, her mom's vague suggestions.

Then she called.

And on the other end of the line, she broke down.

"Naiiyak ako."

I told her to breathe. That everything is okay. That her daughter is there. That I'm here.

She said it was a wave of relief.

I didn't do much. I just stayed on the line.

Sometimes that's all anyone needs.

✦

The heavy conversation came at night.

I made a comment about cheaters. She took it personally.

"How will you trust me then?"

She has a past she wasn't sure I'd accept. I have a past I wasn't sure she'd accept.

So we told each other everything.

I told her about the things in my past I'm not proud of. The things I was afraid would make her walk away.

She said: "I don't care. As long as you're not doing it when you're with me, I'm okay."

No walking away. No judgment. Just acceptance.

I told her about the friend who talked behind my back. About Elaine. She listened, then laughed, then called him a hatdog.

I don't know why that made me feel lighter. But it did.

"A new chapter means you only bring what you've learned. Not the baggage and trauma."

I meant that for both of us.

✦

She gave me a nickname.

Kohi.

Rohy. Ro-hee. Coffee in Japanese is Kohi. She said I make her feel awake like coffee. And "ko" in Filipino can mean possession.

She explained the whole thing like it was nothing.

I told her it's the first time someone put that much thought into something for me.

She sang me a song from the journal. To see if it still stings.

I heard her voice through the call. The song that used to mean something else.

It didn't sting anymore.

I sang along.

✦

Today's Weight: 8/10

Because I got a new founder and didn't panic. Because I told someone every ugly part of my past and she stayed. Because someone finally put that much thought into me. Because the song stopped hurting.

✦

Two things happened today that would've consumed the old version of me.

A new founder.

A new person.

Months ago, I would've built my entire future around either one.

Today...

I simply welcomed them into a life I was already building.

— Ralph.
