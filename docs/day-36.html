The Day Routine Settled In

✦

I logged out at 7 AM.

Ate sisig and bread.

Watched Mr. D.

Finished my Ragnarok dailies.

Nothing worth noting.

Nothing worth processing.

Just another day inside a place that no longer feels new.

✦

I didn't mention the apartment once today.

Not because I forgot about it.

Because I finally stopped noticing it.

It's not the "new apartment" anymore.

It's just...

home.

I think that's how you know you've settled in.

When the thing you worked so hard for quietly becomes part of everyday life.

✦

I realized something else today.

Freedom isn't always loud.

Sometimes it looks like watching whatever I want.

Playing games for hours without feeling guilty.

Eating when I'm hungry.

Sleeping when I'm tired.

Leaving the room exactly the way I left it because no one else lives here.

The freedom isn't in doing something extraordinary.

It's in never having to ask permission for ordinary things.

✦

Then there was Yui.

We argued again.

It wasn't our first misunderstanding.

Different words.

Same fears finding each other again.

She pushes. I pull.

I pull. She pushes.

Neither of us knows how to stand still together yet.

✦

At some point I typed,

"Okay. You win."

I didn't mean she had won.

I meant I was too tired to keep proving what I was trying to say.

She asked if I wasn't going to talk to her anymore.

I answered.

Neither of us walked away.

We were still there.

Maybe that mattered more than who was right.

Not fixed.

Not broken.

Just...

still here.

✦

Maybe that's what stability really looks like.

Not a day without cracks.

A day where the cracks don't decide the ending.

✦

At 9:46 PM I was sitting in front of my PC.

Playing RO.

No one waiting for the TV.

No one asking me to turn the volume down.

No interruptions.

No expectations.

Just me.

And somehow...

that silence no longer feels unfamiliar.

✦

A month ago, every night ended with another question.

Another fear.

Another "what if."

Now my nights are filled with routines.

Work.

Games.

A series.

Maybe a conversation with Yui.

Then sleep.

Nothing spectacular.

But peace rarely is.

✦

A month ago, I would've called today boring.

Tonight...

I think I'd call it stable.

Stable doesn't make headlines.

It doesn't feel exciting.

But after everything these past thirty-five days have asked of me...

I finally understand.

Peace was never supposed to be loud.

✦

— Ralph.
