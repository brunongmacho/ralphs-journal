The Day I Bounced

✦

The email came a few hours into my shift.

We regret to inform you that you have failed the Doneverse training program.

I read it twice.

The words were final in the politest way possible. No longer matchable. Removed from the cohort, effective immediately. Referred to HR for due process. Keep working my shift hours, do the self-study, watch my personal email, because the Doneverse one might go dark.

I'd been awake since eleven the night before, half waiting for it.

I wasn't surprised. It still landed.

Then I did the only useful thing I knew how to do. I opened my résumé and started applying. JobStreet. Indeed. Somewhere in the dark I decided I wasn't going to wait until I got fired to have something else.

✦

Hours before the sun came up, I told Dewy.

"aun na nga hahanap din ako work. pinagiinitan ako sa work now hahahaha ramdam ko na"

She answered late in the morning.

hala pano yan

"Sabay ka sakin maghanap sa sabado haaaa"

The day before I'd told her to spend Saturday fixing her résumé. Now we were both looking. I was doing the thing I kept telling her to do, and it felt steadier than the fear.

✦

Hana messaged later than usual, and she was having a harder day than the one I'd come to tell her about.

hello sorry ngayon lang nakareply… felt really down lately sorry

You don't fix a down day. You sit next to it.

"Sending virtual hug Hana."

"If you feel like talking about something or anything. Nandito lang ako ha. I wont try to fix things. Makikinig lang po."

Somewhere in the middle of her down day, out it came.

"Naghahanap ako bagong work."

"Langya binagsak ako sa training."

Halaaaaa

"Eh nakakaramdam na ako. Edi hanap na kagad."

She didn't panic. She didn't turn it into a lecture or a worry. She just picked the thread up.

Sige na para pang plot twist na rin yan before the year ends

Mornings before, I was the one telling her she could restart. Now I was handing her my own restart, and she held it without flinching.

✦

The job talk went on most of the morning, and somewhere in it I got handed a better way to look at myself.

"Job hunting ako now"

tsaka sayang talent mo dun pag wait ka lng ng wait ng client

pag PH interviewer bounce na

She had moved from comforting me to advising me without me noticing. Direct clients. Bounce off the interviewers who only wanted the accent. Like the failure was a piece of road I already knew how to walk.

"Di na pwede isang work lng tlga eh"

now I respect working single moms more…. and in this economy pa huhu

We were both building the same kind of answer, from opposite corners.

✦

By mid-morning we were planning a movie.

Half a day after the failure notice, I was on a voice call deciding a horror movie and which power station could outlast the brownout in Moalboal.

"ung cobweb sana?"

sige2 lezgooowwee

The failure stayed in the corner of the room. The day kept being ordinary anyway, and I needed the ordinary more than I needed answers.

✦

The NTE came just past one in the afternoon.

"Aun na nakuha ko na ung NTE. Kailangan ko pa magexplain mamaya hahahaha"

"Bkt ako nagfail haha"

Ha??!!! edi kasi mahirap? hahahha

I read it twice again. The Day 14 recording. The two-fail policy I thought I understood. The disciplinary chart listing failure of the retraining as grounds for dismissal. Five days to write my answer.

I signed it, wrote my side, and hit send before two. The decision hadn't been made at the signature. It was made hours earlier, in the dark, the moment I opened the résumé.

Nareplyan ko na

"Grabe nakaka mentally exhausted"

sayang talent mo jan

go where u are appreciated and worth every cent ung shift

Bounce na

She said it before I could. By the time I typed my own version, I'd already meant it.

"Hahanap lang tlga ako ibang work then bounce na tlga"

I told Dewy the same thing a minute later.

"So aun na. NTE na nga hahahahaha"

✦

By early afternoon she was asking about luck.

"Do u like charms ba or u somehow believe in luck ganern?"

"Depends hahaha. Ikaw ba ung magiging lucky charm? Tho i treasure anything na binibigay sakin."

I was only half joking.

pero just found this and this is for good luck. up to u if u wanna save it or sumthn

"Thank you. I really need all the luck right now tlga."

huy true toooo hahahhaa ilang beses na rin… hardworking ka naman just the timing nalang talaga

She kept saying the timing would turn. I didn't ask her to believe in it for me. I just kept what she gave.

✦

Then I said the thing I'd been holding.

"Alam mo sa totoo lang. Iba ka Hana. Like I love to take my time knowing you. Ayoko irush anything. Parang mindset ko ngaun talaga is lets get our goals first and decide after."

"I hope you wont get bored on what we have right now"

No naman, wherever this leads to then okay. Well for me I am also taking time knowing u also because there is more abt u as much as there is more abt me

No hahaha I am taking time and so you should. Focus ka lang sa growth mo

"Gulatin nalang natin sila magkapitbahay na tayo hahahaha"

I didn't tell her the only reason I held the thought for so long was that I didn't want it to sound like a goodbye. It was the opposite of a goodbye.

✦

She asked about the journal late in the afternoon.

Noiceeee so like wala ka ba nakakausap na friend on the regular thus the journaling?

"Ung journal ko lang talaga consistent ko. It keeps me grounded kasi."

"Pag sinusulat ko dun nagkakaroon ako realizations eh. Which is good din sakin."

The timing of the question wasn't lost on me. I'd been journaling the whole day in my head, and the day kept handing me her.

Then she showed me her art. A character she never finished, traced over from paper into the app. I offered to build it a website someday, the way mine grew its own. She said it wasn't worthy of posting. I told her that's how everything starts.

"Pag nakikita mo pano nageevolve"

And then the real thing, with no joke in it.

Thank youuu and also anjan ka pa rin kahit mejo nagiging avoidant ako sa mga usapan natin… notorious ako jan sa pag iisolate just so yk but don't take it personally lang. It's just how I cope

"Its ok dont worry."

Thank you always… napansin mo rin ata pag nagsheshare ako enough na sakin ung may nakikinig. If it matters, d maliit na bagay un. Thank u sa pagpansin

She told me her hardest habit like it was nothing. That is the part I'm not giving back to anyone.

✦

I woke up close to ten in the evening.

Just woke up, I typed, only without the middle.

I logged in for the login's sake, because the paperwork was clear about one thing. Don't go AWOL, or you lose even the wages you're owed.

Henlooo sabi mo wala kang shift hahahaha

"login lang"

"para sa sahod hahahahaha"

The night before I'd said kaya mo yan twice. Somewhere in this day the words came back to me, pointed the other way, and I had to say them to myself.

Bounce na, she said.

So I bounced.

— Ralph.
