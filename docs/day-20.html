Monday, July 13, 2026

3 min read

The Day I'm Choosing Permanent

✦

I woke up at 10:52 AM.

I fell asleep at 11 PM the night before.

Almost twelve hours. I don't remember the last time I slept that long.

No overthinking. No checking. No replaying.

Just sleep.

Then I opened my eyes, and it was Monday.

✦

Hana messaged first.

We kept talking from where we left off the night before. The conversation picked up like no time had passed.

Music. She recommended Royel Otis. I showed her Yoke Lore. We traded songs for a while and somehow ended up talking about everything else.

Tattoos. She showed me hers — traditional style, Franz Kafka's drawings, a Daruma. She asked about my concept, and I told her about the Da Vinci sleeve I've been imagining for years.

I told her about being scared of needles. About donating blood for my mom and never doing it again. She laughed, but not in a mean way.

She told me about her art. Her horror novels. Her solo travels. Her plan to dive in Bali.

I told her about the engineering work I've done. The designs I've made. The clients I've worked with.

She asked about my height because of my fins. I asked about her plans when she visits Manila.

Somewhere in between, she said if she ever dives here, she'd let me know.

Nothing romantic.

Just two people sharing pieces of themselves and watching them fit together naturally.

The kind of conversation where you don't notice the hours passing.

✦

Then I did it.

I messaged a tattoo artist.

Years of thinking about this. Years of collecting ideas. Da Vinci inventions. Engineering blueprints. Guitar. Gemini. Coding. All connected by fine lines, like a notebook page wrapped around my arm.

I finally asked.

The first quote came back at 250k.

I stared at it for a moment. Then I kept looking. Other artists. Other prices. A cousin's artist who charged 50k for a piece that took two full days.

I'm not in a rush. I'm canvassing. Learning. Figuring out what fits.

But I started.

That's the part that matters.

✦

Eana was around too. We talked here and there throughout the day. Nothing heavy. Just presence.

The afternoon passed quietly. Rain outside. Tattoo references open on my screen. I kept searching. Kept planning.

A month ago, rain meant something different.

Today, it was just rain.

And I spent it thinking about what I want to keep. What parts of myself deserve to stay.

Not who left.

Not what hurt.

Just me.

✦

At 11 PM, I logged into work.

Day 20 ended the way it began — quiet. Uneventful. Mine.

✦

Today's Weight: 8/10

Not because I figured something out.

Because I spent years thinking about something permanent.

And today, I finally made the first move.

Not for anyone else.

For me.

✦

Maybe permanence isn't about tattoos.

Maybe it's about finally knowing who you are.

The things I've been carrying for years but never acted on. Design. Music. Curiosity. The willingness to build something that lasts.

Today wasn't about finding answers.

It was about choosing which parts of myself are worth carrying forward.

And for the first time in a long while, that feels exciting.

— Ralph.
