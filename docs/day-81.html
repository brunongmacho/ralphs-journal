The Day I Did Nothing on Purpose

✦

I spent Saturday doing nothing, and the plan still moved. That's the part worth keeping.

The shift ended Friday night at eleven and the game started right where it left off. NTE still quiet. Applications still thinking. Nothing had answered yet. I had the whole day off and I used none of it on anything that mattered.

By ten that night I was finally asleep. The next thing I wanted was the same queue.

✦

The day opened at half past midnight with the one thing I could actually hold.

"gagawa ako ng jobhunt sheet. para mas madali ko matrack lahat. pag natapos ko share ko sayo :)"

sige2 posxzzzz thank you!!!

and goodluck jan sa JH mo

"yassss. malay mo next na gawin na natin client tracker. <3"

I asked myself what a VA could build that someone would pay for. By the time the list came back I already knew the answer. Not the startup. The tracker. The thing I needed at the exact moment I needed it.

At two in the morning I finally ate. Bagoong and longanisa.

"kain tayooo. binagoongan and longanisa"

Two people on opposite clocks, eating dinner at a time the world had already moved past.

✦

She had already been building her future home. I was just arriving in the furniture.

✦

By nine in the evening I was already falling asleep. She was just getting home from the night market.

kakarating ko lang sa bahay

sige2 po good night!

✦

I did nothing on Saturday that looked like progress. The tracker sat on my screen for half the night. The NTE still hadn't answered. And I'd spent the whole day in a lobby.

But somewhere in the doing-nothing, the tracker found its shape, the city lost another piece of us both, and I heard her laugh at odd hours between matches.

That's what a rest day is for. It's how the plan finds its shape without pressure.

I woke up around midnight wanting the game again. Still wanting it. That's how you know the rest was real.

— Ralph.
