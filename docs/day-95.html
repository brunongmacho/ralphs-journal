The Day the Ringing Stayed

✦

I woke up past one in the afternoon and my left ear was only ringing.

The last graveyard shift had ended just past seven that morning. I slept through the end of it. The day had a shape before it had a problem: food, laundry, mineral water, the desk arranged for a client I meet on Monday. Then the ear started, and the day took the shape the ear gave it.

The night came first, though. Start there.

✦

The last graveyard ran its final night the way it ran them all. Still up at 3:35 with hours left, the Ragnarok dailies, her messages coming in.

She already knew about Monday. Around midnight I told her the hours move to eight in the morning, and her answer was:

"Aweeee welcome tao ka na ulit hahaha"

A person again, after all those 11-to-7s. We talked about the part-time hunt too, a graveyard shift somewhere so I keep her hours. She said at least the new schedule sounds healthy. Eastern Australia, two hours ahead. I told her it's not that healthy either. My sleep stays the same shape no matter what hours I work.

At three in the morning we were laughing about Gordon Ramsay drinking ninong Ry's halo-halo with a straw.

Reacher season four ended before the logout, and the ending stayed with me:

"that ending on reacher s4.... the ashes to the beach..."

The soundtrack after it turned heavy first, then warm:

"and for the soundtrack today. Linkin Park - Numb. look it up. actually i feel like that a bit"

"after june. thats what i was feeling now"

Then, out of nowhere:

"then this. wow youtube playlist! Usher - I Don't Mind"

"or the sched of hana. :P"

Her room smells like Vicks, she said. Her Christmas wishlist came in with Katinko on it, and a Shopee link, and I claimed it:

"cge pag lipat mo yan nalang pang house warm ko sayo. since bumili ka na ng diffuser"

At 7:19 the logout came. I bought food and ran the list: laundry, mineral water, making the desk presentable for the new client. She wished me a good start to the rest day, and I went to sleep.

The 11-to-7 era ended without a ceremony. It ended at 7:19 in the morning with a logout and a rest day ahead.

✦

I woke up past one and asked her first if she'd eaten. Nagkaon ka na lab, her word for it. Then I played a song and put the speaker to my left ear.

The right heard everything. The left only felt it vibrate, and somewhere under that, a ring that wouldn't pop even when I equalized.

I messaged her:

"takte pag gising ko ung left ear ko. hindi makadinig"

She asked if something had hit it, or if I'd slept on it. Nothing happened. I don't move when I sleep. It felt like a plane that landed and never let the pressure out, and I kept asking myself if my diabetes could do this.

The clothes were the problem. I had done the laundry in the morning, so by afternoon I owned nothing but pambahay. The hospital is right behind the house, lakad lang. I finished the energen, took a bath, and walked to the ER. She reminded me first:

"nako wag mo muna alahanin funds mo unahin mo yan sarili mo"

The ER couldn't help. The problem is inside, so it needs a specialist, and there was none until Monday. The walk went on to the second hospital for the same answer. By then the day had cost me money for nothing:

"Nagsayang lang ako ng cash today. La tlga kwenta mga hospital ng pilipinas. Monday pa may ENT. Sakto pa. 8-5. Working hours ko."

She said it the way she softens things:

"Ahy sa bagay weekend kasi today"

Right. It was the weekend. The empty hospital had a reason, and the reason wasn't me.

✦

By evening I had the fix: an appointment for tomorrow at noon, ENT-surgery, at the MediCard clinic in Sta. Lucia. Sunday, the last day off before the new hours.

Then the rest of the night belonged to Monday. I configured the little reminder app from June for the new schedule: login at 7, critical at 7:50, prep at 7:55, logout at 4. Australian Eastern time. Built to flex, so a future client's hours just slot in. It sits on the Desktop now, autostart on.

In Discord, Reacher was running again, with Jalo and the others, the same ending I'd watched at dawn.

At eight, the ring was still there:

"the ringing of my ear really bothers me"

"i'll call them before going there. since i dont want to waste my time and money"

The day was mostly handled. Not the ear.

The last graveyard shift ended just past seven in the morning. The day around it was songs, a logout at 7:19, a rest day's list, a hospital walk that bought nothing, an appointment for Sunday noon, an app that learned the new hours, and her messages all the way through.

Only the ringing stayed.

Anyway. This day is done.

— Ralph.
