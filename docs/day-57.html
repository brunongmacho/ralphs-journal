The Day I Went Out to Buy Food

✦

Slept around ten in the morning.

Woke up around two.

Did my dailies in RO.

It's 6:24 PM.

I'm going out to buy food.

✦

That's the whole day.

I could end the entry here and it would be complete.

For the first time in a long time, there's nothing to tell.

No questions that need answers.

No route to plan.

No deep talk to unpack.

Just:

I woke up.

I played.

I'm hungry.

I'm going to get food.

✦

We talked about this yesterday.

That we don't want to revolve around each other.

That we still have our own lives.

Our own hours.

Our own errands.

I think this is what my half looks like.

It's small.

It's quiet.

And it's enough.

It's 6:24 PM.

I'm going out the door.

The day will end with food.

And that's life too.

— Ralph.
