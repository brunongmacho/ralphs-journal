The Things You Forgive Yourself For

ₚ·.°.☆•

There are things we apologize for that were never really our fault.

Not because we're dishonest.

Because pain has a strange way of assigning blame.

Usually...

to ourselves.

✦

I used to think healing meant finding answers.

Finding out what I did wrong.

What I should've said.

What I should've noticed sooner.

What version of myself might have been enough.

Looking back now...

I realize I wasn't searching for answers.

I was searching for permission.

Permission to stop blaming myself.

✦

There are things I've slowly learned to forgive.

I forgive myself...

for loving someone completely.

For believing in a future that never arrived.

For making plans that remained only plans.

For hoping longer than I probably should have.

Hope isn't a weakness.

It's simply proof that my heart was still alive.

✦

I forgive myself...

for crying over people who can no longer hear me.

For missing my mother.

For missing my grandmother.

For hearing certain songs and suddenly becoming the younger version of myself again.

Love doesn't disappear because someone does.

Why should I apologize for remembering?

✦

I forgive myself...

for not always being strong.

Some days survival looked like productivity.

Other days...

it looked like making coffee.

Watching a movie.

Taking a long shower.

Cooking fried rice.

Sleeping.

Breathing.

Not every victory announces itself.

Some simply whisper,

"You made it through today."

✦

I forgive myself...

for the years I thought I'd wasted.

The hobbies I abandoned.

The guitar collecting dust.

The songs I stopped singing.

The programmer who disappeared.

The athlete who grew older.

The dreamer who became practical.

None of them were gone.

They were waiting.

And waiting isn't the same as disappearing.

✦

I forgive myself...

for needing time.

The world loves dramatic transformations.

One decision.

One breakthrough.

One perfect ending.

Real life isn't written that way.

Real healing arrives quietly.

Almost invisibly.

Until one ordinary day you realize...

music doesn't hurt the same anymore.

Coffee tastes warmer.

Rain sounds beautiful again.

And your own name finally feels like home.

✦

The hardest forgiveness wasn't for anyone else.

It was for the version of me who truly believed he wasn't enough.

I wish I could sit beside him now.

Not to change his story.

Just to tell him something he couldn't yet believe.

You were never difficult to love.

You were simply learning.

✦

Maybe that's what growing older really means.

Not becoming someone new.

Learning to stop punishing the person you've already been.

Every version of you was doing the best he could with what he knew.

The child.

The student.

The engineer.

The son.

The man who lost.

The man who healed.

None of them deserve your anger.

They deserve your compassion.

✦

If you're reading this...

there's probably something you've been carrying too.

Something you replay at two in the morning.

Something you wish you'd done differently.

Maybe today isn't the day you completely let it go.

That's okay.

Healing rarely happens in one decision.

Sometimes it begins with something much smaller.

A quieter sentence.

One you've never allowed yourself to say before.

"I understand why I did that."

"I was trying."

"I didn't know then what I know now."

"I forgive you."

✦

Maybe that's all forgiveness really is.

Choosing kindness...

for the one person who has listened to every thought you've ever had.

Yourself.

✦

— Ralph.
