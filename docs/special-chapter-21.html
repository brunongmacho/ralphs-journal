Named After a Bottle

ₚ·.°.☆•

I don't talk about him much.

He's on a list I carry.

Mom.

Lola.

Elaine.

Jinro.

Coco.

The losses that made me who I am.

Today...

it's his turn.

✦

His name was Jinro.

I named him after the drink.

Because when I got him...

I was drowning in it.

My mom and my grandma had both just passed.

I was alone in my condo.

And I drank a lot.

So I named him Jinro.

A small joke only I understood.

The dog who carried the name of the thing that was carrying me.

✦

I always wanted a husky.

And when I was alone long enough...

I finally walked into that shop.

He was three months old.

Shy.

Quiet.

I brought him home.

And he stayed.

✦

At first, he was shy.

Then he became clingy.

Not the annoying kind.

The kind that saved me.

He wanted to sleep between my legs.

Or on my chest.

Curled up.

I never caged him.

I never tied him.

And every night...

when I closed my eyes...

he was beside me.

Curled up.

✦

Wherever I went, he went.

My computer shop.

My uncle's office.

He'd just sleep beside me at work...

because it was air-conditioned.

He didn't care where we were.

As long as I was there.

✦

He howled sometimes.

His ancestors' language.

And the neighbors would get scared.

Because in the Philippines...

when a dog howls...

it means an evil spirit is near.

Hahaha.

Jinro was the furthest thing from an evil spirit.

But he loved being the reason for the story.

✦

And yes.

He was a humper.

Pillows.

Guests.

His own dignity.

There was no saving him from it.

I want people to remember that about him.

Because it was ridiculous.

And I loved him anyway.

✦

He was barely a year old when something went wrong.

Something with his back legs.

I don't remember all the details.

I remember I was at work.

My nephew was with him.

And he died...

without me there.

✦

I'm tearing up writing this.

Because it's the same story every time.

It wasn't losing him that brought those memories back.

It was realizing the pattern never really changed.

My mom died without me beside her.

My grandma died without me beside her.

And Jinro.

The one who never left my side...

left when I wasn't looking.

✦

After that...

it took me a very long time before I got another pet.

Coco.

Because losing Jinro convinced me

that loving another pet meant

signing up for the same heartbreak again.

I never wanted to feel that again.

Loving something that much...

and not being there when it goes.

✦

And here's the funny part.

I can dive into a new relationship.

New people.

New feelings.

All of it.

But a new pet?

I can't.

Not yet.

The heartbreak hits different.

✦

With people, there's almost always a reason.

"It wasn't working."

"We changed."

"We wanted different futures."

Even when it hurts...

there's still something to argue with.

Something to understand.

Something to forgive.

✦

A pet gives you none of that.

Their love has no conditions.

No past.

No mixed signals.

So the loss has nowhere to put its hands.

It's just...

missing.

✦

People sometimes choose to leave.

Pets never would.

And maybe that's why I keep choosing people's heartbreak over a dog's.

With relationships, at least I get a goodbye.

With Jinro...

I never even got that.

✦

But here's the thing I keep coming back to.

Jinro never left my side.

Even when we had visitors.

He'd sit beside me.

Or curl up against me.

He was always there.

For his whole short life...

every single other moment...

I was there too.

✦

I couldn't be there for his last breath.

I've made peace with that as much as I can.

But he spent his life pressed against me.

Sleeping on my chest.

Curled up between my legs.

If he remembers anything...

maybe that's what he remembers.

✦

Some dogs teach you love with words.

Jinro taught me that love doesn't always arrive with words.

It arrives curled up beside you.

Every single night.

Asking for nothing.

And leaving you with everything.

✦

— Ralph.
