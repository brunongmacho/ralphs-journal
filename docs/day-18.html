The Day You Let Me Into Your World

✦

At 1:47 AM, she sent me a link.

Not a song.

Not a meme.

A Discord server.

Her Discord server.

The place where her friends are. Where she spends her time. Where she laughs at 3 AM over things that don't matter.

A place that already existed long before I arrived.

She just sent the link. Didn't make a big deal out of it.

But it was.

✦

I joined.

At first, I was just there. Reading. Watching.

Then I started laughing.

Not the polite kind. The kind where you're on shift at 1 AM, trying to stay awake, and suddenly your jaw hurts from smiling for an hour straight.

"Buti maingay dito. Tahimik ng Burnik eh."

I told her that.

She laughed.

I kept laughing.

It wasn't romantic. It wasn't flirting.

It was just fun.

The kind of fun I hadn't had in a while.

✦

At 4:30 AM, she said goodnight.

I said goodnight back.

No drama. No "don't go yet."

Just two people who enjoyed spending time together, letting the night end naturally.

I stayed up until 7 AM finishing my shift.

Then I slept.

✦

I woke up at 1 PM.

The first thing I did was send her a good morning message.

I didn't think about it. I just did it.

She was probably still asleep.

That was fine.

It wasn't about getting a reply.

It was about letting her know she had someone in her corner.

✦

I went outside around 2 PM.

Bought a tempered glass and a ring for my new phone.

Ate lunch alone at a random spot.

Bought two cans of Heineken on the way home.

Small things. Ordinary things.

But a month ago, I wouldn't have done any of them.

I would have stayed in bed.

Today, I wanted to be outside.

✦

At night, Eana and I watched movies over Discord.

First Gonjiam.

Then National Treasure.

Two completely different movies. Two completely different moods.

We just sat there, watching, reacting, sharing the experience without needing to fill the silence.

When she needed to sleep, the night ended.

Simple.

Easy.

No pressure.

✦

Today's Weight: 8/10

Not because anything extraordinary happened.

Because the entire day was full of people.

Dewy in the early morning.

Strangers in a server.

Friends in Discord.

Eana at night.

✦

A month ago, I was documenting loss.

Today, I was surrounded by connection.

Soundtrack of Day 18

No songs today.

Just the sound of a Discord server at 2 AM, filled with people I'd never met, making me laugh until my jaw hurt.

That was enough.

✦

Maybe that's what healing looks like.

Not the big moments.

Not the dramatic breakthroughs.

Just...

waking up and realizing you have people to talk to again.

And that somewhere, someone thought of you when they opened a door.

— Ralph.
