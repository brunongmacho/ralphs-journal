And that was the day.

I slept through most of it.

She was in every gap I woke into. A breakfast. A diving video. A good morning at ten at night, and then a whole conversation in the room where we'd started, about the life she wants and the one I want, both of them said out loud.

The hours I was awake, I was awake for her.

The hours I wasn't, she was still there.

That's the day.
