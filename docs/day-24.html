The Day the Future Needed an Address

✦

During work, we had a mock meeting. Our TF acted as the founder and we roleplayed like it was our first day meeting them.

A few weeks ago, I was asking how to answer founder questions.

Today, I was just participating.

Not because I had mastered anything.

But because I'd been practicing long enough that it stopped feeling like practice.

✦

Shift ended at 7 AM. I slept almost immediately. Woke up at noon.

Somewhere between those two moments, the mock meeting stopped mattering.

Because the rest of the day belonged to something bigger.

✦

I spent hours looking for a place to rent.

Not casually browsing. Actively searching. Contacting. Comparing. Calculating.

I need to go my own way from my cousin's house. Nothing wrong between us. I just need privacy and independence. My own space. My own routines. My own door.

A place where I can work nights without disturbing anyone.

Play guitar whenever I want.

Watch anime at 2 PM.

Talk to friends on Discord.

Write.

Build.

Just exist without adjusting to someone else's rhythm.

Maybe that's what independence really is.

Not freedom from people.

Freedom to become yourself.

I found one. Not far from here. I'm going to view it tomorrow.

Funny how something can be ten minutes away and still feel like a completely different life.

✦

I didn't eat brunch until almost 4 PM.

That's how long I was searching.

That's when I realized this wasn't just an idea anymore.

✦

Today's Weight: 8/10

Not because I found a place.

Because I finally started looking.

No dramatic breakthrough.

No love story.

No big lesson.

Just a Friday where I decided the next version of my life needs an address.

✦

A few months ago, I was trying to figure out how to survive the next hour.

Today, I was figuring out where I'm going to live.

Yesterday, the future was a Facebook listing.

Tomorrow, it has a front door.

And for now...

that's already more than I had yesterday.

— Ralph.
