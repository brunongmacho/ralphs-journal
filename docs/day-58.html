The Day I Let Her Choose

✦

I asked Hana what movie she wanted to watch.

"Pahinga sa scary?" I said. "Comedy or action maybe?"

"Yess pls hahaha."

"Nakawatch ka na ng project hail mary?" she asked.

I hadn't. I downloaded it. We watched it together on call. When it ended, we started Arrival.

✦

Somewhere in the middle of the movie, we started talking about what we'd eaten.

Pancit and laing. Then tasty bread.

She had tocino and boiled eggs.

"As in, halos araw-araw ko na 'to," she said. "Nag-aalala na ako sa kidney ko."

"Dapat longganisa naman sometimes," I said.

"Hoy, same, processed meat hahahaha."

We talked about staycations next. She told me she packs rice, salt, sibuyas, garlic when she travels. "If may lutuan dun sa sestayhan ko, lulutuin ko 'to."

"Pag punta mo dito," I said, "makikikain nalang ako sa bnb mo."

"Tas ang kakainin lang rin natin is ung tira-tira sa mga pinuntahan natin hahahaha."

She sent a clip she liked. "Bigyan mo kong one year."

"Before October," I said. "Ikaw sa Manila."

I filed that under October too.

✦

We slept around nine in the morning.

I woke up around five. Went out to buy food.

And while I was out, I noticed something.

Yui unfriended me.

Maybe she read about Hana in the journal. Maybe she saw those pages and decided. Maybe it's something I'll never know.

She'd been quiet for days anyway. No replies. Not the connection we used to have. I wasn't chasing her before I found out. I wasn't about to start now.

✦

There was a time this would've eaten me alive.

The last time someone went quiet on me, I spent days asking what I did wrong. Turning every conversation over. Looking for the moment it broke. I needed an answer so badly it hurt.

This time I asked a different question.

Not "What did I do wrong?"

But "What should I do?"

The answer was: nothing.

Not because I don't care. Because I care enough to let her make her own choice.

I can be sad about it without doing anything about it. I can miss her without contacting her. I can wonder if the journal had something to do with it without deciding that it did.

I'm not the type of person now who begs and chases someone to stay or talk to me.

✦

She chose a different path. Who am I to change that?

I don't want anything forced right now. I don't want anything fake. If she chose to cut me off, I'll accept it and go on with my life.

One person can disappear from your orbit while another sits beside you — virtually — watching a movie. Hana being here doesn't require Yui to leave. Yui leaving doesn't invalidate what we had. Neither one decides whether I'm okay.

My job isn't to hold the door shut. It's to keep living on the other side of it.

✦

Tonight my net died. I'm tethered to my phone, watching Supernatural while I wait for my shift, waiting for Hana to wake up so we can talk.

I'm not begging anyone to stay.

I'm just still here.

Living on the other side of the door.

— Ralph.
