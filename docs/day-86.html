Thursday, September 17, 2026

4 min read

The Day I Was Torn Between Two Islands

✦

7:55 in the morning, an email that wanted an answer.

Matched with a new Founder. Rebecca Newman. Newmark Constructions, the same brand the newmark skill has been for all this time. Badge training from the 17th to the 25th, mandatory. Launch on the 28th.

And the shift. AEST 10 to 6, which is 8 in the morning to 4 in the afternoon here.

I confirmed at 7:59.

Four minutes. I didn't even think about it.

Then the trade caught up with me in the same breath.

"i mean im excited but you know. the graveyard shift pay bonus wont be there now."

Anyone would take a day shift and call it a win. The night differential goes with it, though, so I know exactly what I'm giving up. A few days ago I was still wondering if I'd have a job at all. Now I'm already planning the hours after this one. Keep the day job, find a US client hiring direct for the graveyard.

✦

Then I went to the other session and wrote the heavier thing.

My will. Cremation, ashes at a beach. I was crying while I drafted it.

I want to be a freediver even in death. That's the whole reason for the beach.

Then I got to the part that caught me. Marinduque, my hometown, the place that made me. And I still kept going back to Moalboal, my dream home and diving spot.

Even in a document about where my ashes go, I couldn't choose.

"so im still torned."

I pushed the journal to GitHub the same morning, for the same reason.

Some people would say writing a will is dark. Or that nobody reads a journal sitting on GitHub. Fine. But it doesn't get deleted.

Not finished yet. But a start.

✦

Ate properly twice. 2 pancit bihon, 2 hotdogs, then lunch after. 3 rice, 1 pork adobo, a coke. Skipping dinner, two meals a day is what I do now.

Then Ragnarok. Dailies, the usual. New World High Priest, full support, level 85, job 40.

"kinda ok."

That's the review of an afternoon.

Then the job replies started landing, and the ones that came weren't good ones. SourceU won't be moving forward. Yempo decided the same. Two rejections in one day, and I logged both in the tracker like it was nothing.

Ryvo wants the pre-recorded interview. Kent Homes sent an assessment. CallMax acknowledged the resume at 7. The silent ones stay silent until the 20th.

Somebody says no. Somebody says maybe. Somebody doesn't answer, and you keep the rows moving anyway.

Master Cutter after that, episode 26. A scene got me hard enough to tear up. Then a character showed up with the name I stopped writing down, and that did something to me too.

I'd already cried once that morning, over my own will.

✦

2:19 PM. Out of everything sitting on me, the one thing I couldn't stop thinking about was Hana. We hadn't talked lately.

So I told myself to leave it. She'll reply when she replies, and if she doesn't, there's nothing I can do about it anyway.

4:52. I didn't leave it.

"Hi hana. You ok?"

She answered twenty minutes later, and nothing had happened at all. My replies had just gotten shorter, so she thought she was becoming a bother and backed off. Two people, one quiet stretch, two different ideas about what it meant.

I told her about the week. The warning, the match, the training again tonight. Told her I missed her. She said I deserved better.

"Hana. Just remember this ha. Im not going anywhere naman. D ka naman boring kausap. Dami ko nga nalalaman sayo na interesting. Kung minsan naiiklian ka sa message ko. D naman ibig sabihin nun nabbored ako sayo. Wag ka nega jan."

I told her plainly that I like her. Why would I not be interested.

Then she said the one I'm keeping.

"Maybe I just have this level of confidence in you na di ka naman ganung tao na basta2 mangoghost. Oo naman may pagkamiss ganern pero it's a relief to believe na maguusap pa rin naman tayo"

That's not needing to talk all day. That's knowing the conversation will still be there.

I asked if she missed me. She said no, hahahahha. Then she laughed that I'd finally have a normal person's schedule.

Her own future is moving too. The move to Moalboal is still close. An interview at the end of the month for online teaching. She deleted Instagram to kill the doom-scrolling and said she'd put more effort into messaging me.

Then she went to sleep before her shift.

6:54 PM.

"oh hana and i talked again."

✦

9:58 PM, logging in early so I wouldn't be late.

10:18.

"Hello. Just woke up. Shift naaaa. Training na sa 11pm. Goodluck sa work natin."

Her shift and my training, the same eleven o'clock.

✦

Three things moved today and none of them were the same kind of thing.

My work arrived at 7:55 in the morning. My will got started before nine. And Hana came back at 4:52 in the afternoon.

Day shift, ashes, and her.

Still torn between two islands.

Marinduque, where I'm from. Moalboal, where I want my ashes scattered.

— Ralph.
