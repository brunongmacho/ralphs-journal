Thursday, September 17, 2026

6 min read

The Day I Was Torn Between Two Islands

✦

The email came at 7:55 in the morning.

I answered it at 7:59.

Four minutes. I didn't think about it.

✦

"Congratulations on being matched with a new Founder!"

The Founder is Rebecca Newman. Newmark Constructions. The same brand my newmark skill has been working for all along.

The badge training starts September 17 and ends September 25. Attendance is mandatory. Launch is on the 28th. And the shift that comes with it is daylight: AEST 10 to 6, which is 8 AM to 4 PM here.

I'm excited. But I named the trade myself, out loud, the same morning:

"i mean im excited but you know. the graveyard shift pay bonus wont be there now."

The night differential dies with the daylight shift. And my counter-move came out of me already formed:

"well maybe i'll just get another work for the graveyard. i'll look for US clients with direct hiring."

Keep the day job. Sell the night hours to a client I choose. The differential comes back, but this time it's mine.

I sent the confirmation before I'd even finished reading the email:

"This is to confirm my attendance for the Founder Badge training scheduled from 17-Sep-2026 to 25-Sep-2026. I look forward to the training and to supporting Rebecca Newman and the Newmark team."

✦

Earlier that morning, in the other session, I wrote something heavier.

I cried while drafting my last will.

"you know awhile ago. im kinda crying while i was drafting my last will on the other session"

Cremation. Ashes at a beach in Marinduque, my hometown, so I stay a freediver even in death. The wish I carried alone for years, finally on paper: mom 2013, grandma 2014, dad unknown, solo, not close to anyone. Written down so it survives me.

But even with my hometown on the page, I kept leaning the other way:

"actually even if my hometown was marinduque. im still leaning on scattering my ashes in moalboal. thats my dream home and diving spot"

I know why. My logs with Hana say it over and over. I always dreamed of staying in Cebu, in Moalboal:

"if you read my logs with hana. i always dream on staying in cebu, moalboal"

"so im still torned."

The journal went to GitHub for the same reason the will went to paper. Something of me that outlasts the machine:

"if it doesnt get published, it is still there forever"

"its not finish yet. but yeah. its a start"

Wills get started, then revised, like everything else I build. The specific beach is still open. It can wait.

✦

The middle of the day was ordinary on purpose.

I ate twice, properly:

"2 pancit bihon, 2 hotdogs. then i ate my lunch too. 3 rice and 1 pork adobo and coke for drinks"

"i'll skip dinner. i just do 2 meals a day."

RO dailies done. High Priest, full support, lvl 85, job 40.

"kinda ok."

That's the honest review of an afternoon in Ragnarok: kinda ok.

The job replies started landing too:

"some replied. some hasnt"

SourceU said "unlikely to progress further." Yempo said they "decided not to move forward." Two rejections, both logged in the tracker the same day. Ryvo wants the pre-recorded interview. Kent Homes sent the assessment. CallMax acknowledged the resume at 7 PM. Fifteen of twenty-seven have at least answered now. The silent ones wait for the Sep 20 follow-up queue.

Then the series. Master Cutter, ep 26.

"actually. up until ep 26. some scene made my teared too"

And the name. The hero's missing girlfriend carries a name I stopped writing down:

"i was suprised because of the name Elaine. iykyk"

Crying over the will in the morning, tearing up at a series in the afternoon. Heavy days feel everything closer to the surface.

✦

At 2:19 PM I told the talk session the truth about the quiet:

"actually what i am more concern is that me and hana havent talked lately"

And gave it my honest answer:

"nahh i dont have to fix anything. i'll just wait if she replies. if not then i cant do anything about it."

Then at 4:52 I stopped waiting. I reached first:

"Hi hana. You ok?"

She answered right away. She'd noticed my replies getting short and thought she'd become a bother:

"Well napansin ko rin kasi ur mejo short with ur replies so aun... left it at that baka nakaabala pako"

That was it. Not a problem. Just my shortcuts reading as distance. The truth went out plain:

"But really hana. I miss you."

I told her about the week: the NTE answer, the written warning, the match client, training again tonight. She said:

"Ohhhh okeokeeee that's good naman. And u deserve better"

So I said the part that needed saying:

"Hana. Just remember this ha. Im not going anywhere naman."

"I like you nga diba. So bkt ako d magiging interesado sayo."

She softened:

"edi okaaayyyy this is reassuring to hear."

Her news: online teaching, not VA, interview at the end of the month. My news: the AU account might make it 8 AM to 4 PM.

"In ko. Out mo. Hahaha"

"Uy normal person na schedule aa hahaha"

Then the question I can never not ask:

"Namiss mo din ako? 🥹"

"Hindi hahahahha"

But she followed it with the line that actually mattered:

"Maybe I just have this level of confidence in you na di ka naman ganung tao na basta2 mangoghost. Oo naman may pagkamiss ganern pero it's a relief to believe na maguusap pa rin naman tayo"

She's at the site in Moal now, the move still closing in. And she uninstalled IG to kill the doom-scrolling:

"inuninstall ko na rin ung app ng IG hahahhaa idk if you'll take this a good news pero d nako nagdodoom scroll hahahaha"

"Ganda nga eee mas eeffortan ko pa pagmemessage sayo hahahha"

Then sleep, before her shift:

"anw sleep na talaga akooo see u next shifttt"

"Cgeeee. See you later. 🥰"

At 6:54 I said it to the room: "oh hana and i talked again."

The worry from 2:19 was gone by then. Nothing needed fixing. Just the first move.

✦

I logged in early for the night shift:

"jwu. its 9:58pm here now. i'll login earlier so i wont be late"

Then at 10:18, awake in the same hour she'd be starting, my text went out:

"Hello. Just woke up. Shift naaaa. Training na sa 11pm. Goodluck sa work natin."

Her work, my training, the same 11 PM. The night hours still keep us side by side even when our days wander.

I'm still torn between two islands. The one that made me, and the one I keep choosing.

— Ralph.
