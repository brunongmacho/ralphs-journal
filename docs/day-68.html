The Day I Stayed In

✦

I slept.

I ate.

That was the day.

After the month I just closed, I think it was allowed to be.

I spent thirty days learning that an ordinary day is enough.

Today I got to prove it.

✦

Hana didn't get the joke from last night.

The Hell seats one.

"Ha???? d ko magets hahahhaa."

I explained.

"Parang nagiipon na tayo ng front seat sa hell sa mga sinishare ntin."

She rolled her eyes.

It was a good morning in that small way.

✦

She got invited to church.

A former classmate from college kept asking her, for a while now, and she finally said yes.

"innaccept ko na as she has been inviting me na for a while."

"Ahhh ok. Ingat. Enjoy praising."

She ate with them.

Sent a photo.

"Kumain muna kamiii."

Then later:

"I'm home na pala."

"Sorry d ako masyado nakapagreply."

"Its alright. I understand. Ako din puro tulog pa. Nagpapagaling."

"Ohhh hala d ka pa pala gumagaling?" she asked.

"Hb u get yourself checked kaya."

"Ubo nlng nmn to."

That's all it is now. Just the tail end of the cough that's been following me all week.

She worries anyway.

It's a small thing, and it's mine.

✦

So I spent the day sleeping, eating, and being quiet.

No questions that needed answers.

No deep talk to unpack.

Just a room that smells like food, and the cough that's almost gone, and someone who checks on me.

That's the whole day.

It doesn't need anything else.

— Ralph.
