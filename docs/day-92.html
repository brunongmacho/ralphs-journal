The Day the Song Became Hers

✦

The important part of the day happened before eight thirty in the morning. I was asleep for the last hour of it.

The culprit was Oxygen Not Included. It moves time. Two hours of sleep survived it, and the shift still wanted eight.

So the last hour of work ran on songs. The projects, website and funnels, were done by 5:53. All that was left was the wait for seven and the playlist underneath it.

✦

Saksi Ang Langit first. The heavens as witness, nothing left to wish for. Then Libu-Libong Buwan, the sky changing across a thousand moons and the promise that I won't leave you. Then the version of Until I Found You where two voices trade verses. The chorus corrects "her" to "you" every time, and the whole version is better than the chorus.

One line at a time, the morning went looking for songs that say one thing and make it a spell. Tequila. Around the World. Then the Filipino family of it: Tubero pointing the incantation at the gutter, Jurassic Pards pointing it at the mirror, the Chongkeys' Diksyonaryo teaching that the one who works on pipes is a tubero, Giniling Festival's wish and correction. Girlfriend mo si Angelina Jolie, pero ulo mo munggo. I got the formula on the first try.

"payag ka immortal ka pero bed ridden? hahaha"

Then the theme turned gloomy, and the gloomy is where I always land. The ache underneath the noise. Hypnotize. Forsaken, the vow with the crimson stain already on its lips. And Urbandub, from the city that owns the December table, singing that the night belongs to us.

"yeah now my music choice doesnt make sense hahahaha"

Taken together it did. The vows, the gutter, the gloom. The whole honest dial, one dawn.

✦

Then the question.

"you think the whole song remembers me of hana?"

Not any of the love songs. The one named after a woman from Cebu. The markers matched. Closed doors, a second time, you played your part and helped me restart, southern lights, the stay.

"wait i'll go to the other session i want to write a special chapter for this song."

I went and wrote it, and it came the way the songs did. Whole, in one pass. Cebuana, a chapter that ends by calling her by her island.

The hour before, I had already told her to listen. For you talaga un, I said, tapos sabihin mo sakin reaction mo. Her first take was flat.

"Anw oks lang naman standard love song"

Bias na bias, she teased. Then I sent the chapter, with the why attached, and good night.

"oh and i send a copy to her. i hope she likes it"

Sleep came at seven forty. Her answer came after her own shift, at eight twenty-eight.

"And I will treasure this song just as how u remember me with this song"

✦

"Sa wakas nakabawi na ng tulog."

I woke at two in the afternoon, hungry. Lunch was pastille. Her day ran on the same small currencies: no ulam when she woke, sandwich again because the rain wouldn't let her out, coffee that promised six more hours of sleep, and the pay that still had not reflected while her higher-ups kept following up. The hole in her room was okay now. Before she leaves, she said, she'll set aside budget for the roof.

"pra kung sino lilipat dito okay na sya matulog"

The leaving is already built into the thought, and so is the care.

"See you sa shift. Goodnight."

The shift came at eleven. I was six minutes late.

"nalate nga ako ng 6 mins. napasarap ung tulog."

The night before took two hours. The afternoon paid them back. The shift took six minutes back. Interest.

The day was quiet. A dawn of songs, a chapter written and sent before the sun was fully up, a sleep that settled a debt, pastille, her sandwich and her rain and her roof. Nothing about it was loud.

But by eight twenty-eight, the song was no longer mine to carry. It was hers. The one named for her island, and she said she would treasure it the way I remember her with it.

— Ralph.
