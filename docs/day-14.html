The Day Life Started Throwing Curveballs

✦

Sometimes life doesn't hit you all at once.

It pitches.

One curveball after another.

Not enough to knock you down.

Just enough to make you adjust your stance.

That was today.

Every time I thought,

"Okay... now I can finally rest."

Life quietly smiled...

and threw another pitch.

✦

The first curveball happened just after midnight.

My Lenovo Legion Duel 2 nearly gave up.

One moment it was booting.

The next...

nothing.

No lock screen.

No response.

Just silence.

It wasn't just a phone anymore.

It was my banking.

My GCash.

My authentication apps.

My way of paying bills.

My way of ordering food.

My everyday life.

For a while I tried convincing myself it'd come back.

And for a while...

I wasn't sure it would.

✦

After my final break at work, another surprise arrived.

Around 5:15 in the morning, my Team Facilitator transferred me into another breakout room.

Apparently the Learning Development Associate couldn't find the DU Completion Form for the project I submitted last Friday.

If they were right...

I'd receive a behavioral offense.

The problem was...

I knew I had submitted it.

Not because I vaguely remembered.

Because I remembered struggling with it.

The LinkedIn Outreach course wasn't listed under the category options, so I had spent extra time figuring out that it belonged under Social Media before finally submitting everything.

More importantly...

the Learning Lab doesn't even allow project submission without pressing the DU Completion button first.

My Team Facilitator listened.

She believed me.

Instead of immediately accepting the offense, she filed a dispute.

That small act reminded me how important trust can be.

Ironically...

I had already failed that same DU course once and was retaking it that very morning.

When I submitted the retake...

I filled out the completion form again.

Exactly the same way I remembered doing it before.

✦

Just when I thought the morning had settled...

Elaine messaged me.

Not to reopen old wounds.

Not to argue.

She simply asked whether I'd asked permission before mentioning her in my journal.

For a split second...

my body remembered before my mind did.

A chill.

A tightening in my chest.

Eyes that almost watered.

Funny.

I hadn't cried about her in days.

Yet one notification was enough to remind me that healing doesn't erase history.

I explained everything.

That I never mentioned her children's names.

That almost nobody had read the complete journal except Yosh.

That if she ever felt uncomfortable, I'd gladly remove her name entirely.

She thanked me.

She told me it was okay.

The conversation ended peacefully.

Looking back...

what surprised me wasn't the message.

It was my reaction.

I still felt something.

But I didn't fall apart.

Maybe healing isn't the absence of emotion.

Maybe it's simply learning that emotions no longer control the rest of your day.

✦

At 8:47 AM, I was finally ready to sleep.

My uncle called.

Another urgent revision for the carrot cutter.

I laughed.

Opened AutoCAD.

Made the changes.

Sent the file.

By 9:19 AM, it was done.

"Okay," I thought.

"Now I can sleep."

Life wasn't finished.

✦

The next thing I noticed...

ants.

On my mechanical keyboard.

Apparently they'd decided my desk was prime real estate.

So instead of sleeping...

I completely cleaned the keyboard.

At exactly ten in the morning, I remember laughing to myself.

"Please... I just want to sleep."

Life ignored the request.

✦

At exactly 10:21 in the morning, just as I was about to give up on everything...

my phone lit up.

The boot animation played.

Then my lock screen appeared.

It was alive.

For a few seconds, I just stared at it. I didn't know whether to laugh or shake my head. The phone had spent the entire morning pretending to be dead, and now it was acting like nothing had happened.

I picked it up.

Checked the battery.

Still low.

But alive.

For now.

✦

Then the music started.

One song after another.

Drops of Jupiter.

A song that will always belong to my mother.

No matter how many years pass...

it still finds her.

Then Marilag.

A reminder that somewhere out there...

love still exists.

You're Still the One.

The song I still imagine hearing on my wedding day.

Then Stand By Me.

The song I imagine dancing to with my future wife.

Simple.

Quiet.

Meaningful.

Like the wedding scene from Crazy Rich Asians.

Then Waltz of Four Left Feet.

A song that once meant longing.

Today...

it simply reminded me how far I've come.

Finally...

Beige.

The song that somehow became my daily companion.

A month ago...

these songs would've broken me.

Today...

they simply made me smile.

Maybe because now...

they no longer belong to someone from my past.

They belong to someone I haven't met yet.

✦

I finally slept.

The phone didn't.

Sometime during those hours, while I was catching up on rest, it gave out again. By the time I opened my eyes, it had gone quiet.

Black screen.

No response.

This time, it wasn't coming back.

When I woke up around 4:02 PM...

I was starving.

Then I remembered.

My phone was dead.

No Foodpanda.

No Grab.

No convenient way to order dinner.

For the first time in years...

I actually had to leave the house simply because technology failed me.

Right as I was about to step outside...

my uncle called again.

Another revision.

Fortunately...

this one only took a few minutes.

At 4:24 PM, I finished it.

This time...

there was nothing left stopping me.

I walked outside.

After an entire day of being delayed by life...

dinner somehow became the first thing that happened exactly when I wanted it to.

✦

The funny thing is...

I only went outside because my old phone died.

I came home with dinner...

and a new beginning.

I bought food.

Then...

I bought a new phone.

A Xiaomi 17T.

By the time I got home, the rest of the evening disappeared into setup screens.

Signing back into accounts.

Downloading apps.

Restoring everyday life.

Eventually...

I confirmed what I'd already suspected.

The Lenovo hadn't died because of software.

It was simply the battery.

Five years.

It carried a good part of my twenties.

I think it earned its rest.

✦

By 10:30 PM, I clocked into work.

At eleven...

another shift began.

Just another ordinary Tuesday.

Except it wasn't.

Because today quietly showed me something.

Life can throw curveball after curveball after curveball...

and still...

I can laugh.

I can adapt.

I can keep moving.

A few weeks ago...

one unexpected message would've consumed my entire day.

Today...

there were too many surprises to count.

And somehow...

none of them defeated me.

✦

Today's Weight: 10/10

Not because today was easy.

Because every unexpected moment became another chance to discover who I am becoming.

✦

Life kept changing the pitch.

A few months ago...

every curveball felt personal.

Today...

they simply became part of the game.

And maybe that's what growing up looks like.

Not avoiding life's curveballs...

but learning how to keep your swing.

— Ralph.
