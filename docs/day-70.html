The Day the Countdown Didn't Start

✦

September 1.

In the Philippines, this is when the whole country flips a switch.

Christmas songs start showing up.

Malls put up their lights.

People start saying "ber months" like it means something.

For me and Hana, it was just another Tuesday.

We work our shifts.

We play RO.

We eat.

We talk.

September arrived, and nothing had to announce itself for me to let it happen.

I used to look for significance in everything.

Now even something this big can just happen.

It doesn't mean the ordinary became insignificant.

It means I stopped needing every day to prove that it mattered.

Later, I found out I'm back to Day One of refresher training.

It isn't really starting from zero, though.

I know the modules.

I built the workflow.

September 1 showed up and said:

"Repeat the training."

I already know this.

Here we go again.

That's how September started.

It fit.

✦

Then she told me the news.

She's moving to Moalboal by October 4.

The plan she's been slowly building, the independence, the sea, the fresh start.

It's real now.

"I will be moving to Moal na by Oct 4."

"I think d pako magmaMNL that time."

The Manila date we talked about will have to wait.

She said sorry.

"Wala lang hahaha may plano na sana d ba but yk? I have to cancel for now."

I told her not to apologize.

"Prioritize mo yan Moal mo. Im happy for you!"

"What I want to see is you succeeding sa journey mo."

"Pag natagalan ung punta mo dito sa MNL. Ako na ppunta dyan. Tapos ikaw tour guide ko."

✦

And somewhere in that, I said something I meant more than I knew:

"Dati kasi tlga mas sanay ako minamadali lahat. But pagdating sayo, ayoko madaliin eh."

"Like slowly but sure."

"Saka feel ko kasi tlga na wala naman tayo finoforce and finifake."

A year ago, I would've counted the days in my head.

30.

31.

32.

Now I don't.

I know what I'm waiting for, and I'm not in a hurry to make it happen faster than it's supposed to.

There's no formal label between us.

We don't need one right now.

I told her:

"I think we are exclusive na naman eh. No label, but we are comfortable na with each other."

That's enough.

"Magkkita at magkikita din tayo. Matutuloy din natin ung date natin even hindi dito sa MNL. Gulatin nalang natin isat isa kung sino unang dadayo."

✦

We talked about her new room.

Blackout curtains.

Vintage posters.

She said she has to buy this and that.

I told her about the diffuser I got.

The one that fixed my room smelling like food.

In August, I was fixing my own space.

Now she's fixing hers.

Two separate rooms, getting ready for something.

Hers by the sea.

Mine here.

It's a small thing, but I like noticing it.

"Ako na magreregalo sayo pag lipat mo. House warming gift ko sayo."

She asked if you could just sniff the oils straight.

"Ano ka adik? HAHAHAHA."

✦

Then she said she'll have to minimize her screen time when she's there.

Less doomscrolling.

I said we could just call instead.

"Pwede naman kasi mag call nalang para hindi puro tingin sa cp."

And when she's in Moalboal, by the sea, she could just point the camera at the water.

"Parang ung ivvideo mo lang is ung harap ng dagat para makita ko."

She gets conscious about video calls.

She said it limits her.

I told her she never has to limit anything toward me.

Not even her swearing.

She didn't say yes.

She didn't have to.

✦

She thanked me for understanding her.

For being there while everything's been happening for her.

"Thank you for understanding me rin… and u know being there while kung ano2 na nangyayari sakin."

And I told her the truth:

"You dont know it yet. Pero laki din natutulong mo sakin."

She said she didn't think she'd helped at all.

Maybe she doesn't realize it.

She just keeps showing up.

Checks on me.

Asks if I've eaten.

That counts more than she knows.

✦

Later, I sent her a message.

I told her I was thankful that she's consistently there, checking up on me.

"That kahit sobrang busy ka d mo ko nakakalimutan. I really appreciate it. Work work na ulet later!"

That's how September began.

The country was counting down to Christmas.

I wasn't counting anything.

I had what I was waiting for.

And I was okay with it taking its time.

— Ralph.
