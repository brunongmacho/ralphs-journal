The Day I Walked Into My Own Life

✦

I logged out at 7 AM.

Normally that's when I crawl into bed.

Today I started packing.

✦

The movers hadn't arrived yet.

Everything was waiting.

So was I.

There's something strange about seeing your entire life fit inside a handful of boxes.

You realize how little you actually own.

And how much those little things have witnessed.

This desk watched me lose Elaine.

It watched me build ELYSIUM.

It watched me write thirty-one journal entries.

It watched me cry.

Laugh.

Get matched with a new founder.

Meet Yui.

Question everything.

Then slowly find myself again.

It's only furniture.

But it watched me become someone else.

✦

By 6 PM, I was sitting on the floor of a different room.

Electric fan running.

Trash can in the corner.

A basket for my toiletries in the bathroom.

Small purchases.

But every single one of them is there because I decided it belongs there.

That's new.

For the first time, nothing in this room carries someone else's history.

No family.

No old arguments.

No memories that aren't mine.

Only possibilities.

✦

Then it hit me.

I'm living solo now.

Not staying somewhere.

Not sharing space.

Living.

The silence here sounds different.

It doesn't feel empty.

It feels like mine.

✦

This will be my home.

Where I'll complete my journal.

I don't think I realized how much that mattered until I said it out loud.

The room where I started writing belonged to a version of me who was trying to survive.

This room belongs to whoever I'm becoming next.

✦

I found a song today.

We Made It.

Thirty-two days ago, I had nothing.

No founder.

No apartment.

No certainty.

Just a journal and a broken heart.

Now I'm sitting on my own floor listening to a song that finally fits.

Not because everything is perfect.

Because I have something I didn't have thirty-two days ago.

Evidence.

Not that life became easier.

That I didn't stop walking.

Yesterday I was listening to Numb and Short Stories With Tragic Endings.

Today my soundtrack is different.

Not because the pain disappeared.

Because my life kept moving anyway.

✦

Elaine has never been here.

She'll never walk through this door.

These walls have never heard my old conversations.

They've never watched me cry.

They've never watched me wait.

This room has no history of me.

I didn't realize how much I needed that.

A place where nothing has happened yet.

Where every memory is still unwritten.

✦

For the first time...

I think I understand what I've been building toward.

Not an apartment.

Not a room.

A life where I'm no longer the person things happen to.

For the first time...

I don't feel like I'm reacting anymore.

I feel like I'm choosing.

✦

Thirty-two days ago, I started writing because I was trying to survive.

Tonight...

I don't feel like I'm surviving anymore.

I feel like I'm finally living.

That's a different kind of beginning.

✦

— Ralph.
