The Day the Bonding Was Coffee

✦

Rest day.

That's what it was, mostly.

Woke up hungry. Bought food. Played RO.

Nothing needed to happen.

There was a time a night like this would've felt empty.

Now it just felt like a day off.

✦

She was still up when I was up.

Both of us past midnight, neither of us ready to sleep.

She showed me an old photo of her.

"The college 1st year ata yan ee"

"nung d pako nagtransition hahahahahahhaah"

"ung itchura mo ung parating binobotong president sa classroom"

"HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAH"

Then I told her I was a problem kid from elementary.

"well expected kasi u were well off naman kasi"

We didn't mean anything heavy. But we ended up there anyway.

"Sheltered" is how she put it.

"iyaqq kaming mga sheltered and recently lang magiging independent hahahhahaa"

"like kahit pa nung 21 ako takot pakong abutin ng 7 pm sa bahay"

I told her I grew up on a reward system.

"If i want something dapat maayos ung grades ko"

"kaya talagang work padin ako kahit sabihin natin kaya naman ibigay sakin"

Different houses. Same quiet hour, and it just sat easy between us.

✦

She said she gets palpitations when she drinks. Red spots. An allergy to alcohol.

"ano ba iniinom mo at nagpapalpitate ka? kape? haha"

"pwede naman ang bonding is coffee eh."

"magpapalpitate padin naman tayo nun hahaha"

She laughed at that.

"same tama lang hahahhaha"

Two people who grew up differently, and that was alright.

✦

It was raining on my side. Rotational brownout on hers.

"palit tayo dito ka na lang sa mainit tas may rotational brownout hahahahha"

"hahahaha parang mas ayaw ko ng ganyan ah"

"unless tabing dagat papayag ako hahah"

She went off to do laundry. I went down for food.

"pag balik natin gusto mo mag watch?"

"sure2 imma get readddyyy"

She asked what we'd watch.

"Ung cobweb"

Until then, I was on Supernaturals, season nine.

✦

The quiet afternoon hummed along.

She sent word the laundry was drying. "shet ang init sa labassss"

We were both taking our time, and neither of us was rushing the other.

"weeee baka inaantok ka na jan haaa"

Maybe a little. But it was a good kind of tired.

✦

She went out later, hit the night market.

By the time I caught up she was already home.

"tapos na nasa bahay na"

"Kakagising ko lang kasi"

She was heading to bed.

"patulog na"

"good night"

"okie hana. goodnight. sleep well po"

Then, before it was over.

"ingat ka pala tom. papunta ng moal"

She's going to Moalboal tomorrow. To check the internet in the apartment she's moving into.

That was the whole of the day.

Food. RO. Her.

And the bonding, this time, was coffee.

— Ralph.
